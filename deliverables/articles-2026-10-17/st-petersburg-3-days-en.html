Three days in Saint Petersburg are enough for the Hermitage, Peterhof, the Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood and a canal cruise under the bridges, in Europe's most beautiful imperial city, four hours from Moscow by Sapsan train or a direct flight from the Gulf. This itinerary is planned day by day with realistic timings, family alternatives and a note on the White Nights.

In short: day one for the Hermitage, Nevsky Prospekt and the Church on Spilled Blood, day two for Peterhof by hydrofoil, day three for Catherine Palace and the Amber Room or the Peter and Paul Fortress and a canal cruise. Three nights in a hotel near Nevsky.

Day 1: The Hermitage and the city centre

  • Morning: the Hermitage in the Winter Palace on a two-hour route: the Jordan Staircase, the throne rooms, the Peacock Clock, the Egyptian hall (book online to skip the queue; free for children under seven).
  • Lunch: a halal Uzbek or Caucasian restaurant near Nevsky.
  • Afternoon: the Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood with its mosaics, then Kazan Cathedral and a walk along Nevsky Prospekt.
  • Evening: a one-hour canal cruise under the bridges; in June a midnight cruise to watch the bridges open.

Day 2: Peterhof

  • Morning: the hydrofoil from the Hermitage pier to Peterhof (35 minutes); more than 150 fountains run from late April to mid-October, and the trick fountains delight children.
  • Midday: lunch at the park restaurants or back in the city.
  • Afternoon: Vasilievsky Island with the Stock Exchange and the Rostral Columns, or the Fabergé Museum.
  • Evening: a ballet at the Mariinsky Theatre (book ahead) or dinner on the river embankment.

Where to stay and when to go

Near Nevsky Prospekt everything is close: five-star hotels such as Corinthia, Four Seasons Lion Palace and Grand Hotel Europe, four-star such as Novotel Centre and Park Inn Nevsky; see the Saint Petersburg guide. The best time is late May to early September (the White Nights in June); winter is magical but cold.

Practical tips

  • Foreign cards do not work in Russia; cash or a prepaid programme.
  • Book the Hermitage, Peterhof and Catherine Palace in advance in summer.
  • Gulf citizens enter visa-free; see Russia for Saudi travellers.
  • With Moscow: seven days cover both cities by Sapsan; see Moscow in 3 days.

Frequently asked questions

Are three days enough for Saint Petersburg?

Yes for the main palaces, museums and a canal cruise; four days make the programme relaxed and add both Catherine Palace and the city day.

What are the White Nights?

From late May to mid-July the sun barely sets; the city stays awake until dawn and the bridges open after midnight. See the White Nights in Saint Petersburg.

Is halal food available?

Yes, many halal Uzbek, Caucasian and Turkish restaurants near Nevsky.
