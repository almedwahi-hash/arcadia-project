Three days in Almaty are enough to see the best of Kazakhstan: a green city of tree-lined avenues and cafés, mountains at 3,000 metres reached by cable car in 20 minutes, and a turquoise lake 45 minutes away. This itinerary is planned day by day for families and travellers from the Gulf and Europe, with realistic timings, halal meals and alternatives for every age.

In short: day one for the city (Green Bazaar, Panfilov Park, Kok-Tobe), day two for the mountains (Medeu and Shymbulak), day three for Big Almaty Lake or Charyn Canyon. Three nights in a central hotel, with a private car, driver and guide.

Day 1: The city and the view

  • Morning: the Green Bazaar (dried fruit, nuts, Kazakh honey), then Panfilov Park and the wooden Zenkov Cathedral.
  • Lunch: a halal restaurant downtown; try beshbarmak or Uzbek kebabs. See halal restaurants in Almaty.
  • Afternoon: the Kok-Tobe cable car; views over the city and mountains, a small funfair for children and cafés on the hilltop.
  • Evening: the Arbat (Zhibek Zholy) pedestrian street for a walk and shopping, dinner at a Turkish or Arabic restaurant.

Day 2: Medeu and Shymbulak

  • Early morning: drive to Medeu (30 minutes), the world's highest Olympic skating rink; skating in winter, and in summer the famous 842-step staircase for those who like a challenge.
  • Midday: the Shymbulak cable car in three stages to 3,200 metres; snow even in July, and lunch at a restaurant on the mountain.
  • Afternoon: in winter a beginners' ski lesson or sledging for children; in summer a short walk through the meadows and photos.
  • Evening: back to the city for dinner in a traditional Kazakh yurt with dombra music.

Where to stay and when to go

Central Almaty (Dostyk Avenue or the Golden Square) keeps everything close: four-star hotels such as Novotel City Center and Kazakhstan Hotel, five-star such as Swissôtel, Rixos and InterContinental; see the best hotels in Almaty 2026. The best time is May to September for nature and December to March for skiing; details in the best time to visit Almaty.

Practical tips

  • Gulf citizens and most European nationalities enter Kazakhstan visa-free; direct flights take four to five hours from the Gulf.
  • Bring a light jacket even in summer; at Shymbulak it is ten degrees cooler than in the city.
  • Sundays are busiest at Medeu and Shymbulak; plan the mountain day midweek if you can.
  • With children see Almaty with kids, and with two extra days see Kazakhstan in 5 days.

Frequently asked questions

Are three days enough for Almaty?

Yes for the city, the mountains and one lake. Adding Kolsai and Kaindy or Astana needs five to seven days.
