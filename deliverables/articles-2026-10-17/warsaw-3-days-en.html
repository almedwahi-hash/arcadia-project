Three days in Warsaw combine an old town rebuilt brick by brick, royal palaces and gardens, Eastern Europe's best science centre for children, and shopping and halal restaurants at prices below Western Europe. This itinerary is planned day by day for families and travellers from the Gulf and Europe, with a day trip to Kraków or to Chopin's birthplace as the third-day option.

In short: day one for the Old Town and the Royal Castle, day two for Łazienki Park, the Copernicus Science Centre and Nowy Świat street, day three for a day trip (Kraków by train, or Wilanów Palace and Chopin's house). Three nights in a hotel near the Old Town or the centre.

Day 1: The Old Town

  • Morning: Castle Square and the Royal Castle (restored chambers and the throne room), then the Old Town Market Square with the Mermaid statue and the Barbican walls.
  • Lunch: a halal or Turkish restaurant in the Old Town or on Nowy Świat.
  • Afternoon: the Warsaw Rising Museum (the story of the city's reconstruction) or the Palace of Culture and Science viewing terrace.
  • Evening: a walk along the Vistula boulevards and dinner at a traditional Polish restaurant (pierogi and soups).

Day 2: Parks, science and shopping

  • Morning: Łazienki Park with its Palace on the Water and peacocks; free Chopin concerts on summer Sundays.
  • Midday: the Copernicus Science Centre, the best half-day for children in the city (book ahead in summer).
  • Afternoon: Nowy Świat and Krakowskie Przedmieście for cafés, then Złote Tarasy or Arkadia malls for shopping.
  • Evening: dinner at a halal restaurant in the centre.

Where to stay and when to go

Near the Old Town or Nowy Świat: five-star hotels such as Hilton Warsaw City, Sheraton Grand and InterContinental, four-star such as Novotel Centrum and Radisson Blu Sobieski; see the best hotels in Warsaw and Kraków. The best time is May to September, and December for the Christmas markets; details in the best time to visit Poland.

Practical tips

  • A Schengen visa for most Gulf nationalities (UAE citizens are exempt); apply six to eight weeks ahead. EU passport holders enter freely.
  • Malls close on most Sundays; plan shopping midweek.
  • International cards work everywhere and the currency is the złoty; the metro and trams are the easiest way around, and with Arcadia you have a private car.

Frequently asked questions

Are three days enough for Warsaw?

Yes, with one day for a trip outside the city. Adding Kraków and Zakopane comfortably needs six to seven days; see the Poland guide.

Is Warsaw good for children?

Yes: the Copernicus Science Centre, the zoo, Łazienki and the children's museum at the Royal Castle.

Is halal food available?

Many Turkish, Arabic and halal restaurants in the centre; see the Warsaw guide.
