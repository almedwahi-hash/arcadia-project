Three days in Shanghai are enough for the Bund and the Pudong skyscrapers, Yu Garden, a water town and Disneyland, with halal restaurants in every district and high-speed trains to Beijing and Hangzhou. This itinerary is planned day by day for families and for traders adding leisure days after Guangzhou or Yiwu, with realistic timings and payment and internet tips.

In short: day one for the Bund, Shanghai Tower, Yu Garden and Nanjing Road; day two for the Zhujiajiao water town or Disneyland; day three for Hangzhou by train (45 minutes) or museums and shopping. Three nights in a hotel in Pudong or near the Bund.

Day 1: The Bund and Pudong

  • Morning: Yu Garden and its old bazaar (book ahead in peak seasons), then the old town.
  • Lunch: a halal Xinjiang or Hui restaurant near the Huxi Mosque; see the halal food guide for China.
  • Afternoon: the Bund tunnel to Pudong and Shanghai Tower (the world's second-tallest building) by day.
  • Evening: a night cruise on the Huangpu for the lights of the Bund and Pudong, then Nanjing Road.

Day 3: Hangzhou or the city

  • Hangzhou: the high-speed train in 45 minutes, West Lake by boat, Lingyin Temple, the Longjing tea fields, and back in the evening.
  • Or the city: the Shanghai Museum, the Science and Technology Museum for children, the French Concession cafés, and shopping at the IFC mall or Huaihai Road.

Where to stay and when to go

Pudong for views and large hotels (Grand Hyatt, Jumeirah, Holiday Inn Pudong) or the Bund and the French Concession for atmosphere; see the best hotels in Beijing and Shanghai. The best time is April, May, September and October; avoid Golden Week and Chinese New Year; see the best time to visit China.

Frequently asked questions

Are three days enough for Shanghai?

Yes for the city and one day outside it; with Beijing and Xi'an you need ten days; see China in 10 days.

Is Shanghai good for children?

One of Asia's best cities for children: Disneyland, the aquarium, the science museum and the observation decks.

Is halal food easy to find?

Yes; Xinjiang, Hui and Lanzhou restaurants in every district, and Arabic and Turkish restaurants in Pudong and the French Concession.
