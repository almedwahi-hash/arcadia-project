Three days in Moscow are enough to see Red Square and the Kremlin, the most beautiful metro in the world, a river cruise under the bridges and Europe's largest indoor theme park, with halal restaurants in every district and five-hour direct flights from the Gulf, visa-free for Gulf citizens. This itinerary is planned day by day with realistic timings and alternatives for families.

In short: day one for Red Square, the Kremlin and GUM; day two for the metro, the river cruise and Moscow City; day three for families (Dream Island or VDNKh) or for the suburbs. Three nights in a hotel near Tverskaya or the Arbat.

Day 1: The heart of Moscow

  • Morning: Red Square and St. Basil's Cathedral, then the Kremlin with its cathedrals and the Armoury (Fabergé eggs and royal carriages; timed tickets booked in advance).
  • Lunch: a halal Caucasian or Tatar restaurant near the square; options in the Moscow guide.
  • Afternoon: the GUM department store under its glass roof, then Nikolskaya street and Zaryadye Park with its floating bridge over the river.
  • Evening: Red Square illuminated, and dinner on the old Arbat.

Day 2: Metro, river and modern Moscow

  • Morning: a metro tour with the guide: Komsomolskaya, Mayakovskaya and Ploshchad Revolutsii (rub the bronze dog's nose).
  • Midday: a one-hour river cruise from the Gorky Park pier; the Kremlin and the Cathedral of Christ the Saviour from the water.
  • Afternoon: the Sparrow Hills viewpoint, then Moscow City and its observation deck.
  • Evening: a panoramic dinner in Moscow City, or the State Circus, which needs no language.

Where to stay and when to go

Near Tverskaya or the Arbat everything is minutes away on foot or by metro: five-star hotels such as Lotte, Four Seasons and Radisson Collection, four-star such as Novotel Moscow Centre and Holiday Inn; see the best hotels in Moscow. The best time is late May to early September, and December is magical with winter markets; details in the best time to visit Moscow.

Practical tips

  • Foreign Visa and Mastercard do not work in Russia; bring cash to exchange or prepay the programme with Arcadia.
  • Carry your passport at all times; museums and train stations check it.
  • Book the Armoury and Dream Island in advance; tickets are limited in summer and holidays.
  • Citizens of the six Gulf states enter visa-free; details in Russia for Saudi travellers.

Frequently asked questions

Are three days enough for Moscow?

Yes for the main sights and a family day. Adding Saint Petersburg needs six to seven days with the Sapsan train (four hours).

Is Moscow good for children?

One of Europe's best cities for children: Dream Island, the Moskvarium, the space museum, the circus and the big parks; children under seven are free in most museums.

How do I get around?

The metro is the fastest and cheapest way (under a dollar a ride), and with Arcadia you have a private car with driver and an English- or Arabic-speaking guide.
