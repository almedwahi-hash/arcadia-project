💡 Traveler Tips

⚠️ Card Warning

Foreign Visa and Mastercard do not work! Bring enough cash.

🌅 White Nights

June is the best month! The sun doesn't set. Drawbridges open 1–5 am.

🎫 The Hermitage

Book tickets online in advance – the queues are very long.

🚤 Boat Tours

The canal tour is a must – book from any kiosk. Best at sunset.

🍖 Halal Food

Fewer options than Moscow. Focus on Turkish and Uzbek restaurants.

💰 Prices

Cheaper than Moscow. Meal: 30–60 SAR. Coffee: 7–12 SAR.
