Warsaw (3 days) ← Krakow (3 days) ← Zakopane (4 days). Distances: Warsaw–Krakow 290 km, Krakow–Zakopane 100 km.

Route 2: 15-Day Trip (Extended)

Warsaw (3) ← Gdansk (3) ← Poznan (2) ← Krakow (3) ← Zakopane (4). Includes the coast and the north.

Vienna (3) ← Prague (3) ← Ostrava (1) ← Krakow (3) ← Zakopane (4). Excellent circular route.

💡 Traveler Tips for Poland

💰 Currency

Polish Zloty (PLN). 1 Zloty ≈ 0.93 Halalah. 100 PLN = 93 SAR. Poland is very affordable!

💳 Payment

Visa and Mastercard work everywhere! But carry cash – Morskie Oko lake and horse carriages don't accept cards.

🚗 Car Rental

Highly recommended – more comfortable, easier and half the fun of the trip! No toll roads.

🚆 Distances

Warsaw ← Krakow: 290 km. Krakow ← Zakopane: 100 km. Warsaw ← Gdansk: 350 km.

📱 Communications

Orange or Play SIM from the airport. eSIMs available. Or roaming SIMs from your home carrier.

🎫 Tickets

Buy online – cheaper and skip the queues! Always ask about family tickets.

🍖 Halal Food

Warsaw is best. Try: Pierogi ruskie (cheese), fish. Avoid: Żurek, Bigos, Kiełbasa (pork).

🗣️ Language

Czy to jest halal? = Is this halal? Dziękuję = Thank you. English is good in big cities.

🌤️ Best Time

May–September for tourism. December–March for skiing in Zakopane. Avoid weekends at famous spots.

🛂 Visa

Schengen from the Polish embassy – one visa opens all of Europe.
