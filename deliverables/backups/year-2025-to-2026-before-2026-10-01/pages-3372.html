Comprehensive Almaty Guide 2025 – Kazakhstan

Almaty tourism guide for Arab visitors. 17 halal restaurants, 16 tourist attractions, turquoise lakes, ski resorts, mosques

The city of Almaty is Kazakhstan's cultural capital and its largest city. It is surrounded by the towering Tian Shan mountains with turquoise lakes and world-class ski resorts. Its name means "Father of the Apple" – Kazakhstan is the original home of the world's first apple!

💡 Traveler Tips

💰 Currency

Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). 1 SAR ≈ 125 Tenge. Prices are very reasonable – a meal costs 50–100 SAR.

💳 Payment

Visa is accepted at most places. Carry cash (dollars) for simpler venues.

🚗 Transport

Use the Yandex Go app (like Uber). Rent a car to visit the lakes and canyons.

🌤️ Best Time

May–September for nature. December–March for skiing.

🍖 Halal Food

Kazakhstan is predominantly Muslim. Turkish and Kazakh restaurants are generally halal. Always ask.

🛂 Visa

Visa-free for most Arab nationalities (30 days).
