💡 Traveler Tips

⚠️ Card Warning

Foreign Visa and Mastercard do not work! Bring cash (dollars/euros) and exchange to rubles.

💰 Currency

Russian Ruble. 1 SAR ≈ 24 Rubles. Average meal: 40–80 SAR.

🚇 Transport

The metro is fastest and cheapest (65 Rubles). Yandex Go for taxis. Sapsan to Saint Petersburg in 3.5 hours.

🍖 Halal Food

Look for Халяль (Halal in Russian). Uzbek and Tatar restaurants are generally halal.

🗣️ Language

Russian only! Download Google Translate offline. Spasiba = Thank you.

🛂 Visa

You need a visa in advance. Prepare the hotel invitation letter.
