Top Tourist Attractions in China

1. The Great Wall of China

One of the Seven Wonders of the World and China's eternal symbol. The wall stretches thousands of kilometers across mountains and valleys. Popular sections include Badaling and Mutianyu (easily accessible with cable cars), while Jinshanling and Simatai offer more authentic, less crowded experiences.

2. The Forbidden City – Beijing

The world's largest and best-preserved imperial palace, home to China's emperors for over 500 years. With 980 buildings and approximately 9,000 rooms, it offers an unmatched window into imperial Chinese life and architecture.

3. Terracotta Army – Xi'an

One of the greatest archaeological discoveries of the 20th century: thousands of life-sized clay soldiers buried with Emperor Qin Shi Huang. Each figure has unique facial features and clothing details, reflecting extraordinary craftsmanship from 2,200 years ago.

4. Shanghai – Pearl of the East

China's most modern and cosmopolitan city. The Bund waterfront combines colonial-era architecture with a dazzling modern skyline across the Huangpu River. Shanghai Tower (world's 3rd tallest) and the city's vibrant food and nightlife scene make it unmissable.

5. Yangshuo – Karst Landscape Paradise

In Guangxi Province, unique limestone karst formations rise dramatically from green plains. The Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo is one of China's most iconic experiences, surrounded by otherworldly scenery.

6. Chengdu – Home of the Giant Panda

Capital of Sichuan Province and home to China's giant pandas. The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding offers rare close-up encounters with these beloved animals. The city's fiery Sichuan cuisine and relaxed café culture add to its charm.

Best Time to Visit China

Spring (March – May) – Highly Recommended

Temperatures are mild and flowers bloom across the country. Ideal for outdoor exploration, Great Wall visits, and river cruises. Note: northern China can still be dusty in early spring.

Autumn (September – November) – Best Season

Clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and spectacular foliage colors make autumn the most popular season. Perfect for visiting Xi'an, the Great Wall, and Guilin. Many local festivals take place during this period.

Summer (June – August)

Hot and humid, especially in the south and east. Typhoon season along the coast. Better suited for northern or high-altitude destinations. Expect large crowds at major tourist sites.

Winter (December – February)

Cold to very cold across most of China. However, Harbin's famous Ice Festival (January) is a world-class spectacle. Southern cities like Guangzhou remain mild.

Budget and Costs in China

Accommodation

  • Budget: Hostels and budget hotels – 150 to 300 RMB per night
  • Mid-range: 3-4 star hotels – 400 to 800 RMB per night
  • Luxury: 5-star hotels – 1,000+ RMB per night

Food

  • Street food / local restaurants: 20 to 50 RMB per meal – excellent value
  • Mid-range restaurants: 80 to 150 RMB per person
  • Fine dining: 200+ RMB per person

Transportation

  • Within cities: Metro and buses – 3 to 10 RMB per ride. DiDi is also affordable.
  • Between cities: High-speed trains are fast, comfortable, and reliable. Domestic flights are an option for longer distances.

The Essential Apps: WeChat & Alipay

WeChat – China's Super App

WeChat is not just a messaging app — it is the operating system of daily life in China. Arab traders and tourists use it for:

  • Communication: Text, voice, and video calls with Chinese suppliers and partners
  • WeChat Pay: QR code payments accepted in shops, restaurants, markets, and taxis
  • Mini Programs: Embedded apps for ordering food (Meituan), booking rides (DiDi), and more
  • Business Networking: Exchanging contact details with suppliers using QR code business cards

Setup tip: Create your WeChat account and link an international bank card before arriving in China. Verification sometimes requires a Chinese contact — set this up early.

Alipay – Smart Payments

Alipay is WeChat Pay's main competitor and equally ubiquitous. Key advantages for international visitors:

  • Now supports direct linking of international Visa/Mastercard cards for foreigners
  • Accepted in virtually every shop, restaurant, and market in China
  • Provides currency exchange within the app
  • Offers an English-language interface

Important: Set up Alipay at home and add your international card before traveling. The process is smoother outside China.

What Is Blocked in China? The VPN Issue

China's Great Firewall blocks most Western apps and websites:

  • Google (Search, Maps, Gmail, Play Store)
  • YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X
  • WhatsApp and Telegram
  • ChatGPT and OpenAI services

Solution: Install a reliable VPN (ExpressVPN or Astrill recommended) on all your devices before arriving in China. VPN websites are blocked inside China, so you cannot download them there.

Tips for Arab Travelers in China

  • Halal food: Available in major cities — look for Muslim (Halal) restaurants, especially in Xi'an's Muslim Quarter and Guangzhou's Islamic Quarter. Restaurants displaying Arabic script or a crescent symbol are usually halal.
  • Prayer facilities: Major cities have mosques. Huaisheng Mosque in Guangzhou and Niujie Mosque in Beijing are historic landmarks.
  • Language barrier: Few Chinese speak Arabic or English outside tourist areas. Install Pleco and Google Translate with offline Chinese packs. Save key addresses in Chinese characters.
  • Business cards: Bring business cards in both English and Chinese. Chinese suppliers highly respect this professional touch.
  • Cash: Carry some RMB cash as backup, but rely on WeChat Pay or Alipay for most transactions.
