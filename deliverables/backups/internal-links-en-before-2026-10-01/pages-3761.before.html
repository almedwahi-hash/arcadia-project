How to Get to Yiwu

Yiwu is a relatively small city and easy to navigate — but requires advance planning

Fly to Shanghai or Hangzhou
The nearest major airports to Yiwu are Shanghai Pudong (PVG) and Hangzhou (HGH). From Shanghai it's about 2.5 hours by high-speed train.
Hangzhou airport is closer — 1.5 hours by train
2
High-Speed Train to Yiwu
From Hangzhou East or Shanghai Hongqiao stations, take a high-speed train (G or D line) to Yiwu Station. Tickets cost 80–180 Yuan.
Train is faster and cheaper than taxi
3
From the Station to the Market
Taxi from Yiwu station to the market takes about 15–20 minutes and costs 20–30 Yuan. Or use DiDi (China's Uber) which is cheaper.

Practical Info

Before You Travel — Know This

9 AM – 5 PM
Opening Hours: The market is open daily from 9 AM to 5 PM. Best visiting time is 9–11 AM before it gets crowded.
VPN
Internet: Download a VPN before traveling — WhatsApp and Google are blocked. WeChat is essential for communicating with suppliers.
WeChat Pay
Payment: Most suppliers prefer WeChat Pay or Alipay. Cash is accepted but foreign cards rarely work.
Interpreter
Language: Few suppliers speak English. An Arabic-Chinese interpreter saves you a lot of time and secures better deals.
Business Card
Business Cards: Bring cards in English and Chinese — suppliers keep them and contact you later.
DiDi
Getting Around Yiwu: DiDi is the Chinese taxi app — cheaper than regular taxis. Install it before you arrive.
Important: The booth price is not the final price. Add shipping, customs, and agent costs — the actual price may differ by 20–30%. Calculate the total cost before deciding on profitability.
Expert Tips

10 Tips to Multiply Your Results in Yiwu

From the experiences of traders who have visited Yiwu dozens of times

Focus on One or Two Districts
The market is huge — don't try to cover it all. Choose the districts relevant to your trade and give them enough time.
Request Samples Before Large Orders
A good sample doesn't guarantee full order quality. Always request a sample from the same production batch.
Verify Supplier — Trader or Factory?
Most booths are traders, not factories. If you want a better price or customization, verify the product source.
Compare at Least 3 Suppliers
The same product may differ 20–40% in price between booths. Don't buy from the first shop you visit.
Photograph Everything & Take Business Cards
You'll visit dozens of shops — photograph products, prices, and each supplier's card so you don't get confused.
Don't Pay Full Amount Upfront
Pay 30% when ordering and 70% after goods inspection. This protects you from quality issues.
Use a Local Yiwu Agent
An agent helps you negotiate, inspect quality, and arrange shipping. Saves you from costly mistakes, especially on your first trip.
Yiwu is Perfect for Diverse Products
If you want to buy 20–50 different items in one container, Yiwu is the ideal place.
Start with Small Quantities First Time
Test the supplier with a small order first. Once you confirm quality and reliability, increase quantities in the next order.
Check Intellectual Property Rights
Avoid counterfeit products bearing famous brand trademarks — they may cause customs problems in your country.
