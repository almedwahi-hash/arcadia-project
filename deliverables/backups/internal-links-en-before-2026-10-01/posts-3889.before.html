Russia offers travelers an extraordinary mix of imperial palaces, world-class museums, golden domes, and vibrant city life. Here are the best things to do in Russia for your 2026 trip.

1. Red Square & the Kremlin, Moscow

The symbolic heart of Russia: St. Basil's Cathedral with its colorful domes, the historic Kremlin, Lenin's Mausoleum, and the GUM department store all surround this iconic square.

2. The Hermitage Museum, St. Petersburg

One of the world's largest and oldest museums, housed in the breathtaking Winter Palace. Its art collection rivals the Louvre — allow at least half a day.

3. Peterhof Palace & Gardens

Known as the "Russian Versailles," Peterhof's grand fountains and gilded statues just outside St. Petersburg are a summer highlight.

4. Moscow Metro

More than transport — Moscow's metro stations are underground palaces of marble, mosaics, and chandeliers. A self-guided station tour is free and unforgettable.

5. Saint Petersburg's Canals & Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood

Cruise the canals of the "Venice of the North" and visit the dazzling mosaic-covered Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood.

6. Russian Cuisine & Culture

From borscht and blini to the ballet at the Bolshoi or Mariinsky Theatre, Russia's cultural experiences are world famous.

Best Time to Visit Russia

Late May to September offers the mildest weather and the famous "White Nights" in St. Petersburg. For budgeting, see our guide to the cost of tourism in Russia, and don't miss the top festivals in Russia.
