Tourism with a European taste in the winter of Krakow and Zakopane

Winter tourism in Europe has a distinct taste that differs from the rest of the world. Europe not only gives you the experience of snow and skiing but also visits historical cities that have the fragrance of bygone eras in a regal appearance crowned with snow. There is no doubt that Poland is one of the most beautiful countries in winter. Winter tourism in Poland means that you are on a date with legendary scenes formed by ice, but they give you an irresistible warmth inside you. It is a mixture of history, culture, arts, as well as the warmth and welcome of the people to its visitors.

If you are lucky to visit Poland in December, you will double your enjoyment by experiencing the atmosphere of Christmas celebration, which is a wonderful opportunity for a unique trip and events that you only experience in the month of December. Zakopane and Krakow are considered the ideal cities to visit during tourism in Poland in the winter.

Tourism in Poland in winter 

Winter tourism  in Zakopane 

Zakopane, or as some call it the winter capital of Poland, is one of the unique experiences that you should not miss while tourism in Poland in the winter. Let's learn more information about visiting Zakopane in winter. Learn more about Zakopane Zakopane tourism guide.

  1. Suitable weather and clothing:

The temperature begins to drop in October, ranging between 14 and 5 degrees, then continues to drop in November, then temperatures drop significantly in Zakopane winter, ranging between -1 and -10, and you can expect snowfall in the winter months, which extend from December to February. 

When visiting Zakopane in the winter, we encourage you to make sure to wear clothing suitable for the significant drop in temperature. Coats, thermal layers, scarves, wool hats and gloves are all essential to withstand the cold weather and enjoy the vacation. You should make sure to choose waterproof coats and shoes. 

Tourism in Poland in winter

  1. Things to do  in winter in Zakopane:

Winter tourism in Zakopane includes many activities:

  • Skiing: Skiing is at the top of the list among the activities that tourists do in winter in Zakopane. Whether you are a beginner, amateur, or even a professional skier, you will find tracks that suit your abilities. 

  • Walking: If skiing is not your favorite activity, but you want to enjoy the magical winter atmosphere, you can walk on the hiking trails and immerse yourself in the atmosphere of the European countryside and the magical view of the snow.

  • Cable car ride: One of the experiences that tourists will never forget is riding the cable car. While visiting Zakopane for tourism in the winter, you can ride the cable car up to Mount Kasperi, where there is a charming panorama of the Tatra Mountains and their charming valleys.

  • Snowmobile tour: Snowmobiles are an opportunity to experience that magical European winter atmosphere and go back in time. 

  • Visit Lake Moreski Oko: The lake has great prestige and prestige in Poland because of its charming appearance, as it is embraced by the Tatra Mountains in a majestic scene.

  • Visit spas and hot water baths.

  • Krupocki StreetYou cannot visit Zakopane without wandering around Krupóki Street, buying tourist souvenirs, and learning about the handmade products for which Poland, in particular, is famous for the city of Zakopane.

Winter tourism in Krakow 

Tourism in Poland in winter does not only give you winter sports. While your tourism in Zakopane focuses on nature and winter activities, Krakow gives you more historical and cultural tourism in addition to other options. Let's find out what options Krakow offers you when visiting in the winter.

  1. Suitable weather and clothing:

The weather in Krakow in winter is very similar to the weather in Zakopane, although it is warmer compared to the city of Zakopane. This can be understood due to the difference in nature between the two cities, where temperatures drop significantly in the higher and more mountainous areas. But do not let that fool you. Krakow's weather is also cold, especially in the months of December, January, and February, reaching up to -5, and sometimes it can reach a more severe drop. 

Thermal clothing is a must, along with jackets, wool coats, wool hats and gloves. Since it also snows in Krakow, you of course need waterproof coats and shoes.

Tourism in Poland in winter

  1. Things to do in Krakow in winter:

Market Square: Visiting the main market square is as important as putting yourself in the heart of the action. You can learn about the history of Poland and the history of the city while walking around the square and visiting some of its landmarks such as the Cloth Hall building and St. Mary's Church. If you visit in December, you can shop at the market, which is specially prepared in the square for Christmas celebrations. Enjoy street performances and festivals that distinguish the square and make it an open cultural center.

Wawel Castle: Like all castles established in ancient times, the castle tells the story and is located on a high hill, giving you a panoramic view of the city and the Vistula River. Tour the royal chambers and learn about the valuables in the castle treasury.

Kazimierz district: If you wish to get to know Polish culture better, you can visit the neighborhood and try restaurants that serve local food, immerse yourself in the conversations of local cafes, and experience the local culture of the city among the locals.

Visit the Vialchica mine: The salt mine is located in a grassy valley. The age of the salt rocks in the mine reaches up to 300 years. The value of the mine is not only local, but it is the longest active salt mine in the world and the oldest commercial project of its kind in the world. The mine included carved salt rock statues, caves, and underground lakes. The mine is illuminated by a huge chandelier of salt crystals and receives one million visitors annually. Temperatures drop significantly underground so; We advise you to wear warm clothes when visiting the mine.

Krakow Zoo and Krakow Toy Museum: These are considered popular visits when visiting Poland for families or if you are accompanied by children. Know more about Family tourism in Poland.

Advantages and disadvantages of winter tourism in Poland:

Features of Poland in winter:

The cost of tourism decreases in winter compared to summer.

Winter tourism in Poland enjoys tranquility and the beauty of nature, in contrast to the cities being crowded with visitors in the summer and having to wait in long lines for tourist visits.

- A variety of winter activities and sports that you can only find in the winter, such as skiing and snowmobile outings.

- Enjoy cultural events, experiences and winter markets.

- Fun to visit hot springs.

Disadvantages of Poland in winter:

- Extreme cold and extreme weather, which some people do not prefer.

- Shorter daylight hours and therefore less time for outings that take place in open spaces.
