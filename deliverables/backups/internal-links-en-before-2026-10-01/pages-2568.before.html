Description of cottages in Zakopane

If you are hoping for a trip to the Swiss countryside, but at a better cost and a unique experience, Zakopane huts are the perfect choice. Zakopane Cottages are not, as some might imagine, old places that are not equipped for modern life. Zakopane cottages combine classic wooden design with modern, modern décor. The huts are equipped to accommodate both one- and two-bedroom units, and some of the huts are huge and can accommodate a group of friends. The cottages are also equipped with a modern and modern kitchen for use during your stay. 

We advise you to book the cabin well in advance of your travel so that you can choose a good cabin with a panoramic view that will captivate the eyes. Especially during long annual official holidays such as Christmas. People tend to book the best cottages at these times weeks before traveling. 

You can choose between simple, isolated or modern huts or even luxury huts according to your preferences, desires and trip budget. Your choice of the appropriate cottage location depends on your personal desire. You can choose between isolated cottages with greater privacy or cottages close to the city in areas that tend to be lively and active. Some huts have a charming view of the mountains, with scenes that seem closer to fantasy than reality. Zakopane huts offer you privacy and immersion in unspoiled nature, and are often located near trails, mountain activities and various sports venues.

Good time to visit cottages in Zakopane

Choosing the right time to visit the cottages in Zakopane depends on your trip plans and personal preferences. Your priorities also play a major role in the choice. Are you looking for a less crowded and less expensive time? Or are you looking for a suitable time for a specific activity, a personal vacation, or a unique experience?

If you wish to indulge in winter activities such as skiing and enjoy the snow-covered mountain peaks like a bride on her wedding day, then of course your visit should fall during the winter season. Between the months of December and March, but be aware that these are the highest priced and most crowded months due to the demand of many tourists to practice skiing. Book early to ensure you get the right cabin.

Visiting cottages in Zakopane during the summer between June and August will provide you with better prices and save you from the winter crowds. Don't worry, as temperatures are still moderate and tend to get cold at night due to the mountainous nature of the area. Make sure to check the temperatures before you travel so you can prepare clothing for the weather.

Cottages in Zakopane

The most frequently asked questions for those visiting cottages in Zakopane:

  • Is accommodation in Zakopane only available in cottages?

No, Zakopane offers you multiple accommodation options. You can stay in hotels, hotel apartments, or a hostel. But we advise you to try the huts because it is a unique and new experience.

  • Are the cottages in Zakopane safe and comfortable?

Yes, the cottages are safe and comfortable, and some of them provide the luxury you desire. Always check reviews before booking to ensure the quality of the cabin and the satisfaction of previous customers.

  • Will my children get bored while staying in cottages in Zakopane?

No, there are many activities that you can do with your children. We also advise you to book the cottage that has a special area for children to play. These huts provide safety and entertainment for families with children.

  • Is there public transportation in Zakopane?

Yes, Zakopane has public transportation, as well as car rental services through the application, and many people prefer to walk within Zakopane due to the small size of the city. We alert you that public transportation is not available in rural areas, so if you are staying in the countryside, you will need to rent a car.
