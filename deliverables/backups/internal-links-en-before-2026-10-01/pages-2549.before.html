Quick answer: Choose Almaty as your main base for 4–6 nights. Add Astana for 2–3 nights when you have at least eight days. Choose Turkistan and Shymkent for a southern heritage route, or plan Aktau and Mangystau as a separate adventure.

1. Almaty: best for a first Kazakhstan trip

Almaty combines a walkable cultural centre with quick access to the Ile Alatau mountains. In town, build a relaxed route around Panfilov Street, the Park of 28 Panfilov Guardsmen, Green Bazaar and Kok Tobe. Then reserve separate days for mountain and lake excursions.

Medeu and Shymbulak fit one mountain day. Charyn Canyon, Kolsai Lakes, Kaindy Lake and Altyn-Emel are outside the city and require different drive times and road conditions. Do not present them as nearby city attractions or force several major locations into one family day. Check the current city, mountain and regional options on the official Visit Almaty portal.

2. Astana: Kazakhstan’s modern capital

Astana is the capital and the country’s strongest architecture-and-museums stop. A practical route connects Baiterek, Nurzhol Boulevard, the National Museum, Nursultan Mosque, the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation and Khan Shatyr.

The city is spread out and can be very windy and cold, so families should alternate outdoor landmarks with indoor attractions. The official Visit Astana sights guide lists current places and addresses. The city’s name is Astana—not Nur-Sultan.

3. Turkistan: Silk Road and spiritual heritage

Turkistan centres on the Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi and the wider historical complex. It suits travellers interested in Islamic, Turkic and Silk Road heritage rather than a fast checklist of attractions. Dress respectfully and check visitor rules and opening times before arrival.

Kazakhstan’s government tourism information identifies the Yasawi Mausoleum as the city’s outstanding historical monument. A southern trip commonly combines Turkistan with Shymkent instead of flying back and forth to Almaty.

4. Shymkent: the practical base for the south

Shymkent offers warmer weather, parks, markets and a distinctly southern atmosphere. Its main value for many visitors is as a comfortable base for regional heritage and nature trips. Always calculate the real road time from the city rather than assuming every site is nearby.

Choose two or three nights when combining Shymkent with Turkistan. Travellers focused only on Almaty’s mountains do not need to add it to a short first trip.

5. Aktau: Caspian coast and Mangystau

Aktau sits on the Caspian Sea and is the gateway to the striking desert landscapes of Mangystau. This is not a standard city break: remote formations can involve long drives, unpaved roads, limited services and rapidly changing weather.

Use a suitable vehicle and an experienced local driver, carry water and communication equipment, and plan a contingency. Treat Aktau and Mangystau as a separate multi-day route rather than a rushed add-on to Almaty.

6. Karaganda: modern and industrial history

Karaganda is most relevant to travellers interested in mining, Soviet-era history and regional museums. Some important historical locations are outside the centre, so transport and context matter more than simply adding the city name to an itinerary.

It is a focused choice, not a priority before Almaty or Astana for most first-time families.

7. Oskemen: gateway to East Kazakhstan

Oskemen, also known internationally as Ust-Kamenogorsk, is a base for East Kazakhstan and the Altai region. The surrounding landscapes are the main reason to visit, but distances, infrastructure and seasonal access differ greatly from an Almaty itinerary.

Allow several extra days and verify any border-area restrictions, permits, accommodation and vehicle requirements before confirming the route.

8. Semey: literature and historical memory

Semey is the city’s current name. It is associated with Abai’s literary heritage and regional museums. It is best for travellers who want a deeper historical or cultural journey through eastern Kazakhstan.

Do not confuse a city visit with distant historical areas in the wider region; each requires its own transport and safety plan.

9. Taraz: archaeology on the southern route

Taraz is one of Kazakhstan’s oldest cities and is valuable for Silk Road history, mausoleums and regional archaeological sites. The Aisha Bibi Mausoleum is among the best-known nearby stops.

Add Taraz when it supports a southern itinerary. It is not a better first choice than Almaty simply to increase the number of cities visited.

Which city fits your trip?

  • First visit or family holiday: Almaty, plus Astana with eight or more days.
  • Mountains and lakes: Almaty and its surrounding region.
  • Islamic and Silk Road heritage: Turkistan and Shymkent.
  • Desert and coastal landscapes: Aktau and Mangystau as a separate route.
  • Specialist history: Karaganda or Semey.

Suggested 7-day Kazakhstan itinerary

  1. Day 1: Almaty arrival, transfer and rest.
  2. Day 2: Panfilov area, Green Bazaar and Kok Tobe.
  3. Day 3: Medeu and Shymbulak mountain day.
  4. Day 4: One full-day nature excursion suited to the season.
  5. Day 5: Flexible family day, museum or nearby countryside.
  6. Day 6: Second nature trip or shopping and local food.
  7. Day 7: Departure.

Seven days are better spent around Almaty than split across multiple airports. If Astana is essential, allow at least eight days.

Suggested 10-day Almaty and Astana route

  1. Days 1–5: Almaty city, mountain day and two seasonal nature experiences.
  2. Day 6: Fly to Astana and keep the evening light.
  3. Days 7–9: Baiterek, National Museum, Nursultan Mosque, EXPO area and a flexible weather day.
  4. Day 10: Departure.

Transport and safety planning

  • Kazakhstan is vast; compare actual flight, train and road times.
  • Leave a buffer between a remote excursion and a domestic or international flight.
  • Use a suitable vehicle for mountain and desert roads.
  • Check weather, road status and operating hours immediately before each trip.
  • For mountain routes, share the plan, avoid going alone and use an experienced guide when needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best city in Kazakhstan for tourists?

Almaty is the most versatile first base because it combines city attractions with mountain and lake excursions. Astana is the best addition for modern architecture and museums.

How many Kazakhstan cities can I visit in one week?

One city with its surrounding day trips, or two cities at most. Long distances make a nine-city checklist unrealistic.

Is Almaty or Astana better for families?

Almaty usually offers more variety for a first family holiday. Astana works well as a 2–3-night addition, especially when the group enjoys museums and modern architecture.

Can I combine Almaty and Mangystau?

Yes, but only with enough time for a domestic flight and a properly organised desert route. It is not a sensible addition to a short Almaty stay.
