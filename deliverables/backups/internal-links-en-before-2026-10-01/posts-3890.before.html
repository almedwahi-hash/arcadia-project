Uzbekistan is the jewel of the Silk Road — a land of turquoise domes, ancient madrasas, and legendary cities frozen in time. Here are the best things to do in Uzbekistan for your 2026 trip.

1. Registan Square, Samarkand

The most iconic sight in Central Asia: three majestic madrasas framing a vast public square, covered in intricate blue tilework. Samarkand alone is worth the journey.

2. The Old City of Bukhara

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and living museum, Bukhara's ancient mosques, minarets, and trading domes make it one of the best-preserved medieval cities in the world.

3. Khiva's Itchan Kala

Step inside the walled inner town of Khiva — a perfectly preserved Silk Road fortress city of minarets, palaces, and madrasas.

4. Tashkent, the Modern Capital

Uzbekistan's capital blends Soviet-era architecture, leafy boulevards, bustling bazaars like Chorsu, and an ornate metro system.

5. Shah-i-Zinda, Samarkand

A breathtaking avenue of mausoleums with some of the finest tilework in the Islamic world — a must for photographers and history lovers.

6. Silk Road History & Cuisine

Trace the legacy of ancient traders and savor Uzbek plov, samsa, and fresh bread — hospitality here is legendary.
