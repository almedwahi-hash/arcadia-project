Weather and the best times to visit for tourism in Russia

When choosing when to visit Russia, you need to understand that the country is so large that there are four climatic zones on its territory. While freezing winds are already blowing in St. Petersburg, the swimming season continues in southern Russia.

If you are planning tourism in Russia for the first time, and your choice falls on the cities of its northwestern and European part - Moscow and St. Petersburg, then you should know that the climate here is rather cold. Learn more Weather and climate in Russia.

Spring "April-May" 

 The weather in Russia in spring is as unpredictable as the mysterious Russian soul. Pleasant sounds of falling snow, ice cracking on large rivers and unique freshness of air welcome you in Russia in spring. In some parts of the country, spring may surprise you with snow, but more often than not in May the long-awaited warmth comes to the central part of Russia. If you visit in May with late spring, we invite you not to miss the White Nights Festival in St. Petersburg. Enjoy the charming street atmosphere, musical performances and never-dark white nights. Stroll along the river banks in a romantic atmosphere where the sun does not set even in the middle of the night.

Summer "June-August"

Perhaps it is the most enjoyable time when you can travel and tourism in Russia. However, the air in some cities of the European part of the country can warm up to +30 ° C. The warmest weather of the year is summer. It rains throughout the summer and nights are cooler than days. Summer is the peak tourist season, so accommodation prices rise. Be prepared for queues at tourist attractions. Theaters are closed at this time, but many festivals are held, such as Scarlet Sails.

Summer in Russia 

Autumn "September-October" 

 The first weeks of autumn in Russia (the so-called “Indian summer”) are magical, covered in golden leaves falling from the trees. However, by the end of September, the air temperature drops significantly, and the time comes for jazz concerts, the opening of the theater season, cozy gatherings and walks with mulled wine. Autumn is one of the distinctive seasons where you can enjoy tourism in Russia in good weather, less crowded streets, and better prices. Now you can enjoy sightseeing without the boredom of waiting in long lines. Don't miss visiting Lake Baikal or the beaches, which are optimal during autumn. Enjoy visiting the parks and palaces of Saint Petersburg.

Autumn in Russia

Winter "November-March" 

Traditional Russian winter landscape: golden domes of the Orthodox church against the background of freshly fallen white snow. Russia in winter is wonderful and organic. Russians love winter and know how to enjoy it. In honor of the New Year holiday, major cities are decorated. If you are not afraid of the legendary Russian cold, tourism in Russia in winter will be a great joy for you. Enjoy skiing at one of the resorts, playing in the snow, walking on frozen rivers, or watching the Northern Lights in the most breathtaking sight in the world. Enjoy a New Year's holiday in Moscow or join in on popular holiday parties.

Safety and security when tourism in Russia 

Traveling to Russia is completely safe. Russians are very hospitable and warmly welcome tourists.

Of course, we do not recommend walking independently in remote residential areas, due to the lack of infrastructure in some of them. They are usually far from popular tourist routes.

In Russia, traffic rules are sometimes not adhered to. Therefore, be careful when crossing the roads and drive carefully if you are renting a car. We recommend using only licensed taxi services. Pickpockets and street thieves are very rare in Russia, but still, be vigilant and careful.

How much time to visit St. Petersburg: from 4 days

Away from the hustle and bustle of Moscow, allow yourself to enjoy the beauty and tranquility of Petra. Saint Petersburg is the cultural capital of Russia and one of the most beautiful, charming and enchanting cities in the world. Kazan

How much time to visit Kazan: 3-5 days

On the banks of the Volga River, at the meeting point of East and West, lies the beautiful capital of the Republic of Tatarstan - Kazan. The city, founded by the Bulgarians more than a thousand years ago, pleases with its uniqueness. Since the time of the Golden Horde, it has consistently occupied one of the key positions in the region. A unique blend of cultures has taken place here: within the walls of the original Russian Kremlin, you can see the magnificent Kul Sharif Mosque and visit the restored Cathedral of the Annunciation, located nearby.

In Kazan, you can get acquainted with little-known pages of Russian history, learn about Russia's relations with Asia and the Arab world, and immerse yourself in the amazing culture and traditions of the region. You will remember the wonderful city for a long time, leaving memories of vivid impressions and pleasant surprises. Learn more about Kazan Kazan city, Russia.

Veliky Ustyug

How long does it take to visit Veliky Ustyug: 1-3 days

The small town, located among the deep forests in the north of the Vologda region, is famous for the fact that Father Frost and the Snow Maiden live here and fairy tales come to life. The residence of the fairy-tale heroes is located 12 kilometers from the city, and it takes an entire day to meet them in their wooden palace.

Veliky Ustyug is interesting because of its unique architecture, which has been preserved here in its original form. This is a city of icon painters, whose works you can learn about at the City Museum of Iconology.
