Kazakhstan — the world's largest landlocked country — is Central Asia's rising star, blending modern cities, soaring mountains, turquoise lakes, and warm hospitality. Here are the best things to do in Kazakhstan for your 2026 trip.

1. Explore Almaty, the Cultural Capital

Almaty is the gateway for most visitors: tree-lined avenues, great food, the Green Bazaar, and easy access to the mountains. Ride the cable car up Kök-Töbe hill for panoramic views.

2. Big Almaty Lake

A stunning turquoise alpine lake at 2,500 m, surrounded by peaks just an hour from the city — one of Kazakhstan's most photographed spots.

3. Shymbulak Ski Resort & Medeu

Only 30 minutes from Almaty, Shymbulak offers world-class skiing in winter and hiking in summer, while Medeu is the world's highest Olympic-size skating rink.

4. Astana, the Futuristic Capital

Kazakhstan's capital dazzles with bold modern architecture: the Bayterek Tower, Khan Shatyr (the world's largest tent), and the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation.

5. Charyn Canyon

Often compared to the Grand Canyon, the dramatic "Valley of Castles" is a spectacular day trip from Almaty for nature and photography lovers.

6. Turkestan & the Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Central Asia's most important spiritual landmarks — essential for travelers interested in history and Islamic heritage.
