Best Kazakhstan celebrations for travelers

Nauryz: March 21–23

Nauryz welcomes spring and is the strongest cultural travel period in the calendar. City squares and parks commonly feature yurts, traditional music, family activities, crafts and foods such as baursak and Nauryz kozhe. In 2026 the transferred days off extend the holiday period through March 25.

Good bases: Almaty for city and mountain combinations, Astana for capital celebrations, and Turkistan or Shymkent for southern culture. Program details differ by city, so confirm the local schedule before choosing a venue.

Qurban Ait: May 27

The first day of Qurban Ait is a non-working day in Kazakhstan in 2026. Travelers should expect some family-focused schedules and possible changes to business hours. It is better to confirm restaurant, attraction and transport hours in advance.

Capital Day: July 6

Capital Day is especially relevant to visitors planning Astana. Summer weather and long daylight hours make it practical to combine city architecture, museums and evening public spaces. Official event details should be checked closer to July.

Republic Day: October 25

Republic Day is Kazakhstan's national holiday. It commemorates the 1990 Declaration of State Sovereignty. In 2026 October 25 falls on Sunday, so the official day off moves to Monday, October 26. Visitors should expect official ceremonies and possible changes to opening hours.

Independence Day: December 16

Independence Day is a public holiday and a good reference point for travelers interested in modern Kazakhstan. December is cold, so build the itinerary around museums, indoor attractions and reliable private transport.

When should you plan your Kazakhstan trip?

  • For traditional culture: travel around Nauryz in late March.
  • For outdoor events and nature: choose May through September and match the route to the official event calendar.
  • For Astana city events: consider Capital Day around July 6.
  • For national history: Republic Day and Independence Day are the most relevant official dates.

Practical holiday travel tips

  1. Reserve central hotels early for Nauryz and extended weekends.
  2. Confirm museum, mall, bank and restaurant hours one or two days before a public holiday.
  3. Allow extra time for traffic around central squares and public-event areas.
  4. Keep the itinerary flexible because outdoor programs can change with weather or city instructions.
  5. Use the official government and tourism calendars as the final source for dates.
