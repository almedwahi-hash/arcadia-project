Guangzhou, also known as Canton, is the capital of Guangdong Province in southern China. Located on the banks of the Pearl River with a population of approximately 14 million, it is renowned for its rich history, famous Cantonese cuisine (dim sum), and as a global economic and commercial hub hosting the Canton Fair, the world's largest trade fair.
