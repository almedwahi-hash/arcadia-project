In a historic step that redraws the tourism map between the Gulf and Russia, the Russian government announced the implementation of a system Entry without a visa For citizens of Gulf Cooperation Council countries as of May 11, 2026. This decision opens a new gateway for millions of Gulf travelers to discover Russia's tourist treasures, without the need to wait for a visa or consular review.

Gulf countries included in the visa-free entry decision

The visa exemption includes holders of passports of the six member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council:

  • Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
  • United Arab Emirates previously decided without a visa
  • Qatar previously decided without a visa
  • Kuwait
  • Kingdom of Bahrain
  • Sultanate of Oman previously decided without a visa

When does the application start?

The decision will officially enter into force on May 11, 2026It allows citizens of the Gulf countries to transit through all Russian international ports - air, land and sea - without the need for a prior visa.

Permissible length of stay

The exemption system entitles its holder to remain in Russia for a period of up to 90 days During a period 180 days Consecutive. This time is more than enough to enjoy a comprehensive tour including Moscow وSt. Petersburg And major Russian cities.

Documents required for entry

Although the visa requirement has been dropped, some basic documents remain necessary at the port:

  1. A valid passport — Not less than 6 months from the date of entry
  2. Return ticket — or any ticket proving departure from Russia
  3. Confirmed accommodation reservation
