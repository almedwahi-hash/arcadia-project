Our company organizes group trips and tourist groups to Poland

Organizing group trips and tours to Poland can be an enjoyable and beneficial experience, especially if you plan to travel with a group of friends or family or even as part of an organized tour group organized by us.

Our service in Poland includes 

  1. Advance planning

  • Determine the purpose of the trip: whether it is for cultural tourism, enjoying nature, or practicing winter sports.

  • Choose dates: Select suitable dates, taking into account the appropriate seasons for your activities (such as winter for skiing or summer for enjoying the lakes).

  • Determine a budget: Determine the expected cost of the trip, including travel, accommodations, activities, and meals.

2. Choose destinations

  • Kraków: ideal for history and culture.

  • Warsaw: The modern capital with a mix of history and modernity.

  • Wrocław: A charming city with its bridges and islands.

  • Gdańsk: A coastal city on the Baltic Sea.

  • Tatra Mountains: for outdoor activities and skiing.

  • Masurian Lakes: for relaxation and water activities.

3. Book tickets and accommodation

  • Group tickets: You can get discounts when booking flight or train tickets 

  • Accommodation: We arrange hotels or apartments suitable for groups. Large hotels or apartments for rent can be booked to save cost.

  • Internal transportation: We organize a bus or comfortable transportation for the group to move between cities and destinations.

4. Organizing activities

  • Tours: We arrange tours with professional tour guides to visit the main landmarks.

  • Group activities: such as boat trips on the Masuria lakes, or ski trips in Zakopane.

  • Visit museums: such as the Auschwitz-Birkenau Museum or the Warsaw Uprising Museum.

  • Sample Polish cuisine: Sample meals at traditional restaurants to try Polish dishes like pierogi and bigos.

6. Use our tourism company

You can use our tourism company specialized in organizing group trips to Poland. Our company provides:

  • Comprehensive tourism packages: including travel, accommodation, activities, and duties.

  • Tour guides: to accompany the group and provide information about destinations.

7. Additional tips

  • Health Insurance: We provide all participants with travel insurance that covers the trip.

  • Check visa requirements: Make sure your Schengen visas are valid.

  • Emergency planning: Prepare a list of emergency numbers and necessary information in case of any emergency.
