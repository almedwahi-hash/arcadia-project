Where Is Kazakhstan?

Kazakhstan is located in Central Asia, between the continent of Asia and Europe, and is bordered to the north by Russia and to the east by China. Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country in the world. The terrain varies between mountains and steppes, mountainous and desert areas, and canyons. There are more than 7,000 fresh and salty lakes, in addition to rivers.

Halal Food in Kazakhstan

For Arab travellers, the availability of halal food is crucial; Since most of Kazakhstan's people are Muslim, you will find many options, as well as many restaurants that serve halal meat, so you can comfortably enjoy the unique Kazakh cuisine.

Kazakh Cuisine

Kazakhs love horse meat and rely on it in all their traditional dishes. It has a strong flavor different from beef, and it is halal for Muslims. They also use lamb. Kazakhs mainly serve camel milk with meals, as well as horse soup with onions as an appetizer. One of the most famous dishes is beshbarmak, which is cooked from horse meat with potatoes and pasta. As well as the dish of Chezik, Zaya, and Pilaf, and the dish of Khan Sirbaz, which is made from lamb.
