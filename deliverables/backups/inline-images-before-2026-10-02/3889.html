Russia offers travellers an extraordinary mix of imperial palaces, world-class museums, golden domes and vibrant city life, and for families from the Gulf it is only five hours away with an online e-visa. Here are the best things to do in Russia for your 2026 trip, with practical detail on seasons, tickets and what works with children.

1. Red Square and the Kremlin, Moscow

The symbolic heart of Russia: St. Basil's Cathedral with its candy-coloured domes, the Kremlin walls and cathedrals, the Armoury with the Fabergé eggs and royal carriages (children's favourite), and the GUM department store with its glass roof and ice-cream stands. Allow a full day; book Armoury tickets online for a fixed time. In December the square hosts a Christmas market and an ice rink.

2. The Hermitage Museum, St. Petersburg

Three million objects in the Winter Palace. With children, choose a two-hour route: the Jordan Staircase, the throne rooms, the Peacock Clock (it performs on Wednesdays and Fridays at 7 pm), the Egyptian hall and the Rembrandts. Book online to skip the queue; children under 7 are free.

3. Peterhof Palace and Gardens

More than 150 fountains cascading towards the Gulf of Finland, including trick fountains that spray passers-by, a hit with every child. Take the hydrofoil from the city centre (35 minutes) and allow half a day. The fountains run from late April to mid-October.

4. The Moscow Metro

The world's most beautiful underground: marble, mosaics, chandeliers and bronze statues at Komsomolskaya, Mayakovskaya and Ploshchad Revolutsii, where rubbing the dog's nose brings luck. A one-hour ride with the guide costs less than a dollar a station and children love it.

5. St. Petersburg's canals and the Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood

A one-hour boat cruise under the bridges is the easiest way to see the city, and in June the White Nights cruises at midnight watch the drawbridges open. The Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood, covered inside and out in mosaics, is the most photographed building in the city. See the Saint Petersburg guide.

6. Moscow for families: Dream Island, Gorky Park and VDNKh

Dream Island is Europe's largest indoor theme park; Gorky Park has boats, bikes and winter skating; VDNKh has the Cosmonautics Museum with real spacecraft and the Moskvarium aquarium. The 10 best places in Moscow has the full list.

7. Catherine Palace and the Amber Room

The blue-and-gold baroque palace in Pushkin, 30 km from St. Petersburg, with the reconstructed Amber Room and vast gardens the children prefer to the halls. Timed tickets sell out in summer; we book them in advance.

8. Kazan and Tatarstan

Four hours from Moscow by high-speed train, Kazan's Kremlin holds the Kul Sharif Mosque, one of Europe's largest, beside an Orthodox cathedral, and the city is the heart of Russia's Muslim heritage with halal food everywhere. Ideal as a two-night extension.

9. Russian cuisine and culture

Blini with jam, pelmeni, borscht and the Tatar and Caucasian halal kitchens found in every city; a ballet or circus evening (the Moscow State Circus needs no language); and a banya experience for the adventurous. Halal restaurants are easy to find; our guides know them.

Best time to visit Russia

Late May to early September: 20–28°C, fountains and cruises running, daylight until 11 pm in St. Petersburg, and the White Nights in June. Mid-December to early January is magical but cold (-5 to -15°C); avoid the first ten days of January and early May when Russians travel. See the best time for family travel.
