Have you been asked which destinations you repeat every summer? London, Istanbul, Georgia...they are all wonderful, but there is a destination that has seen a strong rise in recent years and has begun to quietly steal the hearts of Gulf tourists. Kazakhstan.

The largest Islamic country in the world by area, and one of the most surprising destinations for those who visit it for the first time. High mountains, turquoise lakes, modern cities, and summer weather that provides relief from the heat of the Gulf. I don't know why we didn't discover it a long time ago!


Why are Gulf Arabs heading to Kazakhstan?

The numbers speak: more than 100 thousand Gulf tourists They visited Kazakhstan between 2022 and 2024, and the UAE came in first with 32,000 visitors, followed by Oman and Saudi Arabia. This is not a coincidence, there are real reasons that attract Gulf people to this destination.

First: ideal summer weather
While temperatures in the Gulf reach 45 degrees, temperatures range in... Almaty Astana in summer is between 20 and 28 degrees. Fresh air, greenery everywhere, and mild nights invite you to wander the streets.

Second: easy access
From the UAE alone, between 84 and 245 weekly flights To Kazakhstan depending on the season, operated by companies such as Air Astana, Fly Dubai, Wizz Air Abu Dhabi, and Air Arabia. The flight duration does not exceed 4.5 hours From Dubai, much less than a flight to London. Flights are also available from Riyadh, Kuwait and Muscat.

Third: A Muslim and comfortable country
Kazakhstan has a majority Muslim population, you will find halal restaurants everywhere, mosques are widespread, and the people are friendly and welcoming. You will not feel any stranger.


The most prominent destinations within Kazakhstan

🏙️ Almaty, the heart of cultural life
The largest and most vibrant city in Kazakhstan. It combines modern skyscrapers with ancient traditional markets. From here, your trips towards the enchanting nature begin.

  • Big Almaty Lake: A turquoise mountain lake at an altitude of 2500 meters, an incredible picture for your eyes.
  • Shymbulak Ski Resort: Even in summer, the place is great for walking, cable cars and enjoying the mountain views.
  • Eastern Vegetable Market (Green Bazaar): A complete sensory experience, spices, dried fruits, and local products.

🏛️ Astana, the city of the future
The modern capital that will surprise you with its unique architectural design. From its huge glass towers to unique buildings designed by world-class architects. A city that looks like it came out of a science fiction movie.

  • Khan Shatyr tent: A huge entertainment center in the shape of a giant tent, inside which is an artificial beach, amusement parks, and markets.
  • Bayterek Tower: An icon of Astana, climb to its top for a 360-degree view of the city.

🏔️ Natural areas

  • Aktau and the Caspian Sea: Quiet beaches and resorts on the world's largest salt lake.
  • Garharao forests: Stunning nature calls for camping and horse riding.
