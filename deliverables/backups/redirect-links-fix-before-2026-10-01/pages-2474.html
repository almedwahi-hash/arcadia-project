Why Poland is a favourite with Gulf families

Poland offers a European holiday at a fraction of Western European prices: mild summers of 22–26°C, green cities, lakes and mountains, fairy-tale old towns and modern shopping. Direct and one-stop flights from the Gulf reach Warsaw and Krakow in six to seven hours, halal restaurants are easy to find in the big cities, and families are welcomed everywhere. The cities every itinerary should include

  • Warsaw: the rebuilt Old Town, Lazienki Park with its summer Chopin concerts, the Copernicus Science Centre and Europe's best shopping malls. See Warsaw tourism guide and the best halal restaurants in Warsaw.
  • Krakow: Wawel Castle, the medieval market square, the Wieliczka salt mine and Energylandia theme park nearby. See tourism in Krakow.
  • Zakopane: Poland's mountain capital in the Tatras, with cable cars, wooden chalets, lake walks in summer and skiing in winter. See tourism in Zakopane.
  • Gdansk: Baltic beaches and a colourful Hanseatic old town, ideal in July and August. See the tourist city of Gdansk.

Sample itineraries

  • 7 days: Warsaw (3) → Krakow (2) → Zakopane (2). The classic family route; see our Poland tour programs.
  • 10 days: adds Wroclaw or Gdansk, or two extra nights in a Zakopane chalet.
  • Winter 5 days: Krakow (2) → Zakopane (3) for snow, sledging and ski lessons; see winter in Krakow and Zakopane.

Best time to visit

June to August is peak family season: warm but rarely hot, days light until 9 pm, and every lake, park and attraction open. May and September are cheaper and quieter with pleasant weather. December brings Christmas markets in Warsaw and Krakow, and January and February guarantee snow in Zakopane. November and March are grey and best avoided. Note that most shopping malls close on Sundays.

What a family trip costs

For a family of four on a 7-day private program with 4-star hotels, a car with driver and an Arabic-speaking guide, expect roughly 2,600–3,900 USD in summer and 2,000–3,000 USD in spring and autumn, excluding international flights. Visa and practical notes

  • GCC citizens need a Schengen visa from the Polish consulate; apply six to eight weeks before summer travel. Arcadia prepares the hotel and itinerary confirmations required for the application.
  • The currency is the Polish zloty; cards are accepted almost everywhere.
  • Children under 7 travel free on most city transport; family tickets are common at attractions.
  • Book Zakopane chalets and Krakow hotels early for July, August and the Christmas period.

Frequently asked questions

Is Poland safe for families?
Yes. It is one of the safest countries in Europe, with low crime and very low tourist-targeted scams.

Do guides speak Arabic?
Yes. Arcadia provides Arabic-speaking guides and drivers in Warsaw, Krakow and Zakopane, and English-speaking guides on request.

Is halal food available outside Warsaw?
Yes. Krakow has a growing number of halal and Turkish restaurants, and Zakopane hotels can prepare halal meals on request; our guides know the options in each city.

Can we combine Poland with another country?
Yes. Prague, Vienna and Berlin are each a few hours away by train or car and work well as a two- or three-night extension.
