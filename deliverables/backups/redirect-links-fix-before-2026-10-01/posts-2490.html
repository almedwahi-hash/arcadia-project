The best tourist destinations in Kazakhstan With Arcadia Tourism, a guide of points and tips for a complete vacation Kazakhstan is a vast and diverse destination that combines towering mountains, turquoise lakes, endless deserts and steppes, modern cities and historic sites on the Silk Road. If you are planning a trip through Arcadia Tourism within the category of tourism and travel to Kazakhstan, Russia, and Poland, this article provides you with an expanded list of the best tourist destinations in Kazakhstan in the style of practical points and advice, so that you can choose the most appropriate program for you from the comprehensive tourism offers and packages. 1) Almaty, the former capital and city of nature and life Almaty is Kazakhstan's most famous tourist gateway, and is suitable for those who want a combination of city, adventure and mountain air. It is distinguished by its location at the foothills of the Tian Shan Mountains, and its excellent infrastructure for restaurants, cafes, shopping and...Trips Daily. Within Almaty, you will find places for walking, such as the Arbat Pedestrian Street, and many green spaces that reflect the character of the city as one of the most widespread cities in Central Asia for parks. It is also easy to navigate through applications, and is suitable for families and newlyweds alike. 2) Medeo and the mountain skating rink, a winter and summer experience Medeo is one of the most popular points near Almaty, known for its high ski area and surrounding mountain scenery. Even in the non-ski season, the place is great for photos, walking and sitting in the cool air. A visit to Medeo can be combined with Shymbulak on the same day, so you have a complete mountain trip without having to spend the night outside Almaty. 3) Shymbulak, a resort of mountains, cable cars and views Shymbulak is the most famous ski resort near Almaty, but in the summer it turns into a destination for cable cars, fresh air and hiking on mountain trails. The cable car gives you a comfortable ride to heights that provide amazing panoramic images. Shymbulak is an excellent choice for newlyweds who want a combination of romance and nature without straying too far from the city. 4) Kolsai Lake, pearl of the forest and turquoise waters Lake Kolsai is one of the most beautiful lakes in Kazakhstan and the most sought after for trips. It is sometimes known as the Kolsai Lakes because it is a group of lakes at different levels. The road to it from Almaty takes several hours, but the experience is worth it, especially for lovers of pure nature. Colsay fits into thePrograms Integrated project that combines nature and villages, and can be easily combined with Lake Kaindi and the nearby valleys. 5) Lake Kaindi, the sunken lake and its famous trees Lake Kaindi is a unique destination in the world, famous for the tree trunks that appear above the surface of the water like pillars emerging from the lake. It was formed as a result of an ancient landslide, which made the scene exceptional for lovers of unconventional nature. Visiting Kaindi is often part of a combined trip with Kolsay, which makes it ideal for multi-day Arcadia tourist packages. 6) Charyn Canyon, Grand Canyon of Kazakhstan Charyn Canyon is one of Kazakhstan's most famous natural landmarks, offering rock formations in warm colors and walking trails within a valley known as the Valley of the Castles. The experience here is based on viewing from high points and then descending to a path closer to the rocks. If you love cinematic scenes, geology and natural formations, Charan Canyon will be a highlight of your trip. 7) Big Almaty Lake, an elevated lake near the city Big Almaty Lake is a mountain lake relatively close to Almaty. Its color changes according to the season and light, and it is surrounded by high peaks that give the feeling that you are very far away despite your proximity to the city. The visit is usually short but effective. This destination is suitable for those who want a quick touch of nature without a long trip, and is also excellent for family photos and couples. 8) Altin Emel National Park, singing dunes and colorful mountains Altyn-Emel is a vast region that offers a completely different experience from the green mountains of Almaty. It combines desert, steppe, colorful rock formations, and famous landmarks such as the singing dunes that make a sound when the sand moves under certain conditions. Combining Altyn-Emel with Almaty gives your trip a balance between mountains and forests and between desert and colorful formations. 9) Turkestan and the Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmad Yasavi, the heart of spiritual history Turkestan is one of the most important historical cities in Kazakhstan, and it includes the mausoleum of Khoja Ahmad Yesoy, which is included in the UNESCO World Heritage List. A visit here is different from nature, as it is a cultural, spiritual and architectural journey. Turkestan is suitable for inclusion in long programs that include multiple cities, especially for those who want to see the Silk Road side of Kazakhstan. 10) Astana, the modern capital and future architecture Astana represents the modern face of Kazakhstan, with bold architecture, wide boulevards, museums and entertainment centres. Anyone who only visits Almaty may miss this aspect, so it is recommended to add Astana if you have enough time. Astana gives you a completely different image than Almaty, adding an urban touch that highlights the breadth and diversity of Kazakhstan. 11) Bayterek Tower, Astana icon and panoramic view Bayterek Tower is one of the most famous symbols of the capital, and it provides a viewing platform that gives you a comprehensive view of the modern city. The experience is short but important as the first stop to understand Astana's layout and landmarks. Having the Bayterek Tower in your program makes Astana more meaningful, because it provides a comprehensive overview before detailing the rest of the monuments. 12) Khan Shater, entertainment and shopping under the giant tent Khan Shatir is a famous architectural and entertainment landmark, combining shopping, dining and indoor activities, which makes it very useful especially in winter when the weather is harsh outside. Khan Shatyr can be considered an organized rest stop in any package in Astana, especially if the trip is short. 13) Menigistau and the Caspian Sea, Kazakhstan, which is not what many expect The Mengiszau region in western Kazakhstan offers desert, chalk landscapes and valleys that appear to be from another planet, with its proximity to the Caspian Sea. This destination is less famous than Almaty, but it is one of the most powerful experiences for those who love exploring. If you want a trip completely different from the stereotype of Central Asia, then put Mengisthau in your long-term plans, and you will feel that you have discovered a new world. 14) Bozgira Valley, the natural masterpiece of Mengisau Bozgera is one of the most famous sites in Mengisau, with its white cliffs and steep formations that look like natural castles. The place is very wide and the viewing points vary, so the experience depends on choosing paths and points carefully. Bozgira is suitable for those who want to add an exploratory character to their vacation, and it can be designated as the most prominent point of the Mengisau trip. 15) Shymkent, a vibrant city, a gateway to the south Shymkent is one of the largest cities in Kazakhstan, and is considered a gateway to the south and nearby nature areas and reserves. It may not be a tourist's first choice, but it is useful on programs that include Turkestan and southern sites. The value of Shymkent is not in one huge landmark, but in being a vital center that facilitates movement to several tourist destinations in the south. 16) Aksu Jabagli Reserve, wild nature and mountain paths Aksu Jabagli is one of the oldest nature reserves in the region, and is famous for its biodiversity, deep valleys and spring flowers. It is an excellent destination for nature walks and wildlife lovers. Visiting a reserve such as Aksu Jabagli adds great value to the trip for those who want to see natural Kazakhstan beyond just the most famous destinations. 17) Lake Balkhash, a lake with two flavors and amazing contrast Lake Balkhash is one of the large lakes in Kazakhstan, and is famous for a distinctive phenomenon: parts of it are fresh water and parts are closer to saline. The area offers a wide horizon, beautiful sunsets, and a feeling of being away from the hustle and bustle of the cities. Balkhash is not a crowded destination, and that is part of its beauty. You will feel the true space and vastness of Kazakhstan. 18) Karkarali region, forests and lakes in the heart of the steppe Karkarali provides a nice surprise for those who think that central Kazakhstan is just a steppe. You will find forests, hills, small lakes and hiking trails, suitable for those looking for less crowded places. Including Karkarali in a long program reinforces the idea that you have visited Kazakhstan in depth, not just famous destinations. 19) Aktorga highlands and steppe hills, open horizon experience The Kazakh steppe is an essential part of the country's identity, and standing at high points in the middle of the steppe gives you a feeling of spaciousness and freedom. It is not a single destination as much as it is an experience that you can add on as you move between cities. This experience may seem simple, but it is often one of the most memorable moments because it reflects the spirit of Kazakhstan. 20) Experience local culture, markets, food, and hospitality Even with the best natural destinations, the experience is incomplete if you don't get a taste of the local lifestyle. Kazakh towns and villages offer popular markets, traditional food and remarkable hospitality, and these can be turned into enjoyable stops within any program.
