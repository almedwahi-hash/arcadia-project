Visa to Russia

Registration and issuance of a visa to enter Russia

Visa to Russia provides the service of submitting Russian visa applications with ease and comfort. We are experts in providing tourist visas, business visas, and student visas to Russia. We handle all necessary procedures for obtaining a visa, from filling out and submitting forms, to direct communication with the Russian Embassy. We take care of all the details so you can focus on planning your next trip to Russia. No matter where you are from, we can process Russian visa applications for travelers from all over the world. We save you time and effort and guarantee that you will obtain your visa on time.

Beneficiaries of the service:

Foreign citizens and stateless persons.

General information:

A visa is required for a foreign citizen or stateless person to enter the Russian Federation.

If there is an agreement, foreign citizens can enter the Russian Federation without visas. Conditions for entry of citizens of foreign countries into the Russian Federation.

To obtain a visa, a foreign citizen must apply to the Russian consular office at his place of permanent residence.

Russian consular offices issue a visa depending on the purpose of the trip and the duration of the foreigner's stay on the territory of the Russian Federation.

Required documents:

Regardless of the visa category, the applicant must submit the following documents to the Russian consular office:

passport.

A valid foreign passport (diplomatic, official or ordinary) or any other identity document recognized by the Russian Federation.

The passport must not raise doubts about the authenticity and ownership of its owner, and must contain marks, reservations, entries, erasures, and corrections not approved by the competent authorities in the foreign country concerned, and must contain torn or embroidered pages, and must contain at least two blank pages. For visas, their expiry date should generally not expire earlier than six months before the visa expiration date. If an ordinary special visa is issued to a foreign citizen due to the need to enter the Russian Federation for emergency treatment or due to a serious illness or death of a relative, the passport should not expire before the expiration of the visa. If a study or work visa is issued, the passport must not expire before one and a half years from the visa validity date.

Completed visa application form. The visa application form is filled out electronically. If technical capabilities are not available, the visa application form can be completed in handwritten or printed form.

The specialized website of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs for filling out the visa application electronically 

One photo, 3.5 x 4.5 cm.

A 3.5 x 4.5 cm photograph (either black and white or colour) with a clear photograph of the full face without glasses with tinted lenses and without a head covering (except for foreign nationals for whom the constant wearing of a head covering is a mandatory feature) of their nationality or religious affiliation, provided that the foreign national is photographed wearing such head covering in the passport photo).

Under reciprocity, visa fees are charged.

Detailed information about tariffs of consular fees charged for visas can be obtained on the website of the relevant Russian consular office abroad.

Documents by visa category.

The visa is issued only after submitting a complete set of documents to the consular office.

The package of documents for obtaining a visa must be submitted in person by the applicant or his legal representative.

After receiving the documents and paying the fees, the applicant receives a receipt indicating the visa processing date. The receipt must be presented to obtain the visa.

Upon receipt of the visa, the applicant must check that the relevant fields in the visa are filled out correctly: last name, dates of travel, passport number, date of birth, etc. If necessary, return the visa immediately for modification.

Regardless of the number of visas received, the total duration of stay of a foreign citizen on the territory of the Russian Federation cannot exceed 90 days during each 180-day period. This provision does not apply to foreigners who enter on work and study visas.

The Consulate reserves the right to refuse to issue a visa to the applicant if there are serious reasons.

If the visa is refused, the fees paid are non-refundable.

Visa categories

Business and humanitarian visas:

For a business or humanitarian visa, applicants should provide the consulate with the following:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  The original invitation to enter the Russian Federation (in paper form or a printed copy of the invitation issued in electronic form), issued by a territorial body of the Russian Ministry of Interior or the original invitation issued by the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or a decision of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  Business and humanitarian visas can be single or double entry for up to three months and multiple entry for up to one year.

Tourist visas:

To obtain a tourist visa, the applicant must provide the consulate with the following:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  Confirmation of the reception of a foreign tourist by an organization, the information about which is in the Unified Federal Register of Tour Operators, extension of its validity, restoration in case of its loss, as well as visa cancellation procedures, or confirmation of reservation of a place in a hotel or other accommodation facility, information about which is in the unified classified list of hotels, ski slopes and beaches.

A regular tourist visa can be single or double entry for up to 3 months or multiple entry for up to 6 months.

Special visas:

To apply for a special visa, the applicant must submit to the consulate:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  The original invitation to enter the Russian Federation (in paper form or a printed copy of the invitation issued in electronic form), issued by the territorial body of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Russia, or a written application of a Russian citizen addressed to the head of the Russian diplomatic mission or the consular office of the Russian Federation for entry into the Russian Federation of a foreign citizen - his close relative.

When applying for an ordinary multiple-entry special visa for a foreign citizen for a period of more than 3 months, it is necessary to attach a certificate of non-infection with HIV, with the exception of spouses, children (including adoptees) and parents (including adoptees) of citizens of the Russian Federation.

Special visas can be single or double entry for up to three months, as well as multiple entry for up to one year.

Study visas:

To obtain a study visa, the applicant must provide the consulate with the following:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  The original invitation to enter the Russian Federation (in paper form or a printed copy of the invitation issued in electronic form), issued by the territorial body of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Russia;

  HIV-free certificate (valid for 3 months).

When applying for a student visa, the passport must be valid for at least one and a half years.

Work visas:

To obtain a work visa, the applicant must provide the consulate with the following:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  The original invitation to enter the Russian Federation (in paper form or a printed copy of the invitation issued in electronic form), issued by the territorial body of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Russia;

  HIV-free certificate (valid for 3 months).

When applying for a work visa, the passport must be valid for at least one and a half years.

Transit visas:

To obtain a transit visa, the applicant must provide the consulate with the following:

  Passport, completed visa application form, two photographs, fees;

  Valid travel tickets to leave the Russian Federation;

  A visa to enter a country neighboring the Russian Federation along the way, or the visa of the destination country, or the passport of this country.

A transit visa is issued to a foreign citizen traveling through the territory of the Russian Federation to the destination country by air for a period of no more than 3 days. A foreign citizen making a non-stop flight over the territory of the Russian Federation does not need a transit visa.
