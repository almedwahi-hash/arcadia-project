Traveling from Saudi Arabia usually needs to spread the expenses over flight, accommodation, transportation, food, activities, and unexpected fees. In this guide we provide practical numbers in Saudi Riyals and US Dollars, with the example of a full week in Moscow and Saint Petersburg. The goal is to know the minimum and average limits before paying, and then choose what suits you without financial surprises while traveling.

Cost of tourism in Russia: basic price ranges before booking

Round-trip flight tickets from Riyadh to Moscow are often between 1,400 and 2,800 riyals per person ($373-746) in normal seasons, and may rise to 3,200 riyals ($853) at peak. Accommodation in Moscow for one night usually ranges between 260 and 620 riyals ($69-165), depending on location and classification. in St. Petersburg A night often ranges between 220 and 540 riyals ($59-144). These numbers give a clear baseline before any comparison of offers. Internal transportation by metro and buses for a light day trip is often between 20 and 55 riyals ($5-15). As for traveling in private cars or intensive applications, it may reach 95-180 riyals per day ($25-48). Food per person averages between 85 and 170 riyals per day ($23-45). Always have a contingency of 10-15% above the total budget.

Budget 7 days process: Moscow 4 days + St. Petersburg 3 days

Average individual model: flight 1900 riyals ($506), 7 nights’ accommodation at an average of 360 riyals = 2520 riyals ($672), high-speed train between the two cities 260 riyals ($69), internal transportation 280 riyals ($75), food 770 riyals ($205), activities and tickets 620 riyals ($165). The approximate total is 6,350 riyals ($1,692) before the emergency clause. Average family model of 3 people: flight 5,700 riyals ($1,520), accommodation for 7 nights in a hotel apartment, 3,500 riyals ($933), internal and intercity transportation 1,450 riyals ($386), food 2,200 riyals ($586), activities 1,500 riyals ($400). The total is 14,350 riyals ($3,825), and it is preferable to add 1,500 riyals ($400) as a reserve.

Hidden expenses rack up the bill quickly

Items that surprise travelers the most: extra baggage fees, exchange rate differences on cards, overnight airport transfers, and cancellation or modification fees. In some cases, these items may add 500-1,200 riyals ($133-320) to the trip without early notice. So compare the final price after all the extras, not just the apparent base price. Before accepting any offer, check: cancellation policy, local taxes, apartment cleaning fees, and check-in timings. Delayed arrival may result in an additional night or higher transportation cost. Having an accurate arrival schedule mitigates these losses and makes financial planning closer to reality.

How to reduce the budget without losing the quality of the trip

Choose accommodation close to a major metro station, as this reduces transportation costs and saves daily time. Only book the paid activities you don't want to miss, and leave the rest of the days for a mix of open tours and free sites. With this method, the cost of entertainment usually decreases by 20-30%, while the experience remains good. Separate the flight decision from the accommodation decision, then connect them after setting the schedule. This prevents accepting an unsuitable package just because the price of one item is low. If you work with a travel company, ask for a written statement that clearly lists what is and is not included.

Execution list before final payment

  1. Confirm final flight price after baggage.
  2. Review the housing location on the map, not just in pictures.
  3. Calculate transportation between the airport and accommodation based on actual arrival times.
  4.  Add a contingency clause of no less than 10%.
  5. Save all reservations in one file.
When these steps are implemented, the cost of tourism in Russia becomes an adjustable number and not an estimate that changes every day. This increases the quality of the trip from the beginning because basic decisions are made financially before travel.

Conclusion

The most important rule is that the cost of tourism in Russia must be calculated as clear items and not as a general amount. When you know the ranges for flight, accommodation, transportation and food in dollars and riyals, you can make a more accurate booking decision and avoid surprises. use Model of the week For initial reference, then adjust the numbers according to the season and the number of travelers. In this way, the plan turns from a theoretical estimate into a practical implementation budget.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the average budget for a week in Russia?

It varies depending on the season and type of accommodation, but good planning begins with a clear division of items for flight, accommodation, transportation, food and activities rather than a general, undetailed number.

What item raises the cost the most quickly?

Mostly flying and staying in central locations. Savings on these two items are reflected more directly in the total budget than on smaller items.

How do I avoid hidden expenses?

Compare the final price after baggage, taxes and payment fees, and set aside a small daily contingency so you are not surprised by unaccounted expenses during the implementation.
