Winter tourism in Almaty

Weather in Almaty winter

If you are planning a yard in Almaty in winter, you should know that Almaty winters are long, cold and characterized by snowfall.  Snow begins to fall at the end of October and may continue until March. November usually witnesses light rain with temperatures as low as -5, but the cold increases with the arrival of December and January, reaching -3, and January is considered the coldest month of the year. Some winter visitors to Almaty prefer February, while others prefer December or early January for Christmas and New Year celebrations.

Historical and heritage visits in Almaty:

Although your trip is in the winter, tourism in Almaty in the winter gives you many other activities. You should not be without visiting other treasures of Almaty and learning about the history and heritage of the city. Zenkov Cathedral may be your first stop, as it is one of the most important tourist places in Kazakhstan. It is the focus of attention of many tourists because of its unique architectural style and the history of the region. The cathedral is considered one of the tallest wooden buildings in the world.

 There are also museums. Almaty offers a good number of museums that you can visit, such as the National Museum of Kazakhstan, the Museum of Kazakh Art, the Girchensky Museum, or as it is called the Martyr’s Museum, and the Natural History Museum.

You can also visit the Central Mosque, which is one of the tourist places in Kazakhstan that is distinguished by its distinguished architectural design. And the Almaty Tower, which gives you a panoramic view of the city and the mountains.
