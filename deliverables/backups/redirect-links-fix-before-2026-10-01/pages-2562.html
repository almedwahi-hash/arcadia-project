Tourism in Russia for Arab travelers involves practicing many activities that are not normally available in Arab countries, including snowboarding. Tourism in Moscow in winter 

How to prepare for winter weather in Moscow?

Temperatures are expected to range between -5 and -20. It will be bitter cold and heavy snow will fall, showing the city of Moscow with a wonderful, legendary scene. The month of November witnesses the beginning of coldness and light snowfall, and in December the weather is colder, and the winter season continues until March and sometimes until the beginning of April. Make sure you pack appropriate clothing for extreme weather, including thermal clothing, waterproof jackets and coats, as well as waterproof shoes. 

What are the activities to do in Moscow in winter?

During tourism in Moscow in the winter, you can practice many activities, some of which are related to the winter itself, including:

  • Skating: You have a number of rinks where you can practice ice skating, such as the famous Red Square rink, as well as Kuki Park and others. You can also skate on the designated tracks if you have previous skating experience and prefer a more difficult skating experience than the arenas.

  • Sled riding: Riding sleds is one of the unique experiences that you have probably seen in many movies and wished to try. It is one of the entertaining experiences that is most likely suitable for tourism in Russia for families, as well as tourism in Russia for young people, as it is one of the activities that delight adults and children alike.

  • Riding quad bikes (ATV): Russia has a variety of ATV bikes, which it produces to suit rough roads and difficult, winding terrain. It is one of the favorite activities for young people.

  • Cable car ride: The cable car ride is one of the most distinctive experiences. Whether you are in the summer or winter, the cable car ride and seeing a panoramic view of nature is considered the perfect experience. Add to the splendor of the scene the view of snow covering the city’s landmarks with a bright white layer, giving you a legendary scene. 

  • Snow Hut: Visiting snow huts in the Russian countryside is one of the unique experiences that is suitable for tourism in Russia in the winter, especially tourism in Russia for families. You can visit the Pine River resort and spend a wonderful time full of activities such as riding husky sleds, horseback riding, or even enjoying the amusement park. Don't miss the local meals, which give you a unique dining experience of Russian cuisine.

  • Attending events: As winter coincides with the celebration of Christmas, you can participate in some winter events such as the Journey to Christmas festival. The festival offers many activities, some of which are various artistic and musical performances.

 You can also attend professional shows that are held in squares such as Red Square and other squares. You will feel that you are inside an open theater where the music mixes with the performers in a magical, breathtaking natural winter setting.

  • Garden tours: Visiting the gardens in the winter has a special character, as the garden turns into attractive winter exhibitions, including the famous Gorky Garden.

  • Subscribe to sightseeing tours: Visiting the most famous historical places of the city is an essential part of your trip to complete the fun. Get to know  More details about important historical and cultural visits to Moscow.

Tourism  in Saint Petersburg in winter

Tourism in Russia in the winter must not be without visiting the city of Petersburg. The city has great cultural and historical value, so it is considered one of the most important cities in Russia. It is one of the favorite cities for tourism in Russia for families and tourism in Russia for young people alike. Let's learn more about Petersburg in winter.

How to prepare for winter weather in St. Petersburg?

The weather is very cold in Petersburg, especially due to its proximity to the sea, and thus it is considered colder than Moscow during the winter. Snow falls in the city and the lakes and rivers freeze in a legendary scene resembling fairy-tale scenes. You should make sure to pack clothes suitable for the cold weather in the city, such as thermal and waterproof layers. Don't forget gloves and warm wool hats.

What are the things to do in Saint Petersburg in winter?

During tourism in St. Petersburg in the winter, you can practice many activities, some of which are related to the winter itself, such as the city of Moscow, including:

Skiing: You can choose between skating on the frozen rivers and canals or skating in the city squares. Skating in arenas may be the best choice if you are a beginner to feel more safe. 

Walking around: Walking tours in the city in winter weather and getting to know its landmarks are considered among the favorite activities for many. The city looks like an open architectural museum. You will not get bored from walking tours and exploring the city. You can walk around Nevsky Prospekt, which is considered one of the city's most famous streets, as it contains luxury shops and ancient historical buildings worth seeing. You can eat in one of the restaurants or drink hot drinks in one of the cafes and enjoy the winter atmosphere.

Balloon flight: One of the distinctive activities, as you can view the city from above with its unique winter views by riding in a balloon and enjoying the scenes of snow accumulating on roofs and trees.

Cultural visits: You should not miss learning about the history, culture and architecture of the city through cultural activities such as visiting the Winter Palace, the Hermitage Museum, and visiting the Peter and Paul Fortress, which will tell you about the history of the beginning of the city itself. You can also visit St. Isaac's Cathedral, which is distinguished by its royal golden dome.

Visiting Bedouin residences: If you are thinking of immersing yourself more in the culture of the local people, you can visit Bedouin homes, try on local clothes for yourself, and take souvenir photos. You can learn more about Petersburg and the places worth visiting Saint Petersburg tourism guide

Winter events: You can participate in many winter events, such as Christmas markets, which give you a unique shopping experience. You can also attend street art and music festivals, which give you a distinct cultural experience through which you can learn about street music and enjoy various artistic performances.
