Poland is one of Europe's most rewarding and affordable destinations, blending medieval old towns, dramatic mountains, and moving historical sites. Here are the top tourist attractions in Poland to put on your 2026 itinerary.

1. Kraków Old Town & Wawel Castle

Kraków's UNESCO-listed Old Town is the heart of Poland's history, centered on Europe's largest medieval market square (Rynek Główny). Above it sits Wawel Royal Castle, the former seat of Polish kings. It's walkable, atmospheric, and ideal for families.

2. Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial

About 70 km from Kraków, this former concentration camp is now a memorial and museum. A guided visit is one of the most powerful historical experiences in Europe. Book tickets well in advance.

3. Wieliczka Salt Mine

A UNESCO World Heritage Site near Kraków, this 700-year-old salt mine features underground chapels, sculptures, and lakes carved entirely from salt — a unique experience for all ages.

4. Warsaw Old Town & Royal Castle

Poland's capital rebuilt its Old Town beautifully after WWII. Stroll the Royal Route, visit the Royal Castle, and enjoy Warsaw's modern dining and shopping scene.

5. Tatra Mountains & Zakopane

For nature lovers, the Tatra Mountains offer hiking in summer and skiing in winter. The resort town of Zakopane is the gateway, with cable cars, mountain views, and traditional wooden architecture.

6. Malbork Castle

The largest brick castle in the world, this Teutonic fortress on the Nogat River is a UNESCO site and a highlight for history and architecture fans.

7. Białowieża Forest

One of Europe's last primeval forests and home to wild European bison — a remarkable destination for wildlife and nature enthusiasts.

Best Time to Visit Poland

The ideal months are May to September, when the weather is mild and perfect for city walks, parks, and mountain trips. Winter (December-February) is great for skiing in Zakopane and Christmas markets.
