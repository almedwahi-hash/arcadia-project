The evening hours before sunset are ideal for enjoying a romantic atmosphere and golden lighting on the historical buildings. The white nights in St. Petersburg are some of the most magical natural and cultural phenomena in the world, as the sun never sets completely for several weeks in the summer, and the sky remains bright until the late hours of the night. This phenomenon occurs from early June until mid-July, and peaks during mid-June. During this period, the city turns into an open stage for art, music and celebrations. Many cultural events and music festivals are held, the most famous of which is the Scarlet Sails Festival, which attracts thousands of visitors annually.
