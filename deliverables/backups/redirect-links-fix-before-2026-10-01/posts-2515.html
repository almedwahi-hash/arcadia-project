Transportation in Kazakhstan is plentiful, making it a great opportunity for those who want family tourism in Kazakhstan, as transportation on buses costs less than half a dollar, while taxis do not exceed $2 per trip. There are also domestic flights, but they are relatively expensive, as the price of the trip starts from $50, while train trips are considered economical options, as they do not exceed $20 for long distances. There is no doubt that staying in the capital of Kazakhstan"Almaty“It is considered one of the best options for travelers who want to enjoy the local atmosphere, but at the same time its cost is the highest, but we can find here varying prices for accommodation starting from $7 for youth hostels, or $20 for medium-sized hotels, while exceeding $80 for luxury hotels. Food prices in Kazakhstan do not differ from Gulf countries such as Saudi Arabia, where the prices of food items in grocery stores are very low, so preparing your meal yourself will not cost you a large sum. For example, the price of a kilo of chilled meat starts at $8, while the price of a liter of milk will not exceed 80 cents.  But on the other hand, eating in restaurants costs you a relatively higher value, as it starts from $5 in local restaurants, and may exceed $20 in luxury restaurants, and the most famous restaurants there are: Gosti Restaurant, Alasha Restaurant, and El Mirador Restaurant. Visiting tourist attractions does not require a large cost, as a full tour throughout the day will cost you between 20 and 50 dollars, while the entrance fee to Lake Kolsai or the Altai Mountains is between 2 and 5 dollars maximum. If your trip is winter, you must visit ski resorts such as Shymbulak in order to enjoy the skiing experience for the equivalent of only 20 dollars. Among the best tourist places in Kazakhstan: Markay National Park, the Caspian Sea, Aisha Bibi Village, and Shakhstan Market. Of course, we cannot ignore the cost of shopping and buying souvenirs, as there are many malls and local markets where you can find valuable gifts, including clothes and shoes whose prices start from $60, and you can also find lower prices during sales seasons.
