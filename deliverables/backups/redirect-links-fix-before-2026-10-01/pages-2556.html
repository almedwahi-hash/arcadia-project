Russian people

Tourism in Russia is distinguished by its people. Russians are amazing in their ethnic diversity. Buryats, Yakuts, Chukchi, Cossacks, Tatars and many other nationalities have lived here since ancient times. 

Russians tend to greet each other in a formal manner, and prefer to maintain an appropriate distance from others. They usually don't start talking first, but they are friendly and pay attention to appearance and dress. They also appreciate gifts when they are invited home, and show their respect and gratitude by eating all of the food offered. Read more about the customs and traditions of the Russian people Customs and traditions of the Russian people.

Russian architecture

Slavic traditions have been carefully preserved for centuries. Despite the many difficult times the country has gone through, the culture has survived and has not lost its authenticity. People all over the country still build wooden houses from logs (huts) and take pride in their ability to shoe horses in a special way or paint traditional nesting dolls in the same way as a hundred years ago. Churches and monasteries remain a symbol of faith and unity of the people, captivating travelers with their unique architecture and peaceful atmosphere. Buddhist datsan, mosques and places of worship of pagan deities are an integral part of Russia's multinational culture.

Tourism in Russia This means, of course, Russian cities - the guardians of the country's history. Many of them are real monuments of tsarist times, works of art that largely determined the self-consciousness of the entire people.

It could easily be called St. Petersburg The most magnificent building of the imperial period. A city where ballet is part of popular culture, and whose banks are lined with an endless series of luxurious palaces.

Due to the preservation of the architectural and historical ensemble of the historical part of St. Petersburg and the absence of high-rise buildings in the center, many tourists believe that it is one of the oldest cities in the country. However, the modern capital of the Russian Federation, Moscow, is already 600 years older than its predecessor. Medieval fortresses, arsenals and castles, built during the reign of Ivan the Terrible, coexist here with megalithic buildings of the Soviet era and modern architecture. Read more about how architecture was influenced by Russia's multinational culture Russian architecture and culture.

Tourist activities in Russia 

The pristine and wild nature of Russia gives amazing freedom of action to lovers of active tourism.

In the Far East, you can see hot spring valleys, some of the most beautiful bays on Earth, reach the stunning Commander Islands and visit the real Kingdom of the Bear.

The Arctic also attracts many travelers with the cosmic beauty of the northern lights or the aurora borealis. The Caucasian part of Russia allows you to touch the mighty power of the mountains and try to conquer the highest point of Europe - Elbrus. Fans of alpine skiing will be delighted with the slopes in Sochi.

Fortunately, tourist activities in Russia are available to all travelers, not just professional adventurers. A cruise along the Volga or a trip along the Trans-Siberian Railway can be an excellent alternative to staying on the beach as usual.

Heritage tourism in Russia

Small villages in Russia have preserved their ancient traditional way of life and lifestyle. You will find yourself in the cozy, original world of the Russian village. These hamlets themselves are already cultural monuments. Here you can still sleep by the fireplace, see the rocking chair rocking, and meet the people in Russian villages.

You will see the original Russian architecture. Wooden huts and temples created without a single nail, you will get acquainted with the works of folk craftsmen: painted clay toys, crafts made of wood and birch bark, folk prints and, of course, nesting dolls - a symbol of Russia. You will appreciate the beauty of Russian costume, the exoticism of national folklore and picturesque rituals.

Family tourism in Russia 

The country's two main cities, Moscow and St. Petersburg, are excellent for holidays with children - another huge plus for tourism in Russia.

In the Hermitage, children can feel like princes and princesses; A walk through the attractive gardens of Peterhof and the opportunity to wander among the "funny rides" are great entertainment. The Central Museum of the Armed Forces will amaze any boy, because here you can climb into a tank and board a submarine.

Many coastal towns offer a wealth of entertainment for children of all ages, from dolphin shows to theme parks and poolside entertainment programmes.

A family trip to Lake Baikal will allow you to enjoy solitude with your family, and is also an excellent opportunity for hiking in the mountains with tents.

Altai will give you the opportunity to find yourself among the majestic mountains and rivers, breathe fresh air and take a break from the city noise. Horse riding of varying degrees of difficulty and kayaking on mountain rivers will be a great adventure for the whole family.
