China is a country of staggering contrasts — ancient wonders, futuristic skylines, and landscapes that range from misty mountains to neon megacities. Here are the best things to do in China for your 2026 trip.

1. Walk the Great Wall of China

The most iconic sight in China, winding over mountains for thousands of kilometers. The Mutianyu and Badaling sections near Beijing are the most accessible and scenic for visitors.

2. The Forbidden City & Tiananmen Square, Beijing

Explore the vast imperial palace complex of the Ming and Qing dynasties, right beside the world's largest public square — the historic heart of Beijing.

3. The Bund & Skyline, Shanghai

Stroll the riverfront Bund for a stunning view of Shanghai's futuristic skyline, then explore Yu Garden and the city's world-class shopping and dining.

4. Terracotta Army, Xi'an

One of the greatest archaeological discoveries of the 20th century — thousands of life-size warriors guarding the tomb of China's first emperor.

5. Li River & Guilin

Cruise the Li River past dramatic karst mountains — one of China's most beautiful natural landscapes, straight out of a classical painting.

6. Chengdu's Giant Pandas

Visit the Chengdu Research Base to see giant pandas up close — a must for families and animal lovers.
