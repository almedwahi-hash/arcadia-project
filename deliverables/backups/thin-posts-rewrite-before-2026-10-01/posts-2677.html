How to verify halal food in Warsaw

  • Ask whether all meat is halal or only selected dishes.
  • Confirm the current supplier or certification if this is important to you.
  • Check sauces and shared cooking equipment, and ask about alcohol on the premises.
  • Use vegetarian and seafood options when a restaurant cannot confirm the meat source.
