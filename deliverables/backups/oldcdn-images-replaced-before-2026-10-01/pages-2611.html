Research has shown that the healthy antioxidants found in nuts are very beneficial in maintaining health, and that the benefit of these substances ranges between two and 15 times more than vitamin E.

A study was conducted in which they compared antioxidants in nine types of nuts, namely walnuts, almonds, pistachios, hazelnuts, Brazil nuts, cashews, chickpeas, and pecans, and found that the highest percentage was found in walnuts.

They reported that the rate of antioxidants can protect the body from harmful natural chemicals associated with the pathogen, and protect against heart disease.

But they pointed out that eating raw nuts is better because heat causes a decrease in the quality of antioxidants.

The researchers stressed that eating an amount of nuts a day is a simple and healthy way to avoid heart disease.

The research was presented at the 241st National Meeting of the American Chemical Society in California.

It should be noted that Ukraine is famous for its abundant crops of walnuts, which it exports to many countries in the Arab world and elsewhere.
