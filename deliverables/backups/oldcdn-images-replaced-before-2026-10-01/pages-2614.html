In order to enter Ukraine you need:

If you will stay more than 90 days (for citizens of countries that do not require a prior visa for tourism in Ukraine), you will need a visa to Ukraine.

You cannot get a visa at the airport or at the border. You must submit an application in person at the Ukrainian embassy or consulate.

You cannot come to Ukraine before you are granted a visa.

It should be noted that in Ukraine the date is written in the day, month and year. If you try to travel to Ukraine before your visa allows you, you could be stopped at the border and asked to return at your own expense.

Ukrainian electronic visa:

On April 4, 2018, the Consular Service at the Ukrainian Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced the launch of electronic registration for entry visas to Ukraine. This service will be available from January 1, 2019, and registration will be via the Internet at the following link: (http://evisa.mfa.gov.ua)، 

This order is being implemented for the first time in Ukraine.

As for visas for tourist or commercial purposes, the Ukrainian Ministry has developed a simplified process so that those wishing can obtain a visa quickly and without going to various embassies.

Those wishing to obtain a Ukrainian visa must take simple steps by completing a set of steps through the website, uploading a scanned copy of the required documents, depositing the visa rights via a bank card (MasterCard International or Visa International payment systems), and printing an electronic visa that will reach the concerned person via his email.

Benefits of e-Visa:

Save documents remotely at any convenient time and obtain electronic visas without having to visit the consulate.

Possibility of registering the tracking status online for the electronic visa.

Central registration of all electronic visas of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine.

Uninterrupted media support via the website evisa@mfa.gov.ua.

Registration time – up to 9 business days.

Cost – 85 USD.

The validity period is from  1 day up to 30 days.
