Visa to Poland

If you are planning to visit Poland, a visa is a basic requirement for most non-European nationalities. Here is a comprehensive guide on how to apply for a Schengen visa to enter Poland:

 Types of visas

Schengen Visa

 It allows you to travel to Poland and all other Schengen countries for up to 90 days within a 180-day period. This visa is suitable for tourism, visiting relatives or business.

National Visa

It allows you to stay in Poland for a period longer than 90 days, and is intended for study, work or long-term stay.

Application requirements for a Poland visa 

To apply for a Poland Schengen visa, you will need the following documents:

  •  Poland Visa Application Form: Must be completely filled out and signed.

  •  Personal photographs: One or two passport-sized photographs (3.5 x 4.5 cm) with a white background.

  • Valid passport: It must be valid for at least 3 months after the date of your departure from the Schengen Area, and contain at least 2 blank pages.

  • Copies of passport pages: including pages containing previous visas.

  • Travel insurance: The insurance must cover medical costs amounting to at least €30,000 and is valid in all Schengen countries.

  • Airline ticket reservation: Proof of round-trip reservation (final tickets may not be required in some cases).

  • Accommodation booking: hotel reservation or invitation from a host in Poland.

  • Bank statement: proving that you have sufficient financial resources to cover your accommodation expenses as specified by the relevant Polish consulate for your circumstances.

  • Cover letter: Explaining the reason for your visit and your itinerary.

  • Proof of occupation: such as a letter from your employer or a registration certificate if you are a student.

Application steps

1. Select Poland visa type: Make sure you are applying for the correct Poland visa (Schengen or national).

2. Book an appointment: Book an appointment at the Polish Embassy or Visa Application Center in your country.

3. Collect documents: Prepare all required documents.

4. Application: Visit the embassy or visa center to submit the application and conduct an interview if necessary.

5. Payment of fees: The general Schengen visa fee is €90 for adults and €45 for children aged 6 to under 12; exemptions or other fees may apply.

6. Wait for processing: A Schengen visa decision normally takes 15 calendar days after submission and may take up to 45 calendar days in some cases; appointment wait time is separate.

Poland visa exemptions

  • Citizens of EU and EEA countries: do not require a visa to enter Poland.

  • Holders of a Schengen visa or a valid residence permit from other Schengen countries: can enter Poland without an additional visa.

Important tips

  • Application timing: Check the responsible consulate's appointment availability. Applications are generally lodged at least 15 calendar days and no more than 6 months before travel.

  • Check the requirements: Requirements may vary slightly by country, so check the website of the Polish Embassy in your country.

  • Be accurate: Ensure that all information and documents provided are correct and complete.
