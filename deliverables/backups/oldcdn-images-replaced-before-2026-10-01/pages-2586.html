Tourism in Kazakhstan is enjoyable in all seasons of the year. Every season of the year is a good opportunity to discover its beauty. Kazakhstan enjoys cold and snowy weather in winter, while the climate becomes continental in summer with temperatures exceeding 30 degrees Celsius. Language and communication in Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan is considered the ninth largest country in the world in terms of vast area, so there are many languages ​​spoken. Although the Kazakh language is the original language of Kazakhstan, it is somewhat close to the Turkish language and is spoken by the majority of the population. However, the Russian language is common, used and known by the majority of the population, and is considered the second official language in the country. The Tatar language, the Uzbek language, as well as the Uyghur and Mongolian languages ​​are also widespread in Kazakhstan.

Nature in Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan is distinguished by its distinctive geographical location between the continents of Asia and Europe, and is also distinguished by the beauty and diversity of its picturesque natural places, and this is attributed to biological diversity. Kazakhstan includes more than 25 natural parks, and types of steppes and deserts, whether sandy, salty, or clay, and northern taiga forests, mountains, and snowy peaks. The city of Almaty is surrounded by mountains from everywhere.

There are also many bodies of water, which include many lakes located in deserts, plains, and mountains, and there are also large rivers and mountain streams. Kazakhstan is also famous for its dense forests and green spaces.

Kazakhstan contains many unique places, as it has one of the largest lakes in the world, which is Lake Balkhash. It also has the Karaji depression, which is the second lowest land area in the world and its height is 132 meters below sea level.

5 Reasons to visit Kazakhstan and enjoy its charming nature

  1. Kazakhstan Home of tulips

You may be surprised to know that Kazakhstan is the home of tulips, not the Netherlands. Compared to the Netherlands, where people gather to watch the annual display of these beautiful flowers, tulips in Kazakhstan grow and bloom in the wild without human intervention and cover vast areas. Here you can see about 40 of the 65 different species of wild tulips found in Central Asia, 18 of which are listed as endangered. Among them are Shrenk and Greig tulips, which are considered one of the most beautiful.

From April to May, orange, yellow, and crimson flowers cover boundless fields and foothills in Gambyl, Turkestan, and Almaty provinces. You can see these beautiful colors while traveling long distances on the highway from South Shymkent to Almaty. With the help of our experienced tour guides, we are ready to help you identify the best locations to view and demonstrate the many types of tulips. It is forbidden to pick tulips in Kazakhstan, but you can enjoy them in an unusual and fragrant form whenever you want!

2.Otrar is the best place to take selfies

Turkestan is often visited and is known by a number of travelers and tourists interested in its historical and archaeological sites and shrines. There you can see one of the most magnificent medieval buildings in Kazakhstan, the Mausoleum of Khoja Akhmet Yazawi. But not many know that there is another amazing site – the ancient city of Otrar, which is on the way to Turkestan, if you are coming from Shymkent.

Scientists and archaeologists are still studying Otrar. Most of the old city buildings have been restored and preserved. This is one of the few historical places in the country, where you can walk through the old streets and take some interesting photos and some selfies as well.

If you want to get to know Otrar, it is better to be prepared in advance – to get information from the Internet, to watch the movie called “The death of Otrar” or you can order an excursion with guides in our office.

Otrar

3. The black beach of Lake Acholia with therapeutic waters

If you are tired of the ubiquitous regular golden sandy beaches, it's time to get acquainted with the black-pebble beach of Lake Alakolia, which is located in southwestern Kazakhstan. The lake not only has an unusual coastline, but also has healing powers. Black Beach attracts people suffering from colds, skin diseases, nervous breakdowns and all those who like to meditate. Even Soviet cosmonauts used to come to the lake after their flights to rehabilitate here.

Black Acholia Beach: The Alakol Nature Reserve was created here to preserve rare species of birds, so you can find the famous silver sea bass in its waters. If you come to the lake at the beginning of May, you can join the many bird watchers who come for the “Wings of Alakol” festival. This event attracts not only bird watchers but also journalists, yoga practitioners and musicians. This is how the season begins on Alakol. Moreover, summer – the time of sun, swimming and water entertainment. In the fall, there is another festival celebrated on the first weekends of September – “Okunkol”, where fishermen catch the famous silver bass.

4. Visit Baikonur, the first and largest astronomical base

When you are tourism in Kazakhstan, you may be surprised to know that Baikonur Cosmodrome is the first and largest space base in the world, located in the Kyzylorda Province of Kazakhstan. Until 2050, the Cosmodrome is leased to the Russian Federation, but it is legally owned by Kazakhstan and the country's tourism companies can organize tours of rocket and satellite launches. Don't you want to visit the place where the first successful flight into space took place?

5. Delicious Kazakh cuisine 

 You cannot resist tasting the delicious food of Kazakh cuisine. Many of you may find it surprisingly interesting and you will soon learn about what we actually eat here in Kazakhstan. With this, you should know that the people of Kazakhstan eat only healthy and natural food, and a lot of dairy products.

Nowadays, gastronomic tourism in Kazakhstan is becoming more and more popular. You will not find such a variety of national cuisine and diverse flavors anywhere in the world as Kazakh cuisine. This is due to 135 nationalities living in Kazakhstan. But its highlight is the Kazakh cuisine, which is based on the traditions of the nomads of the great steppes. These delicious, healthy and “smart” Bedouin foods have become very popular among travelers these days. Get to know me Traditional Kazakh cuisine.

You can try famous Kazakh people's dishes such as beshbarmak (boiled meat with dough strips), burqas (fried puff bread), kazi (horse meat sausage), eremchik (crumbly cottage cheese) in many cafes and restaurants. But it will be interesting to try the national cuisine in the villages of the two ethnic groups. Guests are especially invited to try many unique dishes, which are prepared according to the collected recipes. They will also explain to you how these dishes are cooked and tell you the history in detail behind the myths and unusual stories about these dishes.
