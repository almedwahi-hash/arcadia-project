Winter holidays in the Carpathians in  Kosino: warm water from the fountain and unique sensations  Winter is a great time to plunge into the warm thermal waters. The unique resort is located in the Ukrainian village of Kosino, in a health center. As water is poured on you from a giant mug, you can enjoy drinking a cup of coffee... The highlight of it is the incredible fountain, as if it were hovering in the air!
You do not like frost and do not like extreme cold and often ask yourself this question: where can I relax in Ukraine In the winter, but I don't freeze from the cold? The answer is here! The temperature in this warm jacuzzi is always +41 ... 42 ° C and in the thermal complex there are 7 original saunas: from salt to eucalyptus. There is also an ice cave. By the way, thanks to these baths in which a person can bathe, you can not only diversify leisure time, but also do a lot of healing, such as: restoring the activity of the nervous system, eliminating problems of the musculoskeletal system, the digestive system, and the cardiovascular system. Cost: 3 hours in the thermal complex costs 450 UAH, all day - 900 UAH.  Where to stay: In the Kosino Thermal Waters complex there is a cozy hotel Iváncsó Birtok. Getting there: By bus or taxi from the city of Beregovo (from Rakovtsy Square) about 20 km away. By train from Uzhgorod. You need to get out at Kosino station, which is located in the village of Zapson. After taking a bus along Beregovo - Chop route.   GPS coordinates: 48.259972, 22.492707. A winter tale in Izki In order to see a real vacation in the Karpaty and feel the atmosphere of natural comfort, it is necessary to go to the village of Izki in Transcarpathia at least once during the entire winter season 2020. In the local tourist complex, they have equipped everything  for tourism so that the tourist, with the usual comfort, feels the spirit of the mountains. The wooden houses in Izki really create a wonderful atmosphere at any time of the year and provide an opportunity to isolate yourself from excessive fuss at least for a while. And rest here in the winter is perfect! The area of ​​the Izki complex is 28 hectares. Therefore, at local events, even during the New Year and Christmas celebrations, tourists will feel free. There are two lifts operating on Mount Magura: one of them does not stop the lift even in summer: there are beautiful landscapes. The tracks are of medium difficulty, wide enough (width up to 200 meters, track length - about 3 kilometers). Therefore, in Izkah it will be interesting and fun for both experienced skiers and beginners. For those who are not confident in their skiing abilities, there is a ski school. For lovers of warmth and relaxation, a wonderful gift will be the Carpathian bath in Izka and the services of professional masseuses. In addition, the eco-friendly resort offers many interesting attractions: sleigh rides, snow tubing, winter cycling. Savvy connoisseurs of Karpati products will be very interested in visiting the local cheese factory. And of course  taste the delicious local cheese!
