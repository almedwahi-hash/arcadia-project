Who does not need a Kazakhstan visa?


Visa-free entry with the right to stay in the country for up to 30 days is granted to citizens of the following countries:

Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Indonesia, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Kuwait, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Malta, Mexico, Monaco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand, United Kingdom, USA, Vatican, Vietnam.

Visa-free entry with the right to stay in the country for up to 90 days is granted to citizens of the following countries: Armenia, Belarus, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Mongolia, Russia, Tajikistan, Ukraine.

Hong Kong citizens can enter Kazakhstan without a visa with the right to stay in the country for up to 14 days.

Until December 31, 2020, a 72-hour visa-free entry, stay and departure regime from the Republic of Kazakhstan was in effect for citizens of China and India traveling through international airports in Nur-Sultan, Almaty, Aktau, Shymkent, Karaganda and Taraz to a third country or returning from a country. As of April 30, 2021, this system is suspended until December 31, 2021.

How to apply for a Kazakhstan visa?

For foreign citizens who need a Kazakhstan visa, today there are two options for obtaining it:

The first option

Visas are issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan and foreign agencies and consular centers of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan. In the absence of this, by specially authorized representatives of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Multiple visas may be issued to foreigners at the request of the host organizations.

The second option 

Resuming the issuance of the Kazakhstan electronic visa

From June 13, 2022

The Ministry of Internal Affairs and the National Security Committee have resumed the service of issuing electronic KZ visas (e-Visa) through the Visa-immigration Portal vmp.gov.kz.

Citizens of 109 countries* can apply for one of the following categories of single-entry e-visas:

Business B1, B2, B3

Tourist B12

For medical treatment C12

The Kazakhstan electronic visa is issued based on the invitation letter by the KZ (individual or legal entity). The invitation is only valid for the visa recipient, does not apply to accompanying persons and is not subject to renewal.

To obtain a Kazakhstan electronic visa, a foreigner must:

  • Registration on the portal vmp.gov.kz

  • Follow the “E-Visa” section and enter the invitation number (the invitation number is forwarded by Kazakhstan)

  • Fill in the required passport details and proceed to pay the consular fee ($60 for one-time tourist visa, $80 for business visa and medical visa)

  • Print the electronic visa and present it at passport control at one of Kazakhstan's international airports

  • Since June 13, access to the Visa and Immigration Portal of the Republic of Kazakhstan for issuing electronic visas has been resumed. vmp.gov.kz.

Principles for issuing a Kazakhstan visa and extending its validity



The reasons for issuing entry visas must be compatible with the purpose of residence. Thus, those who arrive in Kazakhstan at the invitation of friends and relatives, i.e. for a special purpose, as well as those who arrive for work, study or tourism, cannot be employed in Kazakhstan, engaging in other activities not related to the purpose of entry indicated in the visa, except as provided for by the legislative acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

The category and types of visas, the procedure for issuing them, as well as the reduction and extension of their validity are determined by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan in coordination with the National Security Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Foreigners traveling through the territory of Kazakhstan on their way to visa-exempt countries are required to obtain a transit visa from Kazakhstan.

Traffic through the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan with vehicles, including those engaged in international transportation of goods, should pass only roads open to international traffic.

Do I need to register with the immigration service after arrival?

30 days without registration

Since 2020, in accordance with the law signed on December 30, 2019, mandatory registration for foreigners staying in Kazakhstan for up to 30 days has been abolished. However, the receiving party remains obliged to notify the Immigration Service of the arrival of a foreign guest within three working days from the moment of crossing the border. Access can be notified online, through www.vmp.gov.kz.

The service is free and has been provided by the Ministry of Interior since 2014.

If a foreign citizen stays in Kazakhstan for more than 30 days

When a foreign citizen stays in Kazakhstan for more than 30 days, the receiving party (hotels, hostels, rented apartments, medical institutions, other legal entities and individuals) must issue a temporary residence permit (Kazakhstan registration).

Please note that registration of aliens and notification of the arrival of aliens in Kazakhstan are two separate duties that are now assigned to the receiving party.
