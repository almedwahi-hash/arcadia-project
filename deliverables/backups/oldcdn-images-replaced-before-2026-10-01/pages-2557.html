Cities in Russia

Tourist cities in Russia

Russia is a world of countless tourist opportunities. Although the most famous tourist cities in Russia are Moscow and St. Petersburg, there are many tourist cities in Russia, each of which has a distinct and different character. Here we will learn about the most important tourist cities in Russia. 

Information about tourism in Russia


Welcome to Moscow

Moscow is the capital and the main station in the tourist cities of Russia. The Russian capital is crowded with museums, art galleries, theaters, and beautiful old buildings. You will find something beautiful everywhere, even during your trip on the metro and entering the luxurious marble stations. When you've had enough of sightseeing, you can go shopping in the markets and malls.

Highlights and activities in Moscow

Visit Red Square: Red Square is the best tourist attraction in Moscow and is bordered on one end by the Kremlin and on the other end by St. Basil's Cathedral, which is distinguished by its bulbous domes.

Real shopping pleasure: There is also the famous department store, GOM, in Red Square, which is famous for its arched corridors and charming and picturesque wrought-iron balconies. Do not forget to enjoy watching it when it is lit up at night. It is also home to many fashion houses. Serious shoppers should head to Okhotny Ryad Mall and upscale department store Tsum.

Gorky Park: It is the place preferred by Muscovites to picnic, play table tennis and relax in the midst of this vibrant city. In the summer you can enjoy going to the outdoor cinema and in the winter you can skate on the ice rink.

Buy souvenirs in the market: If you want to pick up some authentic Russian gifts, just head to the Izmailovo Market where you can find polished miniatures, expertly painted wooden handicrafts and Russian dolls.

Getting to know some cultural aspects: Visit the Tretyakov Gallery, which displays more than 100,000 works of art dating from the 10th to the 20th centuries. Don't miss the opportunity to enjoy opera or ballet concerts at the Bolshoi Theater.

Welcome to Saint Petersburg

The most famous tourist city in Russia at all is the City of White Nights, which is considered the second largest in terms of area among Russian cities after Moscow. Its name goes back to its founder, Tsar Peter the Great, and the city is distinguished by its charming architectural style and unique location. You can think of St. Petersburg on the Neva River as a window to Europe.

Saint Petersburg is a city of history, culture, entertainment, nightlife and distinctive restaurants, so; It is one of the favorite tourist destinations in Russia. 

Feature landmarks and activities in Saint Petersburg

Palace Square: It is one of the largest public squares in the world and dates back to the eighteenth century. One of the important landmarks of the square is the Alexandrian Column, made of red granite and 47.5 meters high. The column commemorates the victory of  Emperor Alexander I over Napoleon. The column, which is considered one of the tallest columns in the world, is considered a symbol of the Russian Empire.

Hermitage Museum and Winter Palace: The largest and oldest museum in the world and contains more than 3 million artistic, cultural and historical pieces. The museum takes you on a journey through world cultures, starting from Greek and Roman antiquities and extending to contemporary art. The museum consists of 5 buildings overlooking the Neva River, the most important of which is the Winter Palace. The Winter Palace follows Russian Baroque architecture and was the residence of the Russian Tsar for hundreds of years.

Peterhof PalaceThe palace, its gardens, and its fountains have a very distinctive design. The palace was rebuilt after World War II and is considered one of the favorite destinations for many. The palace buildings include the main Grand Palace and other small palaces spread across the main palace gardens.

Peter and Paul Fortress: The castle is an architectural masterpiece and dates back to the eighteenth century. The castle was built as a defensive fortress. Through the castle towers, you can see a distinctive view of the city. The castle is the nucleus of the city of St. Petersburg. The castle also includes the Peter and Paul Cathedral, which has an artistic design and a huge golden dome.

Catherine PalaceOne of the most distinguished Baroque palaces in the world, the palace was destroyed during World War II and then beautifully restored afterward. The most distinctive feature of the palace is the “Amber Room.” The room is completely lined with amber, gold, and mirrors. 

Saint Petersburg ZooThe park includes about two thousand animals, and the park includes a veterinary village, by visiting which you can follow the care that the animals receive. The park is a great destination for family tourism. Do not miss also visiting the summer gardens, which are rich in fountains and sculptures.

Tips for travelers:

Kronstadt "Kronstadt" is one of the Russian cities affiliated with the federal entity Saint Petersburg. The city is located on Kotilin Island and was a naval base. Do not miss visiting the Naval Cathedral, built in the Byzantine style and distinguished by its decorative interior. You can visit Petrovsky Park, its monument to Peter the Great, and the Kronstadt Historical Museum. Looking across the beach you can see Fort Alexander Island, which is a man-made island.

As for the city of Pushkin, it is only 24 km from St. Petersburg, and it is the city that bears the name of the prince of Russian poets, Alexander Pushkin. The city looks like a fantasy city rich in palaces, gardens and parks, and it is an opportunity to learn about an era in Russia's history. The city is definitely worth a visit.

Welcome to Kazan "Kazan"

Kazan is very different from other tourist cities in Russia. The city is located above the Volga and Kazanka rivers and has distinctive sandy beaches. In the winter, the city is covered in snowy white as it freezes completely. Russians consider it the third capital of Russia after Moscow and Saint Petersburg. Highlights and activities in Kazan

Qul Sharif MosqueThe mosque has a unique design and its courtyard area is very large. You can learn more Qul Sharif Mosque in Kazan.

Temple of all religions: The site is a museum and includes a mosque, church, synagogue and buildings for other faiths. The site is designed to look like a complex of religions in peaceful coexistence, and its architecture is very unique in its design and overlapping colors as well.

Lake Gabon: Also called the Black Lake. The lake is surrounded by a walking path, gardens, and fountains and is a great place for a quiet stroll amidst clean green spaces and serene nature.

Riviera "Water Park": It is considered one of the largest water parks in the world. The place is great for family tourism and friends' trips full of movement and activity.

Bowman Street: The street is like an artery extending through the city and includes restaurants, cafes, and some performers and presenters of street performances such as singing, dancing, and playing. You can shop for gifts and souvenirs from the shops. The street also includes historical buildings, monuments, churches and fountains. Enjoy a street tour that has everything you are looking for.

Kremlin building: The main castle in Tatarstan, which is classified as a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. The building was the only Tatar fortress in Russia. The site includes historical buildings dating back to the sixteenth and nineteenth centuries and some traces of structures dating back to the tenth and sixteenth centuries.

Welcome to Irkutsk

The city is located at the confluence of the Irkut and Wangara rivers. The city was founded in the seventeenth century to be a military fortress. The city was also used as a trading center with China.

Welcome to Sviyazhsk Island

The island includes a castle dating back to the era of Ivan the Terrible in the Middle Ages. The island mixes the magic of nature with history and culture. The island includes several landmarks such as the Monastery of the Ascension and the Monastery of John the Baptist.
