What to see in Odessa and what places are worth visiting?

We have collected some interesting places in Odessa because it is not possible to collect all the information and present it in one article as this would require a very long time. Therefore, we will now only talk briefly about the most popular places in Odessa:

Odessa Opera and Ballet Theater

View of the main opera building in OdessaOpera building from the inside

Surely we can call this building the city's great theatre. The Opera Building is the best choice for architecture and art lovers to go and see in Odessa. The history of this building dates back to the 19th century. We recommend visiting the building and seeing it not only from the outside but also from the inside. The main hall has unique acoustics – even in its far corners, the whisper of anyone on stage will be clearly audible. The building was designed from the inside in a style French Rococo.

Potemkin stairs

Stairs leading to the Odessa Naval Station 

One of the most important places in the city, which is mentioned and included in all lists of “Main Attractions of Ukraine”. The design was created in the 19th century by order of Vorontsov. The prince ordered its construction as a gift to his beloved wife. The runway consists of approximately 200 steps. Tourists must try it.

Monument to the Duke de Richelieu

Bronze monument to Duke 

Bronze monument to the Duke (Duke) de Richelieu on Primorsky Avenue in Odessa

Richelieu is one of the most important founders of the city Odessa. Thanks to his efforts, the city became the largest seaport. The memorial is located at the top of the stairs. Events, festivals, and some comedy shows are held next to it. In addition, there is a belief that by touching the money bag on the statue, you can become richer and more successful in business.

Alderyasavskaya Street 

Alderyasavskaya Street 

Deribas Street – one of the important streets and the center of Odessa

It is a walkway in the city center, which will allow you to experience the color of the cityOdessa Unique. Whatever your short trip, a walk along this street  with its shops, cafes and lanes is a must and is considered one of the first places to visit. Here are the best hotels in Odessa. Most of the buildings were built in the nineteenth century. Many of the houses that have survived to our time belong to world-famous personalities.

Primorsky Bulvar

Primorsky Bulvar

Primorsky Street is one of the most favorite places for tourists.

You can access the street by climbing stairs. In Odessa it is often recommended to start your acquaintance with the city from this place. The view from this street is that you can enjoy the view of the coastal station. The list of attractions located here includes a museum with a glass dome, which stores the remains of the settlement of the ancient Greeks.

Vorontsov Palace

Southeast facade of Odessa Vorontsov PalaceThis palace was built in 1834 AD and was owned by a member of the Vorontsov family. The house was almost destroyed during the Crimean clashes. After the war with the Anglo-French army, only ruins remained of the palace. After that, in the future, it was decided to restore the historical building and use it again as a residence for a ruler. Odessa. This place also had a gym for men, and then a center for creative children.

Shah's palace

Shah's palace 

The palace of Shah Muhammad Ali was built in the 19th century Gothic style  

One of the magnificent works of Gothic architecture, the palace got this name  because  of the fact that Muhammad Ali (Shah of Persia) lived here. The Persian king had to flee to the Ukrainian city in connection with the revolution in Iran. Today this building built in the 19th century is used as offices for private companies.

Spaso-Preabrazhentsky Cathedral

Spaso-Preabrazhentsky Cathedral 

Spaso-Preabrazhentsky Cathedral – Foyle Orthodox Cathedral in Odessa

The city's largest church for Orthodox parishioners is located on Cathedral Square in the center of the city. Initially, this beautiful white-walled building was a modest temple. Then the complex was restored in the twentieth century. If you want to see one of the sights of Odessa then come here.

Passage

Interior view of the building

This elegant building, built in the 19th century, is located in the city center on Deribas Street. Your trip to Odessa should definitely include a visit to this complex. The interior of the courtyard resembles a building people In the Russian capital. Inside in the arcade with glass domes, Baroque sculptural compositions on the facades and intricate arched windows, there are shops, restaurants, an indoor shopping arcade and a hotel.

Bolshaya Moskovskaya Hotel

Bolshaya Moskovskaya Hotel

A five-storey luxury building in the former hotel was called Bolshaya Moskovskaya

This famous hotel, which was built in the 20th century. It is located in the middle of the main Deribas Street and is considered a wonderful masterpiece, but until now, after its restoration and preservation of its beautiful appearance, it has not been opened or used, but it is still one of the beautiful landmarks of the city with its wonderful architectural style.

Bristol Hotel

View of the Bristol or Krasnaya Hotel

View of the Bristol or Krasnaya Hotel from Pushkinskaya Street Odessa 

Another historical building created in the 19th century. According to the current classification the hotel has Five stars. And even previously it was considered the most luxurious and expensive in the city. The last reconstruction and restoration in it was carried out for 8 years (until 2010). The building has been restored to the appearance it originally had.

The one-wall house

An optical illusion view of the famous house

General view of the “Witch House” in Odessa city

There is an exceptional architectural structure on Primorsky Boulevard. If you look at the building from a certain angle, it will seem to you that the house has no side walls. The original effect of this is due to the triangular design of the house structure. The other name of the house is the Witch House.

Pete Falz Finn

A view of Falzvin's house 


This building is in a city Odessa , which is decorated with sculptures of atlas, on whose shoulders lies the sky vault, one of the most beautiful architectural objects of the city. The sculptures are not bas-reliefs, they are separate elements. In previous years the house belonged to the wealthy Pfalz-Finn family.

Odessa Museum of Western and Eastern Art

Odessa Museum of Western and Eastern Art

Museum of Western and Eastern Art in the Palace of Count Alexander Mikhailovich Abaza 

The museum complex, which began its work in 1923, carefully preserves a collection of art pieces from the museum Hermitage in St. Petersburg , Kyiv Museum and special exhibitions. The pieces belong to antiques of different ancient eras.

Odessa Art Museum

Odessa Art Museum

Art Museum in Naryshkin Palace in the center Odessa

The museum was opened in the Potocki Castle, built in the 19th century. The first elements of the exhibition organization came from the Academy of Arts in St. Petersburg. Currently  the halls are galleries of arts, crafts, drawings, paintings and icons.

Catacombs of Odessa (Catacomb)

The catacombs of death, as some call them

A tour of the underground mazes and basements Odessa Which is considered the longest in the world, with a length of 2,500 kilometers. It consists of stone mines (quarries) and natural caves found from the birth of the city of Odessa and playing an important role throughout its history.

These basements were used by criminals, smugglers, revolutionaries and resistance fighters during World War II.

One of these events was in the village of Nyrobaiskaya, where there is now a museum 14 meters underground
