Astana city

The capital of the Republic of Kazakhstan is the city of Astana. It is located in the northern part of the country on the Ishim River. On the left bank of Ishim stands the symbol of the capital - the modern monument "Peterek" with a height of 97 meters. The tower's observation deck offers a stunning panoramic view of the city. In June 2017, the capital of Kazakhstan received the status of a city with a population of one million. The basis of its economy consists of: trade, transportation, communications, and construction. Petrik is the main symbol of Astana and one of the main attractions of Kazakhstan.

Almaty city 

One of the largest cities with a population of more than 1.8 million people is the city of Almaty, which is rightfully considered the cultural, financial and economic center of the country. It is located in the center of the Eurasian continent, in the southeast of the Republic of Kazakhstan, in the foothills of the Trans-Ili-Alatau-Northern Tien Shan mountain range. Many tourist routes start from here. In just a few days, when traveling to Almaty in winter you can visit the snowy mountain peaks and descend into the valley to the sights of the desert. 

Shymkent city

In June 2018, Shymkent was also recognized as a city of republican importance. Earlier Shymkent was the administrative center of the South Kazakhstan (now Turkestan) region. Shymkent is the third largest city in Kazakhstan in terms of population and the first in terms of occupied area, and is one of its largest industrial, commercial and cultural centers.

Kazakhstan is a multiethnic country, representatives of more than 120 nationalities and nationalities live here, so the principles of tolerance have been elevated to the status of state policy.

Kazakhstan is a secular, multi-confessional country. The main widespread religious denominations: Islam and Christianity.

Tourism in Kazakhstan 

The head of state himself recently identified tourism as a priority sector of the economy. And although there is still a lot of work ahead regarding improving infrastructure, transportation logistics and other important aspects of tourism development, guests are welcome in Kazakhstan, and a warm welcome, as you know, is sometimes more important than accommodation in a five-star hotel! Kazakh hospitality, coming from the depths of centuries, remains relevant today.

Here ancient culture and customs coexist with modernity, and the eastern traditions of Turkish civilization - with Western modernity.
