Early reservations for tourism offers

In Europe, European tourists have long and continuously used the benefits of early booking. For example, in the United Kingdom, about 85% of tourists book their trips in advance; In other European countries the numbers are similar. This is because discounts on tours and excursions for early booking may reach 40%. This service deserves your attention as a tourist to save you money and time and ensure early quality!

What is early booking?

Early Booking is a promotion jointly developed between operators, hotels and airlines. This means that tour prices are reduced when booked in advance.

How are these savings possible?

It is profitable for the hotel owner to sell some rooms before the start of the season, even at a lower price, to ensure occupancy during the season, and to plan and implement the costs of preparing for the season. The same can be said about tour operators and airlines, for whom ensuring future product sales is beneficial and necessary.

What are the benefits of early booking for tourists? Benefits of early booking tours

1. Tourists can save up to 40% of their money when purchasing a tour (and this does not affect its quality!) through early booking promotions. As a rule, discounts on early booking tours are 10-30%. For example, on a tour that costs $1000, this is a large amount of money ($100-300), and this amount would be worthwhile to save for the tourist.

2. Early booking is also beneficial because with it, you don't have to worry about fluctuations in exchange rates (and during the season, the price usually rises). Once you have paid for the tour, any changes in the foreign exchange market will not affect the cost of the tour. There will be no need to pay any additional amounts in the event of an increase in hotel prices, air travel or other expenses.

3. Availability of the best hotels and comfortable flights. A good hotel is a hotel in high demand. It should be remembered that tourists from all over the world come to quality hotels, especially from countries where early booking is common, so all the best hotels are likely to be sold out in advance.

4. No worries and no fuss associated with preparing documents to obtain a visa. This especially applies to countries that require a visa. Some certificates, permits  and paperwork require a long time to process.

5. The fun of planning and anticipating a future vacation. When we know that your trip is booked and a sense of impressions and complete relaxation awaits us ahead, we work differently - more inspired and easier.

What do you need to know when booking early?

When you book early, you can first pay a certain percentage of the trip cost, and pay the other part directly before departure. When booking an early bird tour, you will be required to pay within several days after making your reservation.
