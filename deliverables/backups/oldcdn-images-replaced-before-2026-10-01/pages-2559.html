Tourist offers Russia

Enjoy the best tourist offers in Russia and enjoy visiting the best Russian cities

Cultural tours  to Russia are a great opportunity to discover a huge country with a population of about 150 million people, home to more than 160 nationalities and a rich and diverse culture that has developed over thousands of years.

You will be able to see how Russian people live today among the historical monuments of Moscow, in imperial St. Petersburg and in the ancient cities of the Golden Ring. Discover how geography has shaped the lives of people living along Europe's longest river, the Volga, around one of the world's largest freshwater lakes, Lake Baikal, and in the Siberian steppe.

You'll experience Russia's diverse and fascinating culture by visiting some of the country's 18 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Historical and ethnographic museums, Orthodox churches and shamanic villages await you. Decorative and applied arts of the Russian people, cuisine, religious traditions and holidays.

From one-day individual tours in and around historic cities to multi-week in-depth cultural excursions along the Siberian or Volga Rivers, our Russian cultural tours have something to suit all tastes.
