Information about sunflower oil

Sunflower oil is the oil extracted from sunflower seeds (Helianthus annuus). It is characterized by its pale yellow color and mild flavour, because it is rich in vitamin E and polyunsaturated fats. It contains small amounts of saturated fat, is popular in cooking and salad dressings, and is also used as a skin moisturizer because it contains vitamin E and skin-protecting properties.

Benefits of sunflower oil

Sunflower oil provides many health benefits to the human body, most notably:

❤️ Reducing cholesterol

It helps reduce total cholesterol and bad cholesterol levels in the blood.

🦶 Treatment of athlete's foot

Studies indicate its effectiveness in treating athlete's foot when applied to the skin for 6 weeks.

🩺 Reducing arteriosclerosis

It can reduce plaque in the arteries of people with atherosclerosis.

✨ Protect the skin

It contains vitamin E with antioxidant properties that reduce cell damage.

Benefits of sunflower seeds

Sunflower seeds are characterized by their white and black shells and contain edible seeds that are rich in beneficial nutrients. Its most prominent benefits:

  • Good fats for health: reduce bad cholesterol levels, reduce the risk of heart attacks, regulate blood sugar levels, lower blood pressure.
  • Rich in Vitamin E: It has antioxidant properties that reduce cell damage caused by free radicals.
  • Contains selenium: important for DNA production and reduces oxidative stress.
