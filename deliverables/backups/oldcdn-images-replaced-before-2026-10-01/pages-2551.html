The best places to engage in fishing activity in Kazakhstan

Hunting wild animals

  • The Tasmuryn fishing area is located in the eastern part of the Ili district of the Almaty region. The distance from Almaty to the fishing area is 130-150 kilometers, depending on the fishing area. The hunting grounds are characterized by a slightly undulating plain of varying elevations. In the eastern part of the fishing area, a tributary of the Kurta River flows into the Ili River. To the west, the hunting area's territory includes the Moenkoma Sands. On the hunting farm it is possible to hunt Siberian deer, wild boar, fox, badger, hare, as well as pheasant and ducks. The main hunting direction is pheasants and wild boars. The Tasmuryn hunting area is actively engaged in game farming. The spacious enclosures contain wild boars that anyone can hunt.

  • The fishing place “Bartogai” is located in the Enbekshikazakh district of  Almaty. The distance from the city is about 200 kilometers in the south-eastern direction of the Almaty-Kygin highway. The eastern border extends along the Almaty-Kygin road. The southern border of the Bartogay Fishing Area runs along the Toraygir Ridge watershed and is bordered by the fishing area of ​​the same name to the south. Toraigyr is one of the eastern spurs of the Trans-Ili Alatau, located above the Karagili peak above sea level.

  • Matai hunting farm is located in the Kerbulak district of Almaty. The area of ​​the fishing area is also vast. Located  in a mountainous area, on the northern slopes of the Matai and Altyn-Emel mountain ranges, it is the spurs of the Dzhungar Alatau. The upper limits reach 1,500 meters above sea level. The climate is sharply continental with large seasonal and daily temperature fluctuations, low precipitation in the flat part of the territory, dry summers and little snow in winter.

  • As for the “Toraigyr” hunting farm in the Kegen region of Almaty. The distance from the city is about 300 kilometers in the southeast direction of the Almaty-Zhalanash highway. The Toraigyr farm occupies the southern slopes of the Toraigyr range and part of the adjacent Zhalanash valley. The western border of the farm is the Chilek River valley, and in the east the farm border runs partly along the Charyn River. fishing

  • Akshiyskaya Manor  Akshiyskaya is a place that many Almaty fishermen know and love. It is convenient because you can fish here in summer and winter. There are piers with pavilions on the lakes, and artesian water is supplied to each sector. The waters contain carp, grass carp, trout and Siberian sturgeon. 2- Sports and tourist activities in Kazakhstan in the winter

There are many tourist activities in Kazakhstan and winter sports are especially distinctive for tourists who come from countries that do not have suitable weather for snowfall. Snow falls in Kazakhstan during the winter from November to March. There are a variety of winter activities and means of skiing, such as skiing with sleds, boards, or balloons, and means suitable for children as well, riding in dog sleds, walking, and horseback riding through the snow trails, and other activities.

The best places to practice tourist activities and winter sports in Kazakhstan

Snowboarding in Kazakhstan 

Snowboarding is one of the winter sports and tourist activities in Kazakhstan that is loved by many people of all levels. Kazakhstan is considered the ideal place for practicing skiing, as it offers many resorts and ski tracks in mountainous areas that are covered with snow during the winter.

  • The slopes of Zailiiysky Alatau  in Almaty are one of the favorite places for skiing. These slopes are very steep, so there are many ski resorts in the area providing the best services. The slope is a sunny and scenic mountain area worth visiting.

  • The Medeu track is a huge ski track and is considered one of the world famous tracks. It is a great opportunity if you wish to practice skiing but are afraid of skiing on the mountain slopes, especially if you are not a professional skier.

  • Shymbulak is the perfect place for those looking for a challenge and those who do not find any difficulty in skiing can visit the resort of Shymbulak. The product is not far from Almaty, as the distance can be covered in only 30 minutes by car. The Chemolac product is distinguished by providing multiple skiing tracks. You can choose the track that suits your abilities. Some of the trails are for beginners and others are trails with listed slopes, which is among the most difficult slopes in the world. The resort provides elevators to serve visitors and many of the best and latest ski equipment and is considered one of the famous ski destinations in the region.

  • Skiing in Pavlodar is also a distinctive experience. You can choose from more than one ski resort. You can also practice frame skiing in Pavlodar, which is fun for adults and children.Snowboarding in Kazakhstan

3- Sports and other tourist activities in Kazakhstan 

Walking and horse riding In Kazakhstan

Walking and horse riding are popular sports. There are many walking and horse riding trails in the mountainous areas of Kazakhstan, and they are unique experiences where you can practice them and enjoy the charming natural landscapes of Kazakhstan. However, if you choose to practice horse riding during the winter, the pleasure is doubled by walking on the trails and enjoying the view of the mountains covered in white snow. Among the distinguished tracks:

  • Bora Bay is the best choice for nature and forest lovers as you can pass through forest trails and meet some wild animals.

  • Kolsai Lakes are separated by horse riding trails. Here you will feel that you are inside a painting where the colors of the mountain slopes and the water tones of the lakes blend with the sprawling trees and colorful flowers with the greenery of the plains. Some of these paths are somewhat steep, but do not worry, as the horses used on these trips are calm and trained.

  • Other horse riding areas: Zailiiysky Alatau, where the atmosphere resembles a cowboy movie.  Kungei Alatau is recommended for professional riders more than beginners. Alam Ati Park There are many other areas. It is enough for you to search in the cities you intend to visit for the best horse trails available to ensure the most suitable for you. Taste the food

Food tasting is one of the favorite tourist activities in Kazakhstan for many, as it is one of the doors to learning about other cultures, the nature of peoples, and the secrets of their cuisine. Do not miss tasting beshbarmak, which is the national dish of Kazakhstan and is made from lamb or beef. Qaras, which is fermented mare's milk, is very popular. Cocktail, which is smoked carp and grilled in the oven. Burchak, which resembles a donut, is fried dough with different mixtures, including savory and sweet, and it is one of the local recipes.
