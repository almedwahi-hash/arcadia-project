Baths in the Carpathians and Zakarpattia: Yaremsh, Lomshori, Yaremshi, Bukovel, Vorokhta In the Ukrainian Carpathians and Zakarpattia, regardless of the season and all year round you can bathe in these baths, which are popularly called the “Carpathian jacuzzi”. The water in it is heated to a temperature of 40 - 45 ° C, and for the best therapeutic effect, essential oils and mixtures of medicinal plants are added to the basin. Immersion in hot water improves human health, tones the body, soothes the nervous system, eliminates insomnia, and improves skin condition. This spa is used to prevent rheumatism and joint diseases. Carpathian basins are kettles of Cast iron These are therapeutic baths that can be visited throughout the year. It is called the "Carpathian Jacuzzi". Enjoying a spa bath in the Carpathians is a kind of “special feature” of the Carpathians. The water in the tanks is heated to a temperature of 40-45 degrees Celsius. Under the basin, a fire is lit, and to prevent anyone from getting burned, flat river stones are placed at the bottom of the basin. This type of basin is a popular tradition and originated a long time ago, in Austria and Hungary. The basin was installed near healthy water sources. The person is immersed in the basin and the water used can be different - healthy water and sulfur water. A mixture of medicinal plants collected from the valleys of the natural Carpathian region is added to it. Hot baths help in relaxing, strengthening the body, calming the nerves, eliminating insomnia, treating skin diseases, and preventing rheumatism. The tubs vary in size, some are small and designed for two people. The huge tubs, which resemble real baths, can accommodate a group of eight to twelve people.
The picturesque village of Lomchori, located high in the mountains, is one of the most beautiful places where you can “bathe” in these ponds. This tradition began here after a local resident found one of the old 17th-century basins left over from the Austrians, and began using it for its intended purpose. In this village there is a spring of healthy and sulfurous water, which has long been used in healing. These pools have become so popular with tourists that you may not be able to find an empty place and must book in advance. Today in the Carpathian village there are more than twenty bathtubs. As they differ in sizes. These iron basins were cast according to ancient techniques. Mineral water is heated in it over an open fire. At the bottom are laid flat stones on which you can sit and lie comfortably without fear of getting burned. There are pools on open terraces beside the mountain river with artificial slopes where guests can lie down and dry off. As the water temperature in the kettle rises, you can open the tap and add cold water. Dried herbs are added to the basins. The owners of the resorts spread there care about making the place as comfortable as possible for the guests. Guests are served a delicious lunch and are served Carpathian tea with honey. The ponds in this village are useful for people with problems with the joints and musculoskeletal system, because this sulfur water quickly eliminates inflammation. Conducting these sessions also strengthens the nervous and immune systems, improves skin condition, and improves overall health. After treatment in Lomchori, you can visit the popular wooden restaurant.
