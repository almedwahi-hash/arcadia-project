The “Study in Kazakhstan” service offers a personal manager to every potential student who wants to study in this wonderful country. From advice in choosing the right study program to accommodation and travel arrangements, we are here to help you every step of the way. In addition, we handle all technical matters such as searching for scholarships and applying for visas. We can even contact universities and colleges in Kazakhstan on your behalf.
