International airports forUkraine Ukrainian airports - overview If you look at the list of airports of Ukraine, you can find about 30 air terminal complexes. But only half of them can receive international flights and even fewer offer this service. Due to the high prices of domestic flights, most Ukrainians prefer trains. This is the main reason why there are about ten airports actually operating in Ukraine.
There are two airports in the Ukrainian capital Kyiv. These international airports are Boryspil and Juliana. The rest are spread throughout the country and are located in the largest cities of Ukraine. Unfortunately, not all of them offer a relatively high level of comfort - only Kiev airports and two others.
The largest international airports in Ukraine International Airport "Boryspil" International Airport "Boryspil" International Airport "Boryspil" is the largest airport in Ukraine - more than 60% of Ukraine's air passenger traffic takes place through it. Located about 30 km southeast of central Kiev, it annually serves more than 8 million passengers.
Boryspil is the only airport in Ukraine from which intercontinental flights are operated. You can get to and from Kiev Airport by buses, taxis and buses, as a railway line has currently been established. Igor Sikorsky International Airport "Kyiv" (Zhuliany) The oldest airport in Kyiv, it is located 7 km southwest of the city center. This is the second largest airport in Ukraine in terms of the number of flights and passenger traffic. In 2017, its passenger number reached 1.85 million people (1.76 million - international flights). On the territory of the airport there is the largest aviation museum in Ukraine with a lot of different civil and military aircraft. Kiev Airport can be reached by taxi or public transport. airport Odessa International The airport is located 7 km southwest of the center of Odessa. Odessa International Airport is one of the largest airports in Ukraine and connects the city with Antalya, Vienna, Warsaw, Vilnius, Dubai, Krakow, Kiev, Minsk, Prague, Riga, Sofia, Istanbul, Tallinn, Tbilisi, Tel Aviv. In 2017, passenger numbers were approximately 1.2 million people. The new terminal opened in 2017.

 Danylo Haltsky International Airport "Lviv" The largest airport in western Ukraine, located 6 km southwest of central Lviv. The airport was completely rebuilt during Lviv's preparation for the European football championship "Euro 2012". In 2017, its passenger number exceeded one million people.
The airport offers flights to more than twenty international destinations including London, Vienna, Munich, Berlin, Rome, Madrid, Barcelona, ​​Warsaw and Istanbul.

Kharkiv International Airport Located 12 km south of the center of Kharkiv, it is the largest airport in eastern Ukraine. There are regular flights to Vienna, Milan, Batumi, Dortmund, Katowice, Minsk, Warsaw, Tel Aviv, Istanbul, Kiev. In 2017, the number of passengers reached about 800 thousand people.
