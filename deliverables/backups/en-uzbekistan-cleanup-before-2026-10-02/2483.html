Why 2026 is the year to visit Uzbekistan

  • Visa-free entry for citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman (30 days), and a quick e-visa for most other nationalities.
  • Direct flights from Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Doha and Sharjah to Tashkent in about four hours, with new routes added every season.
  • High-speed Afrosiyob trains now link Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara in two hours or less, with Khiva reachable by a new fast line.
  • Restored cities and new hotels: boutique heritage hotels inside the old cities of Samarkand and Bukhara, and the Silk Road Samarkand resort complex with its eternal city and congress hall.
  • Prices remain among the lowest in the region: a week for a family of four costs roughly 2,200–3,400 USD excluding flights.

What to see in one week

Tashkent (one night: the Hazrati Imam complex, Chorsu Bazaar and the metro), Samarkand (two nights: Registan, Shah-i-Zinda, Bibi-Khanym and the shrine of Imam al-Bukhari), Bukhara (two nights: Poi Kalon, the Ark, the trading domes and Lyabi-Hauz), and either Khiva or a desert yurt night. Best time and practical notes

April to early June and September to early November are ideal (20–30°C). Summer exceeds 40°C in Bukhara and Khiva; winter is cold, cheap and empty. All food is halal, cards work in hotels and larger restaurants, and bazaars are cash only.
