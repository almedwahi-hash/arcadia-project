Frequently asked questions

How large a conference can Moscow host?

Hotel halls seat up to around 1,500, and congress centres more. We recommend holding sessions in the same hotel as the accommodation to simplify logistics.

Can a gala dinner be held in a palace?

Yes. Saint Petersburg in particular has palaces and historic restaurants available for private events with entertainment and photography.

Is winter suitable for corporate groups?

Yes. Temperatures are low, but hotels, venues and museums are heated, and the snowy scenery is exactly what many employees from hot countries want.
