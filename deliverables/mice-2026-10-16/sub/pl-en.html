Why Poland for a corporate trip?

  • A meeting point: two hours by air from most of Europe, five to six hours from the Gulf with direct flights to Warsaw.
  • One visa for a distributed team: a Schengen visa for those who need one, free entry for EU passport holders.
  • European conference infrastructure at lower cost: modern congress centres and international hotel chains priced below Western Europe.
  • Variety close by: old towns, historic salt mines and the Tatra mountains within a day trip.

Sample programme: Kraków and Zakopane, 4 nights, conference plus team building

  1. Day 1: arrival in Kraków (direct or via Warsaw), old-town hotel, welcome dinner.
  2. Day 2: conference day in the hotel hall or at ICE Kraków; evening walk around the Main Square and Wawel.
  3. Day 3: morning workshops, then the Wieliczka Salt Mine and a group dinner in the underground chamber.
  4. Day 4: transfer to Zakopane, mountain team-building activities, closing dinner in a highland lodge with live folk music.
  5. Day 5: free morning in Zakopane, transfer to Kraków airport.

Season and logistics

  • Best time: May to September for the cities, December to February for snowy Zakopane and the Christmas markets of Warsaw and Kraków.
  • Flights: direct to Warsaw from Dubai and Doha, and a dense network from all of Europe to Warsaw and Kraków.
  • Transport: fast train between Warsaw and Kraków in about two and a half hours, air-conditioned coaches for the group, executive cars.
  • Catering: halal, Turkish and Arabic restaurants in Warsaw and Kraków, with custom event menus.

Frequently asked questions

Is one Schengen visa enough for the whole team?

For those who need one, yes. We provide bookings and invitation letters that support the application; visa issuance is not part of our services.

Warsaw or Kraków for a conference?

Warsaw is bigger, easier to reach and has more business hotels; Kraków is more photogenic for incentives and has the ICE centre for large conferences.

Does Zakopane suit large groups?

It is best for teams up to 80 in one resort; for larger groups we make it a day trip from Kraków.
