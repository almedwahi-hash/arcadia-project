Why Kazakhstan for a corporate trip?

  • Variety in a single day: a morning meeting in a city hotel, lunch at 2,200 metres on Shymbulak in the afternoon.
  • Group-ready hotels: international chains in Almaty and Astana with equipped meeting rooms and events teams used to conferences.
  • Lower cost than Europe or the Gulf for the same service level, especially for rooms, catering and transport.
  • Two strong seasons: summer (May to September) for nature and lakes, winter (December to March) for skiing and snow activities.

Sample programme: Almaty, 4 nights, incentive plus one meeting day

  1. Day 1: airport meet-and-greet with company signage, check-in at a five-star hotel, welcome dinner.
  2. Day 2: working sessions in the hotel's conference room (screens, sound, interpretation if needed, two coffee breaks, working lunch); free evening downtown.
  3. Day 3: Shymbulak and Medeu cable car, mountain lunch, team-building activity (team challenges or a cooking workshop).
  4. Day 4: Big Almaty Lake or Charyn Canyon, then a closing gala dinner with awards and professional photography.
  5. Day 5: free time and shopping, airport transfer.

For larger groups or formal conferences we move the sessions to Astana and keep Almaty for the leisure days.

Season and logistics

  • Best time: May to September for nature and mountains, December to March for skiing. October and April are quieter and cheaper.
  • Flights: direct to Almaty and Astana from Dubai, Doha, Riyadh and Jeddah, and from most European and Asian hubs.
  • Transport: modern coaches and executive cars, with English- and Arabic-speaking escorts.
  • Catering: wide choice of international, Turkish and halal restaurants in Almaty; custom group menus on request.

Frequently asked questions

How large are hotel conference halls in Almaty?

International hotels seat from 20 to around 500 in theatre layout; for bigger conferences we use exhibition centres.

Does winter work for an incentive trip?

Yes, and it is a favourite for companies from hot climates: skiing and snow activities on Shymbulak with instructors and rental gear, with alternatives for non-skiers.

Can we combine Almaty and Astana?

Yes, with a two-hour domestic flight; it suits a five- to six-night programme with a conference in the capital and leisure in the mountains.
