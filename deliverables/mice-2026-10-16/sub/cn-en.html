Sample programme: Guangzhou and Shanghai, 6 nights, business mission plus incentive

  1. Day 1: arrival in Guangzhou, hotel next to the fair complex, briefing with the team's interpreters.
  2. Days 2 and 3: the Canton Fair or a specialised show with a pre-planned booth schedule; supplier meetings at the hotel in the afternoon.
  3. Day 4: two selected factory visits in Foshan or Dongguan with car, driver and interpreter; dinner on the Pearl River.
  4. Day 5: high-speed train or flight to Shanghai, partner meetings at a Pudong hotel, evening on the Bund.
  5. Day 6: incentive day: Shanghai Tower, a water town, closing dinner with team awards.
  6. Day 7: departure from Shanghai.

Season and logistics

  • Best time: March to May and September to November, matching the Canton Fair sessions in April and October. Avoid Chinese New Year and the October National Day holiday.
  • Book early: Guangzhou hotels fill up and prices rise during the Canton Fair; we book eight to twelve weeks ahead.
  • Connectivity and payments: we help the team with data SIMs, payment apps and a working VPN set up before departure.
  • Catering: halal restaurants (Xinjiang, Hui and Arabic cuisine) in all four cities, with custom group menus.
  • Note: we provide the bookings and invitation letters needed for the visa application; issuance is not part of our services.

Frequently asked questions

Does an interpreter accompany the team at the fair and factories?

Yes, one business interpreter per small group (four to six people) who knows the sector's vocabulary, and we prepare the booth and supplier list in advance.

Can a business mission be combined with an incentive?

This is the most requested format: working days in Guangzhou or Yiwu, then two days in Shanghai or Beijing as a reward for the team.

How are factory visits arranged?

We select factories by your product category, coordinate appointments and provide the car and interpreter; see our guide to negotiating with Chinese suppliers.
