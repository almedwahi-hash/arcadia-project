Why Uzbekistan for a corporate trip?

  • A different experience at a reasonable price: five-star hotels and equipped halls at clearly lower cost than Europe or the Gulf.
  • Conference plus culture in one programme: the Afrosiyob high-speed train reaches Samarkand from Tashkent in about two hours, so no day is lost in transit.
  • Exceptional gala venues: heritage restaurants and courtyards in Samarkand and Bukhara seat hundreds of guests.
  • Easy access: three to four hours from the Gulf, direct flights from Dubai, Doha, Riyadh, Jeddah and Istanbul, and growing links with Europe.

Sample programme: Tashkent and Samarkand, 4 nights, annual conference

  1. Day 1: arrival in Tashkent, meet-and-greet, registration, welcome dinner at a national restaurant.
  2. Day 2: first conference day in the hotel hall (simultaneous interpretation, registration desk, breaks, working lunch); short city tour in the evening.
  3. Day 3: morning sessions and workshops, afternoon high-speed train to Samarkand, hotel check-in, gala dinner beside the Registan.
  4. Day 4: Samarkand tour with an English- or Arabic-speaking guide, free time in the bazaars; second night in Samarkand or evening train back to Tashkent.
  5. Day 5: departure from Samarkand or Tashkent depending on flights.

Season and logistics

  • Best time: March to May and September to November (mild weather). Summer is hot but fine for indoor programmes; winter is quiet and cheaper.
  • Train: Afrosiyob between Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara; we pre-book a full carriage or grouped seats.
  • Transport: air-conditioned coaches and executive cars, with a coordinator in each city.
  • Catering: Uzbek cuisine is halal by default; custom, vegetarian and international menus on request.

Frequently asked questions

Are Tashkent venues of international standard?

Yes. Hilton, Hyatt and InterContinental have ballrooms and conference halls with modern AV and events teams experienced in international conferences.

Can a gala dinner be held inside a historic site?

Yes, at selected venues in Samarkand and Bukhara with prior permits; we arrange décor, entertainment and photography.

How much lead time is needed?

Four to six weeks for groups up to 50, and eight to twelve weeks for large conferences and the spring and autumn peaks.
