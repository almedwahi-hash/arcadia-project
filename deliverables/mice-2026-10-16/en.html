How we work with companies

  1. Brief (day one): you send the purpose, approximate headcount, tentative dates, a destination (or leave it to us) and, if available, a budget per person.
  2. Proposal (within 48 hours): one or two hotel options, the meeting venue and its specification, day-by-day programme, transport, meals and a total price with payment and cancellation terms.
  3. Confirmation and preparation: contract and confirmed bookings under participant names, a venue checklist (screens, sound, interpretation, registration) and a detailed run sheet approved by the company.
  4. Delivery: an Arcadia coordinator on site from arrival to departure: meet-and-greet, registration, venue operation, excursions, gala dinner and transfers, with a direct line to the organiser.
  5. Report: a post-event summary of attendance, expenses and official photos for your management.

Sample corporate programmes

Almaty, 3 days: incentive trip for a sales team

Day 1: arrival, 5-star hotel, welcome dinner. Day 2: Shymbulak and Medeu cable car, lunch in the mountains, team activity, free evening in the city. Day 3: Big Almaty Lake or Charyn Canyon, closing gala dinner with awards. Suits 20 to 150 participants; a meeting day can be added at the start.

Tashkent and Samarkand, 4 days: annual conference with a cultural day

Two days of sessions in a Tashkent conference hotel with simultaneous interpretation and coffee breaks, then the high-speed train to Samarkand for a cultural day and a gala dinner near the Registan, returning on day four. Suits 30 to 200 participants.

Guangzhou and Shanghai, 5 days: business mission for a procurement team

The Canton Fair or a specialised trade show, two days of factory and supplier meetings with a business interpreter and a car with driver, then Shanghai for closing meetings and a city tour. See also our Canton Fair guide and Yiwu market guide.

Moscow or Warsaw, 3 days: executive meeting and gala dinner

A boardroom in a central hotel, a short city tour after the sessions and a gala dinner in a historic restaurant. Suits boards and senior management teams of 10 to 40.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with event agencies or with companies directly?

Both. We act as the ground partner (DMC) for event agencies and conference organisers, and we work directly with HR and administration departments.

Is this service only for Gulf companies?

No. We serve companies from any country, whether European, Middle Eastern or Asian, with participants of any nationality. The programme is designed around the company's culture and its employees' preferences.

How much lead time do you need?

For groups up to 50, four to six weeks is enough. For large conferences or peak seasons (the Canton Fair, summer in Almaty, year-end holidays), three months is recommended to secure the venues and hotels you want.

Can meeting days be combined with a leisure programme?

This is the most requested format: sessions in the morning and activities in the afternoon, or two working days followed by two leisure days. We shape the programme around your objectives.

How is payment handled?

Contract and invoice in the company's name, a deposit to confirm bookings and the balance before arrival on an agreed schedule, with clear cancellation terms in the proposal.

Do you publish per-person prices?

No. The cost per person varies widely with the hotel, number of nights, venue and group size. Send your requirements and you receive an accurate quote within 48 hours.
