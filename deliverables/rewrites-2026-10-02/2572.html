Arcadia's hotel reservation service in Poland selects the right hotel for your family in Warsaw, Krakow, Zakopane, Gdansk and Wroclaw, confirms it at a fixed price, and provides the booking confirmation required for a Schengen visa file. Where to stay in each city

  • Warsaw: the centre near the central station and metro for convenience, or the Old Town for atmosphere.
  • Krakow: the Old Town if you want everything on foot, or Kazimierz for halal restaurants nearby.
  • Zakopane: hotels on Krupowki street, or mountain cottages for large families.
  • Gdansk: the Old Town on the waterfront, or the seaside resorts of Sopot.

What to check before confirming

  • Arrival and departure dates and the number of nights.
  • Room type and the real number of beds (European rooms are small; a family of four needs a family room or two rooms).
  • Cancellation and change policy, and check-in time.
  • Breakfast: included or not, and its halal options.
  • Parking if you have a car, and distance from the station.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book only the hotel without a program?
Yes; hotels alone are available, and cheaper as part of a program with a car and guide.

Is the confirmation enough for a Schengen visa?
Yes, together with the other documents (flight tickets, insurance, bank statement).

When should I book?
At least two months ahead for summer and the Christmas markets, one month for the rest of the year.

How do I pay?
At a fixed price including taxes, by transfer or card link.
