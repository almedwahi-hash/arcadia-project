Uzbekistan is a Muslim-majority country in the heart of Central Asia, once the centre of the Silk Road and the home of the great scholars of hadith and fiqh. Since 2018 it has become one of the fastest-growing destinations for travellers from the Gulf: visa-free entry, direct flights from the Gulf, high-speed trains, new hotels and some of the lowest prices in the region. This page answers the basic questions before a trip: where it is, its language, currency and climate, how to get there and around, what it costs, and what makes it special.

Why Gulf travellers choose Uzbekistan

  • Living Islamic history: the shrines of Imam al-Bukhari, Imam al-Tirmidhi, Imam al-Maturidi and Baha-ud-Din Naqshband, and the Uthman Quran in Tashkent.
  • Silk Road cities: Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva, all UNESCO World Heritage Sites. See the best things to do in Uzbekistan.
  • Safety and calm: one of the safest countries in the region, with hospitable people used to Arab visitors.
  • Value: a week for a family of four with 4-star hotels, a private car and a guide costs roughly 2,200–3,400 USD excluding flights.
  • Easy transport: the Afrosiyob high-speed train links Tashkent with Samarkand in two hours and Bukhara in under four.

Climate and best time to visit

A dry continental climate: mild spring (April–May, 18–27°C) and golden autumn (September–October, 20–28°C) are the best seasons; summer is hot (June–August, 35–42°C in Bukhara and Khiva); winter is cold (December–February, 0–8°C), quiet and cheap.

Uzbek food

Plov (rice with meat and carrots) is the national dish and every city has its own version, alongside shashlik, samsa from the clay oven, manti, laghman, decorated Samarkand bread and green tea at every meal. The fruit (melons, grapes, pomegranates, apricots) is among the best in Asia. All food is halal.

Frequently asked questions

Is Uzbekistan safe?
Yes, one of the safest countries in the region; the tourist cities are calm and welcoming.

Do I need a visa?
Not if you hold a passport from one of the six Gulf states (30 days); most other nationalities get an e-visa online within a few days.

How long is the flight from Riyadh?
About four hours direct to Tashkent.

Do cards work?
Yes in hotels and larger restaurants; carry cash for bazaars and small shops.
