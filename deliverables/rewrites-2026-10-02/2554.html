Group trips to Kazakhstan are what we organise most in the summer season: extended families of 10 to 30 people, groups of friends, corporate and incentive trips, student delegations and women's groups. We handle the trip from airport to airport: meet and greet, modern coaches seating 15 to 50 passengers, hotels at contract rates, an Arabic- or English-speaking guide on every coach, daily programs designed for the group's size and ages, and a halal menu agreed in advance. This page explains what we provide for groups, the most requested programs, how the price per person is calculated, and what we need from you to start.

Who group trips suit

  • Extended families: grandparents, parents and children on one coach, adjoining hotel rooms and a program that suits seniors and children alike.
  • Friends and young travellers: more adventure: Kolsai, Kaindi and Charyn by 4x4, skiing, horse riding.
  • Companies: incentive and team-building trips with meeting rooms, group dinners and entertainment.
  • Students and schools: educational programs (museums, universities, nature) with chaperones.
  • Women's groups: a female guide and a program that respects privacy.

What group organisation includes

  • Meet and greet at Almaty or Astana airport with a sign in the group's name, and help with passports and luggage.
  • Modern air-conditioned coaches: 15-seat vans, 30-seat and 50-seat buses with professional drivers and mountain-road permits.
  • 3- to 5-star hotels at group contract rates, adjoining rooms and halal breakfast.
  • A guide on every coach throughout the program and a group coordinator on WhatsApp.
  • Cable car, museum and park tickets booked in advance to avoid queues.
  • Group lunches and dinners in halal restaurants with an agreed menu, or free choice.
  • Travel insurance, a doctor on call, and program adjustments during the trip according to the weather.

How the price per person is calculated

The larger the group, the lower the price per person, because the coach and guide costs are divided among more people. Estimates for a 5-day program with 4-star hotels including breakfast, coach, guide and tickets, excluding international flights:

Group sizeApproximate price per person
10–15 people500–650 USD
16–30 people430–530 USD
31–50 people370–480 USD

Children under six get a large discount or stay free in some hotels. Prices rise in July and August and fall in May and September. See the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

What we need to send a quote

  1. Approximate number of travellers and the ages of children and seniors.
  2. Travel dates or preferred month and number of nights.
  3. Departure city (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Doha, Kuwait).
  4. Hotel level and room types.
  5. Interests: nature, shopping, skiing, Astana, Uzbekistan.

We send a detailed day-by-day program with the price per person within 48 hours, and fix the price when the deposit is paid.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum group size?
Ten people for group rates; smaller parties are handled as private family programs with a car.

Is entry visa-free for the whole group?
Yes for citizens of the six Gulf states (30 days); other nationalities see travel procedures to Kazakhstan.

Can meals be customised?
Yes; all meals are halal, and we adapt menus for children, vegetarians and diets.

Do you organise group trips to Russia and Uzbekistan too?
Yes, with the same model.
