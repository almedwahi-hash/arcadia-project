Season

May to September for the cities (22 to 26°C and long days), December for the Christmas markets in Warsaw and Kraków, January and February for snow and skiing in Zakopane. Avoid November and March. See the best time to visit Poland.

A sample four-night programme: Kraków and Zakopane

  1. Day 1: arrival in Kraków (direct or via Warsaw), old-town hotel, welcome dinner.
  2. Day 2: session day in the hotel hall or at ICE Kraków; evening walk around the Main Square and Wawel Castle.
  3. Day 3: morning workshops, then the Wieliczka Salt Mine and a group dinner in the chamber carved from salt 125 metres underground.
  4. Day 4: transfer to Zakopane (two hours), team-building activities in the Tatra mountains, closing dinner in a highland lodge with live folk music and awards.
  5. Day 5: free morning on Krupówki street, then transfer to Kraków airport.

Visas and logistics

  • EU and UAE passport holders enter visa-free; other Gulf and Arab nationalities need a Schengen visa applied for six to eight weeks ahead. We provide bookings and invitation letters; issuance is not part of our services.
  • The fast train between Warsaw and Kraków takes two and a half hours; groups travel by air-conditioned coach and executive cars.
  • Halal, Turkish and Arabic restaurants are available in Warsaw and Kraków, and we arrange custom menus for events.
  • International cards work everywhere; the currency is the złoty.

What determines the cost

Group size, hotel category (four or five stars), season, number of hall and interpretation days, and activities (Wieliczka, Zakopane, the gala dinner). We send an itemised proposal within 48 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Warsaw or Kraków?

Warsaw is easier to reach with more business hotels; Kraków is more photogenic for incentives and has the ICE centre for large conferences. A five-night programme can combine both by train.

Does Zakopane suit large groups?

It is best for teams up to 80 in one resort; for larger groups we make it a day trip from Kraków.

How much lead time is needed?

Eight to ten weeks because of Schengen visas, and three months for the summer and holiday seasons.
