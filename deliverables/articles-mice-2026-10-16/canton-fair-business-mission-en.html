A business mission to the Canton Fair is the corporate trip with the highest return: a procurement team comes back with new suppliers, samples and comparable prices in a single week. But the fair is huge (more than 25,000 exhibitors across three phases), Guangzhou is crowded during it, and teams that go unprepared lose half their time. This guide explains how a company organises a successful mission for its procurement team: timing, registration, hotel, booth schedule, factory visits and what happens after the return.

Step 1: Choose the right phase

The Canton Fair runs twice a year (April to May and October to November), each session in three phases by sector: the first for electronics, machinery, lighting, vehicles and building materials; the second for consumer goods, gifts and home décor; the third for textiles, footwear, food and medical products. Choosing the wrong phase means a wasted week. See the Canton Fair guide and the best China trade fairs 2026.

Step 2: Registration and hotel at least eight weeks ahead

  • Each participant registers as a foreign buyer on the official website and receives a badge; we help with registration and print the badges before arrival.
  • Hotels in the Pazhou area (next to the fair complex) fill up and raise prices during the fair; early booking saves an hour a day on the road. Five-star options include Shangri-La Guangzhou and Langham Place, four-star options Holiday Inn and Novotel.
  • The fair's invitation letter and confirmed bookings support the visa application; visa issuance is not part of our services.

Step 3: The booth schedule before departure

Before the trip we prepare, for each small team of four to six people, a list of target exhibitors from the fair directory based on the company's products, with hall and booth numbers, arranged into a daily route that minimises walking. A business interpreter who knows the sector's vocabulary accompanies each team, and every supplier is recorded on a standard form (product, minimum order, price, lead time, certificates) so comparison after the return is easy.

Step 4: One or two days for factories

The best deals are closed after seeing the factory, not the booth. Together with the company we select two to four factories in Foshan, Dongguan and Shenzhen (one to two hours from Guangzhou), coordinate the appointments and provide the car with driver and the interpreter, with a short report after each visit. After the return: what we arrange

Sample shipping, supplier licence checks, final quotations, and introductions to reliable freight forwarders from China to the Gulf by sea and air with follow-up on the first shipment. See Arcadia's business and sourcing services in China and shipping from China to the Gulf.

Team checklist

  • Business cards in English and Chinese, and written product specifications and quantities.
  • An active WeChat account and an international card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay.
  • An eSIM or VPN ready before landing.
  • An extra bag for samples and catalogues, and comfortable shoes; daily walking at the fair is 10 kilometres or more.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for the fair itself?

Two to three for one phase if the booth schedule is prepared in advance; without a schedule a team needs twice as long.

Can we attend two phases?

Yes, with a stay of eight to ten days covering the break days between phases, which we use for factory visits.

Can incentive days be added for the team?

This is the most requested format: working days in Guangzhou, then two days in Shanghai or Beijing; see corporate trips and business missions in China.
