Moscow has the largest supply of conference venues and international hotels in the region: ballrooms for several hundred delegates, a standalone congress centre at the World Trade Center, five-hour direct flights from the Gulf with visa-free entry for Gulf citizens, and halal restaurants in every district. This guide explains how a company organises its conference or annual management meeting in Moscow: format and size, season, venues and hotels in both categories, a sample programme and what determines the cost.

Why Moscow for a conference?

  • Unmatched capacity: hotels with halls for 500 to 1,500, the World Trade Center for larger congresses, and meeting rooms in every international hotel.
  • Two hotel categories: five-star such as Lotte Hotel Moscow, Radisson Collection and Four Seasons, and four-star with excellent halls such as Novotel Moscow Centre, AZIMUT City Hotel Olympic and Holiday Inn; you choose by company culture and budget.
  • Venues that leave an impression: a gala dinner at a panoramic restaurant in Moscow City, a private river cruise or an evening at the Bolshoi.
  • Easy entry: Gulf citizens enter visa-free, and for other nationalities we provide bookings and invitation letters (issuance is not part of our services).

Step 1: Format and size

A management meeting for 20 runs in a central hotel's boardroom with a working lunch and a gala dinner. A conference for 100 to 300 needs a hotel ballroom with parallel sessions. Above 500 you need a congress centre with accommodation spread across two or three neighbouring hotels. Also define the interpretation languages (English, Arabic, Russian) and whether there is an accompanying exhibition or awards ceremony.

Step 2: Season

From late May to early September the weather is mild, days are long and river cruises run. September and October are quieter and cheaper. December is magical with winter markets and the Red Square ice rink, but avoid the first ten days of January and early May when Russians travel. See the Moscow guide.

Step 4: Venue requirements checklist

  • Seating layout and number of seats, plus breakout rooms for parallel sessions.
  • LED screens, sound system, wireless microphones, recording and streaming if needed.
  • Simultaneous interpretation with booths, headsets and two interpreters per language.
  • Registration desk and company signage, and breaks with menus that respect allergies.
  • Practical note: foreign Visa and Mastercard do not work in Russia; we pay all local expenses on the company's behalf under the contract.

What determines the cost

Number of participants, hotel category and room type, number of hall days, simultaneous interpretation, the gala dinner format and accompanying activities (river cruise, theatre, the Saint Petersburg extension). We send an itemised proposal within 48 hours instead of a generic price.

Frequently asked questions

How much lead time is needed?

Six to eight weeks for a conference of up to 100, and three months for large conferences and the summer and White Nights season.

Can Moscow and Saint Petersburg be combined?

Yes, by Sapsan train in four hours; the most requested format is a conference in Moscow and two incentive days in Saint Petersburg with a palace gala dinner.

Is Arabic simultaneous interpretation available?

Yes, professional English, Arabic and Russian interpreters with full equipment.
