Organising a corporate conference in Tashkent has become a smart choice for companies from the Gulf, Europe and Asia: international conference hotels with halls for several hundred delegates, clearly lower costs than Dubai, Istanbul or Western Europe, direct flights of three to four hours from the Gulf, food that is halal by default, and then two hours by high-speed train to Samarkand for a gala dinner the delegates never forget. This guide explains how to plan the conference step by step, which venues fit, and how to add the cultural day without losing a working day.

Why Tashkent for a conference?

  • Ready venues: Hilton Tashkent City, Hyatt Regency and InterContinental have ballrooms and meeting rooms with modern AV and events teams experienced in international conferences; for mid-range budgets Wyndham, Ramada and Mercure offer excellent smaller halls.
  • Reasonable cost: rooms, halls and catering are clearly cheaper than traditional conference destinations for the same service level.
  • Easy entry: Gulf citizens and many European nationalities enter visa-free, and most others get a quick e-visa.
  • The cultural dimension: Samarkand and Bukhara are two hours away by train; no other conference destination offers a gala dinner beside the Registan.

Step 1: Define the size and format

A conference for 60 people runs entirely inside one hotel: rooms, hall and meals in one place, the simplest and most economical format. A conference for 300 to 800 needs a large hotel ballroom or a standalone congress hall with accommodation spread across two or three neighbouring hotels. Also define the number of parallel sessions, whether simultaneous interpretation is needed (English, Arabic, Russian, Uzbek), and whether there is an accompanying exhibition or awards ceremony.

Step 2: Season and dates

The two best windows are March to May and September to November (20 to 30°C). Summer is hot but fine for indoor programmes with a hotel pool, and winter is quiet and cheaper. Avoid Nowruz week (21 March) if you want calm, or choose it deliberately if you want delegates to see the country's biggest celebration. Details in the best time to visit Uzbekistan.

Step 4: Venue requirements checklist

  • Seating layout (theatre, classroom, round tables) and number of seats.
  • LED screens or projectors, sound system, wireless microphones, video recording if needed.
  • Simultaneous interpretation: booths, headsets and two interpreters per language.
  • Registration desk and signage with the company and conference names.
  • Coffee breaks and lunches with menus that respect allergies, plus vegetarian options.
  • Dedicated internet for the hall and charging points.

Frequently asked questions

How much lead time is needed?

Six to eight weeks for a conference of up to 100, and three months for large conferences and the spring and autumn peaks, to secure the hall and hotels.

Can the gala dinner be held inside a historic site in Samarkand?

Yes, at selected sites with prior permits, including décor, entertainment and photography.

Is Arabic simultaneous interpretation available?

Yes, professional interpreters for English, Arabic, Russian and Uzbek with booths and headsets.
