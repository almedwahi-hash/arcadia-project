Team-building activities in Kazakhstan have what hotel meeting rooms lack: mountains at 3,000 metres, turquoise lakes, open steppe and a nomadic culture that gets a team cooking, riding and competing outdoors. This guide presents the ten best group activities for companies in Almaty, Burabay and Astana, and how to pick the right one for your team size and travel season.

The ten best team-building activities in Kazakhstan

  1. The Shymbulak mountain challenge: cable car to 3,200 metres, then an orienteering race between teams with maps and checkpoints, and a mountain lunch. Suits 20 to 150 people, summer and winter.
  2. Beginners' ski day: ski school with instructors and equipment, and a friendly race on the green slope at the end of the day. December to March.
  3. Beshbarmak workshop: each team cooks the national dish inside a Kazakh yurt under a chef's guidance, and the dishes are judged. The best activity for multinational teams.
  4. Nomad games: archery, a demonstration of kokpar, tug of war and horse riding on the Assy plateau or around Almaty.
  5. Boat race on Burabay lake: teams row between the lake's islands, then a forest walk and a barbecue on the shore. Ideal for small leadership retreats.
  6. Outdoor cooking at Big Almaty Lake: with a view of the turquoise lake and mountain air that stays cool even in July.
  7. 4x4 expedition to Charyn Canyon: a convoy between teams, photo missions in the Valley of Castles and lunch on the river bank. A full day for active teams.
  8. Almaty treasure hunt: a city route between the Green Bazaar, Panfilov Park and Kok-Tobe with puzzles and photos; no fitness needed, suits everyone.
  9. Talent show at the closing dinner: each team performs a short act, with awards and professional photography.
  10. Futuristic Astana day: a challenge to visit as many landmarks as possible (Baiterek, Khan Shatyr, the Nur Alem sphere) by public transport on a limited budget; for conferences in the capital.

Tips from the field

  • One main activity per day and free evenings; exhaustion kills enthusiasm.
  • Every physical activity has a comfortable alternative in the same place (the cable-car café, a quiet boat instead of the race).
  • Instructors and coordinators speak English and Arabic, and equipment and insurance are part of the arrangement.
  • Summer (May to September) for lakes and steppe, winter (December to March) for snow and skiing; see the best time to visit Kazakhstan.

Frequently asked questions

Is special training or fitness required?

No. Every activity has levels, and instructors adapt the difficulty to the team.

How many days does a team-building programme need?

One day within a three-night trip is the most common; leadership retreats in Burabay need two nights.

Can it be combined with business meetings?

Yes: morning sessions in the hotel hall and an afternoon activity, or a working day followed by a field day.
