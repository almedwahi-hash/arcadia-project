Dates of the main fairs we accompany you to

  • Canton Fair (Guangzhou): two sessions a year, spring (April to May) and autumn (October to November), each in three phases by sector: electronics and machinery, then consumer goods and gifts, then textiles and food. See the Canton Fair guide.
  • Yiwu Commodities Fair: every October, alongside the Yiwu International Trade Market open all year; see the Yiwu guide.
  • Specialised fairs in Shanghai and Shenzhen: electronics, auto parts, furniture, home appliances and building materials throughout the year; full dates in the best China trade fairs 2026.

Checklist before a business trip

  • Business cards in English and Chinese (we can print them for you in Guangzhou).
  • Written product specifications and target quantities, with photos of the samples you need.
  • An active WeChat account; most suppliers communicate through it after the fair.
  • An international card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, plus a small amount of yuan in cash.
  • An eSIM or VPN ready before landing so WhatsApp and email keep working.

Frequently asked questions

Is pre-registration required for the Canton Fair?

Yes. Foreign buyers register on the official website and receive a buyer badge; we help with registration and printing before arrival.

How many days are enough for a first business mission?

Five to seven: two or three days at the fair, one or two for factories and one for closing meetings. For companies and delegations we arrange the full programme in corporate trips and business missions.

Can a trader bring the family?

Yes. We arrange a leisure programme for the family on the fair days, then shared days in Shanghai or Beijing.
