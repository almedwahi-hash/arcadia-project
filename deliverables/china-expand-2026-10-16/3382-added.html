How to be sure a restaurant is halal

  • Look for the green sign with the characters 清真 (qingzhen), often with the Arabic word "halal" beside it.
  • Hui minority restaurants and Uyghur (Xinjiang) restaurants are halal by nature, as are Lanzhou lamian shops.
  • Use the 清真 filter in the Meituan app or the HalalTrip app, or ask your Arcadia guide.
  • In ordinary Chinese restaurants avoid pork dishes and sauces containing cooking wine, and order vegetables and fish when necessary.

Halal food in hotels, trains and planes

Four- and five-star hotels in the main cities serve breakfasts with pork-free options (eggs, bread, fruit, vegetarian noodles), and some hotels provide a halal corner on request for groups. On high-speed trains bring a meal from a halal restaurant before boarding, as train menus are limited. Gulf and Turkish airlines serve halal meals, and on Chinese domestic flights request a MOML meal when booking. Arcadia programmes guarantee a halal restaurant for every main meal; see travel programmes and travel tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is the meat in 清真 restaurants slaughtered according to Islamic rules?

Yes. These restaurants are supervised by local Islamic associations and the meat is slaughtered according to Sharia.

Are there Arabic restaurants?

Guangzhou, Yiwu, Shanghai and Beijing have Arabic and Turkish restaurants thanks to the trading communities; prices are higher than local Hui restaurants.

Is halal food available in the natural areas?

In Guilin, Chengdu and Hainan yes, and in remote places we arrange meals in advance within the programme.
