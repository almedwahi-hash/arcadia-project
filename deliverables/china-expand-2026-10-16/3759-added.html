What to watch when importing home appliances

Electrical appliances are the category most sensitive to specifications: 220 V and 50 Hz, a type G (British) plug for the Gulf, Arabic manuals and labels, and conformity certificates customs will ask for. Any mistake in these details means a held shipment or a product that does not work at the customer. We review the specifications with the factory before production and test a sample from every batch.

Where are home appliances made in China?

Shunde (Foshan) is the capital of large appliances: air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines and ovens · Zhongshan for small appliances and lighting · Ningbo and Cixi for small appliances and power tools · Shenzhen for smart electronics and screens. All are one to three hours from our Guangzhou office and can be visited during a business mission with your team in the Canton Fair electronics and appliances phase.

Planning the container

A 20-foot container holds about 28 cubic metres (for example 150 to 200 small split air conditioners or 300 to 400 vacuum cleaners), a 40-foot about 58 and a 40-foot high cube about 68. For smaller orders we consolidate a shared container from several factories at our warehouse. See shipping from China to the Gulf for a sea versus air comparison.
