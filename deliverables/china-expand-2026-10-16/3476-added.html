Return flights from the Gulf to Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou usually cost $500 to $900 outside peak seasons and rise in summer and holidays. For families see the cost of a China trip 2026 and the best hotels in Beijing and Shanghai for families.

What raises or lowers the cost

  • Season: Golden Week, Chinese New Year and the two Canton Fair sessions raise hotel prices by 50 to 100% in the cities concerned.
  • Number of cities: every extra city means a train or domestic flight and a transfer night; three cities in ten days is the best balance.
  • Group size: the private car and guide are fixed costs shared by the group, so a family of four or five pays clearly less per person than a couple.
  • Food: local halal restaurants (Xinjiang and Hui cuisine) are far cheaper than Arabic restaurants in hotels and excellent; see the halal food guide.

Frequently asked questions about costs

Is an Arcadia package cheaper than arranging it yourself?

For families and groups usually yes, because we have contracted hotel and train rates and save you the lost time and costly mistakes in a country where many global apps do not work.

How do I pay in China?

Link your international card to Alipay or WeChat Pay before travelling and carry a little yuan in cash; foreign cards are rarely accepted directly outside large hotels.

Does the package include the visa?

No. We provide the bookings and supporting documents for the visa application; issuance is through the embassy or an authorised centre. See the Chinese visa guide 2026.
