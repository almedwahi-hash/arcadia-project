How to succeed in importing clothing and accessories

Clothing and accessories are the easiest category to start with and the most competitive: good margins, but quality varies widely between factories and the season rules everything. Success comes down to three things: a physical sample before any quantity, a schedule three months ahead of the season, and a mix of items in one container instead of one item in bulk.

Timing by season

Order Ramadan and Eid goods three to four months ahead, winter clothing in July and August, summer in January and February, and avoid production deliveries during Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February) when factories close for three weeks. Usual production time is 20 to 35 days, then 25 to 35 days by sea.

Quality inspection for clothing

We check fabric composition and weight (grams per square metre), colour fastness after washing, sizes against the Gulf size chart, stitching, zips and buttons, and Arabic composition and care labels. We send a photo inspection report before loading. To visit the markets yourself see the Yiwu market guide and the Guangzhou guide.

More questions

Can several categories be mixed in one order?
Yes, and we recommend it: a shared container combining clothing, bags and accessories from several factories and markets.
Can my brand be printed on the clothing?
Yes: woven or printed labels, care labels and custom bags from 300 to 500 pieces per style.
How do we handle size differences?
We send your size chart to the factory, request a sample of each size before production and check sizes randomly before shipping.
