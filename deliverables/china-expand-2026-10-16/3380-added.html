Activities for families and children

China is one of Asia's best destinations for children: Shanghai Disneyland with the largest Disney castle in the world, Universal Beijing (Harry Potter, Transformers, Kung Fu Panda), Chimelong in Guangzhou with its safari park and ocean kingdom, and the Shanghai aquarium and natural history museum. Each theme park needs a full day and advance tickets through the official apps; see Beijing with kids and China in 10 days for families.

Seasonal activities not to miss

  • Winter: the Harbin Ice and Snow Festival (January and February) with illuminated ice sculptures the size of buildings, and skiing at resorts near Beijing.
  • Spring: cherry blossoms in Wuhan and Beijing's parks, and the tea harvest in Hangzhou.
  • Autumn: the colours of Jiuzhaigou, Huangshan above a sea of clouds, and the best weather for the Great Wall.
  • Summer: river cruises in Guilin and beaches in Qingdao; the cities are hot, so plan activities early and late.

Frequently asked questions

Do activities need advance booking?

Theme parks, the Forbidden City and the Terracotta Army do, often with passport numbers; we book them within the programme.

Can cultural activities be done in English or Arabic?

Yes. An English- or Arabic-speaking guide accompanies you at the tea ceremony, cooking workshops and tai chi lessons and translates the explanations.

How many activities per day?

One main activity plus one or two sights; distances in Chinese cities are large and exhaustion spoils the fun. See the best things to do in China 2026.
