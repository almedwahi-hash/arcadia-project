Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order value?
Usually from $500 through consolidated orders with other clients, and from $3,000 for independent part-container orders.
How do I pay the supplier?
We recommend a 30% deposit on order confirmation and 70% after the inspection report and before shipping, by bank transfer to the factory, never to individuals.
How long from start to delivery?
Four to eight weeks: one week for research and samples, two to four weeks for production, then 25 to 35 days by sea or 5 to 10 days by air.
Do you help with Gulf conformity certificates?
Yes. We confirm the factory can provide CE or ISO certificates and the conformity tests needed for clearance (such as SABER in Saudi Arabia and ESMA in the UAE) before shipping.
