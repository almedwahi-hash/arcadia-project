How we guarantee auto parts quality

The Chinese auto-parts market is vast: OEM factories producing for global brands, excellent aftermarket factories and poor imitation workshops. The difference shows neither in photos nor in price, but in the visit and the inspection. That is why we work in four fixed steps:

Where are auto parts made in China?

Guangzhou has the largest parts market in southern China (the Yongfu area) · Zhejiang (Ruian and Taizhou) is the capital of mechanical and electrical parts factories · Shenzhen for electronics, cameras and lighting · Changzhou for batteries and tyres. We pick the city by your product list, and traders can visit the factories with us during a business mission to the Canton Fair (the vehicles and machinery phase opens each session).

Shipping and clearance for auto parts

Heavy parts (engines, bumpers, glass) go by sea in a part or full container, and light high-value parts (electrics, lighting) by air. We attach the correct HS code for each item and the certificate of origin, and confirm Gulf conformity requirements for tyres, batteries and lamps before shipping so the consignment is not held at customs. More questions

Can we order parts for older models?
Yes. Chinese aftermarket factories cover Japanese, Korean, American and European models back to the 1990s, and we verify compatibility by the original part number.
Is there a warranty on the parts?
We work with factories that give a written warranty (usually 6 to 12 months), fix it in the contract and follow up replacements for any manufacturing defect.
Can I put my own brand on the parts?
Yes for most items (OEM/ODM) from medium quantities, with packaging and labels designed in Arabic and English.
