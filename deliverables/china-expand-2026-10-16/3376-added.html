How to choose the right program

  • First visit and limited time: the Classic program; Beijing and Shanghai sum up old and new China in one week.
  • Family with children: the Comprehensive program; Xi'an adds the Terracotta Army and cycling on the city wall, which children remember most, and a Shanghai Disneyland day can be added.
  • Nature and photography: the Complete program; Guilin and Chengdu look like nowhere else in China.
  • Traders and businesspeople: the Business program with two or three leisure days at the end; for companies and delegations see corporate trips and business missions in China.

Best time for each program

April, May, September and October suit every program: mild weather in Beijing and Shanghai and clear skies over the Great Wall. The Complete program is best from April to October, when the Li River cruise in Guilin is at its finest. The Business program follows the Canton Fair sessions (two phases, April to May and October to November), so Guangzhou hotels are booked at least two months ahead. Avoid Chinese New Year and the October Golden Week; details in the best time to visit China.

Frequently asked questions

Do the prices include international flights?

No. The prices cover accommodation, tours, domestic transport, the guide and entrance tickets; we can book international flights at the day's fare on request.

Can a ready-made program be modified?

Yes. Adding a day, changing the hotel category or swapping a city are all possible, and we re-quote within 24 hours.

How many people are needed to run a program?

Programs run from two people with a private car and guide, and the cost per person falls as the group grows.
