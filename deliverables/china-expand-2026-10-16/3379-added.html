Frequently asked questions about nature in China

Are the natural sites suitable for seniors and children?

Mostly yes: the Li River by boat, the Huangshan cable car, flat boardwalks in Jiuzhaigou and a level panda base; we choose the easy routes and provide a private car.

Is halal food available near the natural areas?

Guilin, Chengdu and Hainan have halal restaurants (Hui and Xinjiang cuisine), and in remote areas we arrange suitable meals in advance; see the halal food guide.

Which single site if time is short?

Guilin and Yangshuo: two nights are enough, access from Shanghai or Guangzhou is easy, and the scenery is China's most famous.
