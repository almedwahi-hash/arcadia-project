2. Purpose

Partner sells group tours; Arcadia provides ground handling only (no international flights unless agreed in writing). Scope: Kazakhstan / Almaty base programmes and extensions to RU, UZ, KG as quoted per booking.

3. Services (standard inclusions)

As per confirmed booking confirmation and attached B2B Rate Sheet (Almaty 5D/4N or other quoted programme):

  • Licensed Arabic-speaking guide (8h/day where stated)
  • 4-star downtown hotel (or equivalent) — double/twin on B2B net basis
  • Daily breakfast + halal lunches/dinners as per programme
  • Transport per pax tier (coaster / mid-bus / 49-seat as quoted)
  • Entrances listed in standard programme (Medeu, Shymbulak, Kok-Tobe, Oi-Qaraghay, Bear Valley, etc.)
  • 24/7 WhatsApp support with Partner group leader

Optional extras (Charyn Canyon, Kolsai Lakes, Kaindy Lake and other far destinations) are not included in base net rates; quoted separately before confirmation.

4. Pricing and payment

  • Net B2B rates per person as in Rate Sheet or written quote; valid for group size band confirmed at booking.
  • Minimum group: 15 paying pax unless otherwise agreed.
  • Deposit: 30% on confirmation.
  • Balance: due 14 days before arrival (unless otherwise agreed in writing).
  • Currency: USD (or KZT equivalent at Arcadia bank rate on invoice date).
  • Invoices: info@arcadia-tour.com. Late payment may delay services or cancel booking per cancellation policy.

5. Booking and changes

  • All bookings in writing (email) with pax count, dates, hotel level, programme version, and special requests.
  • Name list and passport copies: minimum 21 days before arrival (or as stated in booking confirmation).
  • Changes after confirmation subject to availability and extra cost.

6. Cancellation (indicative — confirm per booking)

  • 30+ days before arrival: deposit may be applied to future group within 6 months (case-by-case).
  • 15-29 days: up to 50% of total ground cost.
  • Less than 14 days: up to 100% of non-recoverable costs.

7. Liability and insurance

Partner is responsible for its clients' travel insurance. Arcadia liability limited to direct ground services provided. Force majeure applies under Kazakhstan law.

8. Term and exclusivity

Term: 12 months from signature; auto-renewal unless either party gives 30 days written notice.
Exclusivity: Non-exclusive. No obligation for minimum volume; good-faith cooperation on Arabic / GCC group departures.

9. Confidentiality and marketing

Net rates and commercial terms confidential. Partner may use Arcadia name/logo in sales materials with prior approval. Arcadia may list Partner as B2B reference with consent.

10. Governing law

Republic of Kazakhstan. Disputes: good-faith negotiation, then courts of Almaty.
