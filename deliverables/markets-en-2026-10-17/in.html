Kazakhstan has become one of the fastest-growing destinations for travellers from India, and the reason is simple: holders of an Indian passport enter visa-free for 14 days, nonstop flights from Delhi and Mumbai reach Almaty in about four hours, and the city sits at the foot of snow-capped mountains with a ski resort 30 minutes from the centre. Arcadia, a destination management company based in Almaty, runs private tours, family holidays, honeymoon trips and corporate offsites for Indian groups, with vegetarian and Jain meals arranged in advance and English-speaking guides throughout.

In short: no visa for Kazakhstan for stays up to 14 days (42 days in any 180-day period). Uzbekistan, China, Poland and Russia require a visa for Indians, so we build Indian programmes in Kazakhstan: Almaty, Astana and the national parks. Best months: March to May and September to November; December to February for snow.

Flights from India

  • Delhi to Almaty: Air Astana nonstop, about four hours; IndiGo has also operated the route (check current schedules).
  • Mumbai to Almaty: IndiGo nonstop (launched September 2025), about five hours.
  • To Astana: via Almaty (two hours) or one stop via Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Istanbul.
  • Within Kazakhstan: the mountain parks are two to four hours by road from Almaty with our private vehicles.

Food for Indian groups

Almaty has several Indian restaurants, and our partner hotels prepare vegetarian, Jain and no-onion-no-garlic meals with advance notice. Kazakh and Uzbek cuisine also offers vegetarian options: plov without meat, samsa with pumpkin, salads, breads and dairy. We confirm every meal on the itinerary before arrival.

Practical notes for Indian travellers

  • Stay limit: 14 days per visit; a 15-night trip would need a visa, so we plan Indian itineraries at up to 13 nights.
  • Time zone: Almaty is 30 minutes behind India.
  • Money: cards work in cities; carry US dollars to exchange. Prices are lower than Europe or Dubai; see what a Kazakhstan trip costs.
  • Language: Russian and Kazakh; English with our guides and in hotels. Hindi-speaking guides are available on request in Almaty.
  • Weather: dry continental climate; summers warm, winters cold. Pack layers.

Frequently asked questions

Do Indians need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No, for stays of up to 14 days per visit and 42 days in total within any 180-day period. Longer stays require a visa, which we do not process.

Is Kazakhstan good for a honeymoon?

Yes. Mountain hotels above Almaty, private cars, lake excursions and snow in winter make it a popular honeymoon destination from India.

Can you arrange vegetarian and Jain food?

Yes, with advance notice at every hotel and restaurant on the programme, plus Indian restaurants in Almaty.

What about Uzbekistan?

Indian citizens need an e-visa for Uzbekistan, which we do not process. Kazakhstan alone fills up to 13 nights comfortably.

Is there a Hindi-speaking guide?

On request in Almaty, subject to availability; our standard guides speak English and Russian.
