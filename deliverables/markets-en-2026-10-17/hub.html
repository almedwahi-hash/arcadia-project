What stays the same wherever you come from

  • Passport validity: most of our destinations require at least six months of validity beyond your entry date (China asks for three), plus one blank page.
  • Registration: hotels in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan register foreign guests automatically. If you stay privately, registration is your own responsibility; our team explains the steps when needed.
  • Onward ticket and funds: border officers may ask for a return ticket and hotel confirmation. We send both with every booking.
  • Russia e-visa: for passports that are not visa-free, Russia offers an online e-visa (16 days) to citizens of over 60 countries including Malaysia, Singapore, Japan, India, Vietnam, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. We do not apply on your behalf, but we provide the hotel confirmation the form requires.
  • Schengen (Poland): the ETIAS online authorisation for visa-free nationals is expected to start in late 2026 with a transition period; it is a short online form, not a visa.

Our five destinations at a glance

  • Kazakhstan: Almaty with the Tian Shan mountains next door, Astana and the steppe. Four to five hours from the Gulf, seven to eight from Kuala Lumpur or Seoul.
  • Uzbekistan: Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva, the heart of the Silk Road, usually combined with Tashkent in six to eight nights.
  • China: Guangzhou, Shanghai and Beijing for leisure and business, plus the Canton Fair and Yiwu market for trade missions.
  • Poland: Warsaw, Kraków and Zakopane, an affordable Schengen destination with easy connections across Europe.
  • Russia: Moscow and Saint Petersburg, with the Hermitage, Red Square and the imperial palaces.

Frequently asked questions

Do you arrange visas?

No. We are a tourism company and do not process visas. This page helps you choose destinations where your passport does not need one. Where a visa is required, we send the hotel confirmation and programme letter for your own application.

Can I combine two visa-free countries in one trip?

Yes. The most popular combinations are Kazakhstan with Uzbekistan (a two-hour flight or a comfortable train from Almaty to Tashkent) and Kazakhstan with China via Urumqi or direct flights to Guangzhou and Shanghai. Each entry counts separately against the visa-free allowance.

Is the 30-day limit per visit or per year?

Per visit in most cases. Kazakhstan additionally caps visa-free stays at 90 days in any 180-day period, and South Korean citizens have a 60-day cap in Kazakhstan. Longer stays require a visa or permit, which we do not handle.

Do children need anything extra?

Children travel on their own passports and the same visa-free rules apply. A child travelling with one parent should carry a consent letter from the other parent for Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Russia.
