Only Russia still asks Singaporeans for an e-visa. Flights from Singapore

  • To Almaty: no nonstop service at present; one stop via Kuala Lumpur (AirAsia X), Seoul, Istanbul, Dubai, Doha or Delhi; ten to twelve hours in total.
  • To Tashkent: one stop via Kuala Lumpur (AirAsia X nonstop from KL three times a week), Istanbul, Dubai or Seoul.
  • To China: many daily nonstop flights to Guangzhou, Shanghai and Beijing; Guangzhou is under four hours.
  • To Poland: one stop via Doha, Dubai, Istanbul or Frankfurt to Warsaw and Kraków.
  • Between destinations: Almaty to Tashkent under two hours; Almaty to Urumqi, Beijing or Guangzhou nonstop.

Why Central Asia from Singapore?

  • Contrast: mountains at 3,000 metres, canyons and alpine lakes an hour from the city, and snow in winter.
  • Value: four- and five-star hotels, private drivers and guides at a fraction of Japanese or European prices; see what a Kazakhstan trip costs.
  • Food for everyone: halal by default in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan; vegetarian options are easy to arrange with notice.
  • Short breaks work: a four-night Almaty break or a five-night Uzbekistan loop fits a long weekend plus two days of leave.

Practical notes for Singaporean travellers

  • Time zone: Almaty and Tashkent are three hours behind Singapore.
  • Money: cards accepted in cities; bring US dollars to exchange for markets and small towns.
  • Language: English with our guides and in hotels; Russian and the local language elsewhere.
  • Weather: dry and continental; pack layers, sunscreen and comfortable shoes. Winter in Almaty needs a proper coat.
  • Connectivity: local SIM cards are cheap and available at the airport; we help on arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Do Singaporeans need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No. Singapore citizens enter visa-free for up to 30 days per visit, within 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is Uzbekistan visa-free for Singaporeans?

Yes, for up to 30 days.

Is China visa-free for Singaporeans?

Yes. The mutual visa exemption allows stays of up to 30 days per visit for tourism, business, family visits and transit.

What about Russia?

Russia requires an e-visa for Singaporeans, applied for online and valid for a 16-day stay. Arcadia does not process visas but provides the hotel confirmation needed.

Is Kazakhstan safe for solo and family travellers?

Yes. Almaty and Astana are calm, modern cities. Our tours use private vehicles and licensed guides throughout.
