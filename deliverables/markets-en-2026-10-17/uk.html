Practical notes for British travellers

  • Time zone: Almaty and Tashkent are four to five hours ahead of the UK depending on the season.
  • Money: cards work in cities and hotels; bring US dollars or euros to exchange for markets and villages. ATMs are common in Almaty, Astana and Tashkent.
  • Language: Russian, Kazakh and Uzbek; English in hotels and with our guides.
  • Health and safety: no mandatory vaccinations; tap water is best avoided outside hotels; cities are calm and well policed. Take out travel insurance with medical cover.
  • Driving: all our programmes use private vehicles with professional drivers; distances in Kazakhstan are long and roads vary.
  • Dress: casual is fine; cover shoulders and knees at mosques and mausoleums in Uzbekistan.

Frequently asked questions

Do British citizens need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No. UK passport holders enter visa-free for up to 30 days per visit, within 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is Uzbekistan visa-free for UK citizens?

Yes, for stays of up to 30 days.

Can UK citizens visit China without a visa?

Only in transit: up to 240 hours when travelling on to a third country, which works well for a stopover in Shanghai, Beijing or Guangzhou between Central Asia and the UK. A dedicated China holiday still needs a visa.

What about Russia?

Russia requires a consular visa for British passports and the UK is not on the e-visa list. Arcadia does not process visas; check the latest FCDO advice before planning.

Is Kazakhstan expensive?

No. Four-star hotels, private drivers and guided excursions cost well below Western European prices; see our guide to the cost of a Kazakhstan trip.
