A Thai passport enters three of Arcadia's destinations without a visa: 30 days in Kazakhstan, 30 days in China under the permanent mutual exemption in force since March 2024, and 30 days in Russia. Kazakhstan is the natural first choice: Air Astana flies nonstop from both Bangkok and Phuket to Almaty, Thai AirAsia X adds more Bangkok departures, and the flight takes about seven hours. For travellers from a tropical climate, Almaty offers what Thailand cannot: snow from December to March, ski slopes 30 minutes from the city, alpine lakes and canyons in summer. Arcadia, a destination management company based in Almaty, plans and runs private tours, group holidays and corporate trips with English-speaking guides.

In short: no visa for Kazakhstan, China or Russia. Uzbekistan and Poland require a visa, so Thai programmes are built around Kazakhstan, with China or Russia as a second country. Best months for snow: December to March; for lakes and mountains: May to October.

Flights from Thailand

  • Bangkok to Almaty: Air Astana nonstop about six times a week and Thai AirAsia X three times a week; around seven hours.
  • Phuket to Almaty: Air Astana nonstop daily in season.
  • Bangkok to Astana: Air Astana plans a twice-weekly nonstop from December 2026; otherwise via Almaty (two hours).
  • China and Russia: many daily flights from Bangkok to Guangzhou, Shanghai and Beijing; nonstop Bangkok and Phuket to Moscow with Aeroflot and others.

Suggested itineraries

  • Snow week in Almaty, 5 nights: skiing and sledging on Shymbulak with instructors and rental gear, the Medeu ice rink, hot springs and a snowy drive to the Kolsai Lakes; see Shymbulak and Medeu.
  • Almaty mountains and lakes, 4 nights (summer): Big Almaty Lake, Charyn Canyon, Kolsai and Kaindy lakes; based on Almaty in 3 days.
  • Almaty and Astana, 6 nights: the mountain city plus the futuristic capital and Burabay; see Astana in 2 days.
  • Kazakhstan and China, 8 to 9 nights: Almaty, then nonstop to Urumqi or Guangzhou and Shanghai in 3 days.
  • Kazakhstan and Russia, 9 nights: Almaty followed by Moscow in 3 days and Saint Petersburg.

Practical notes for Thai travellers

  • Time zone: Almaty is two hours behind Bangkok.
  • Winter clothing: temperatures in Almaty drop to -10°C in January; we advise on gear and arrange rentals for ski days.
  • Money: cards work in cities; carry US dollars to exchange.
  • Food: grilled meat, plov, noodles and dumplings; halal by default; Thai, Korean and Chinese restaurants in Almaty.
  • Language: Russian and Kazakh; English with our guides and in hotels.

Frequently asked questions

Do Thai citizens need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No. Thai passport holders enter visa-free for up to 30 days per visit, within 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is China visa-free for Thais?

Yes. The permanent mutual exemption allows up to 30 days per visit and 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is Russia visa-free for Thai citizens?

Yes, for tourism stays of up to 30 days.

Can I ski in Almaty without experience?

Yes. Shymbulak has beginner slopes, English-speaking instructors and full rental equipment; non-skiers enjoy the cable car, sledging and the Medeu ice rink.

What about Uzbekistan?

Thai citizens need an e-visa for Uzbekistan, which we do not process; most Thai groups combine Kazakhstan with China or Russia instead.
