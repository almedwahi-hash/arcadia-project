Halal food and prayer

Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are Muslim-majority countries. Restaurants serve halal beshbarmak, plov, shashlik and samsa as standard, supermarkets label halal meat, and every city has central mosques with Friday prayer. In Almaty we also book Malaysian and Indonesian-style restaurants for groups who miss home cooking. In China we route groups through the Muslim quarters of Guangzhou, Xi'an and Beijing and book halal-certified restaurants in advance; see our halal food in China guide.

Practical notes for Malaysian travellers

  • Currency: Kazakhstani tenge and Uzbek som; cards work in cities, and we recommend carrying some US dollars to exchange.
  • Language: Kazakh, Uzbek and Russian; English is common in hotels and with our guides, limited elsewhere.
  • Time zone: Almaty and Tashkent are three hours behind Kuala Lumpur, so jet lag is minimal.
  • Weather: dry continental climate; summers are warm (30°C) and winters cold (down to -10°C in Almaty), so pack layers rather than tropical clothing.
  • Insurance: travel insurance with medical cover is strongly recommended and required for Schengen.

Frequently asked questions

Can Malaysians enter Kazakhstan without a visa?

Yes. Malaysian citizens with an ordinary passport can stay up to 30 days per visit for tourism or business, within a limit of 90 days in any 180-day period.

Do Malaysians need a visa for Uzbekistan?

No. Malaysia is on Uzbekistan's visa-free list for stays of up to 30 days.

Is China visa-free for Malaysians in 2026?

Yes. Under the mutual visa exemption agreement in force since July 2025, Malaysians may stay up to 30 days per visit for tourism, business, family visits or transit.

Is Russia visa-free for Malaysians?

Not yet. Malaysians apply online for a Russian e-visa valid for a 16-day stay. Arcadia does not process visas but provides the hotel confirmation your application needs.

Is it easy to find halal food in Kazakhstan?

Very easy. Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are Muslim-majority countries, and mainstream restaurants serve halal food by default.
