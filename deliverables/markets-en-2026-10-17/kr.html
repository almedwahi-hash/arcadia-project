Holders of a South Korean passport can visit all five of Arcadia's destinations without a visa: 30 days in Kazakhstan, 30 days in Uzbekistan, 30 days in China under the unilateral exemption extended to the end of 2026, 90 days in Poland and 60 days in Russia. Nonstop flights link Seoul with Almaty, Astana, Shymkent and Tashkent in around seven hours, and Central Asia has a special connection with Korea: the Koryo-saram communities of Almaty and Tashkent, each more than 100,000 strong, mean Korean restaurants, Korean churches and Korean-speaking locals are part of city life. Arcadia, a destination management company in Almaty, runs private tours, family holidays and corporate trips with English-speaking guides, and can arrange Korean-speaking guides on request.

In short: no visa for any of the five destinations. Flight time from Seoul to Almaty or Tashkent is about seven hours and the time difference is three hours. Best months: April to June and September to October; December to March for skiing on Shymbulak.

Flights from Korea

  • Seoul to Almaty, Astana and Shymkent: nonstop flights several times a week, rising to around 20 weekly flights between Korea and Kazakhstan in summer; about seven hours.
  • Seoul to Tashkent: nonstop flights by Uzbekistan Airways and Korean carriers, about seven and a half hours.

  • China: dozens of daily flights from Incheon, Gimpo and Busan to Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou.
  • Russia: no nonstop service at present; connections via Almaty, Tashkent or Istanbul to Moscow and Saint Petersburg.

Korean heritage in Central Asia

Almaty and Tashkent are home to the largest Koryo-saram communities in the world. Visitors from Korea can see the Korean Theatre in Almaty, Korean markets with kimchi and morkovcha, and the memorials to the 1937 deportation. Many of our guests combine a heritage visit with the mountains: the Shymbulak ski resort and Medeu ice rink are 30 minutes from the city centre, and Charyn Canyon and the Kolsai Lakes make a two-day excursion.

Practical notes for Korean travellers

  • Payment: Visa and Mastercard work in cities; Korean app payments do not. Carry some US dollars to exchange.

  • Weather: continental climate with hot summers and cold winters; spring and autumn are the most comfortable.
  • Registration: hotels register foreign guests automatically in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Russia.

Frequently asked questions

Do South Koreans need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No. Korean citizens enter visa-free for up to 30 days per visit and 60 days in total within any 180-day period.

Is Uzbekistan visa-free for Koreans?

Yes, for stays of up to 30 days.

Can Koreans visit China without a visa in 2026?

Yes. China's unilateral visa-free policy for Korean citizens covers stays of up to 30 days and is currently valid until 31 December 2026.

Is Russia visa-free for Korean passport holders?

Yes. The mutual agreement allows up to 60 days per visit and 90 days within any 180-day period.

Can you provide a Korean-speaking guide?

Yes, on request in Almaty and Tashkent, subject to availability. Our standard guides speak English and Russian.
