Holders of a Japanese passport can visit most of Arcadia's destinations without a visa: 30 days in Kazakhstan, 30 days in Uzbekistan, 30 days in China under the unilateral exemption restored in November 2024 and extended to the end of 2026, and 90 days in Poland and the Schengen area. Only Russia asks Japanese citizens for an e-visa. Central Asia has long attracted travellers from Japan drawn to the Silk Road: the Registan in Samarkand, the mud-brick lanes of Bukhara and Khiva, and the Tian Shan mountains above Almaty. Arcadia, a destination management company based in Almaty, plans and runs private tours, small groups and corporate trips with licensed English-speaking guides, and can arrange Japanese-speaking guides in Uzbekistan on request.

In short: no visa for Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, China or Poland. Nonstop Tokyo to Tashkent flights with Uzbekistan Airways; Almaty via Seoul, Beijing or Istanbul. Best months: April to June and September to October; December to March for skiing above Almaty.

Flights from Japan

  • Tokyo to Tashkent: Uzbekistan Airways nonstop from Narita, about nine hours; also via Seoul or Istanbul.
  • Tokyo or Osaka to Almaty and Astana: one stop via Seoul (nonstop Korea to Kazakhstan flights), Beijing, Istanbul or Dubai; eleven to thirteen hours in total.
  • To China: many daily nonstop flights from Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya and Fukuoka to Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou.
  • To Poland: LOT Polish Airlines nonstop from Tokyo Narita to Warsaw.
  • Between destinations: Almaty to Tashkent under two hours; Tashkent to Samarkand two hours by high-speed train.

Practical notes for Japanese travellers

  • Time zone: Almaty and Tashkent are four hours behind Japan.
  • Money: cards work in cities; carry US dollars to exchange. Cash is useful at bazaars.
  • Language: Russian, Kazakh and Uzbek; English with our guides and in hotels; Japanese-speaking guides available in Samarkand and Bukhara on request.
  • Food: plov, grilled meat, noodles, dumplings and fresh bread; mild spices. Vegetarian meals on request.
  • Weather: dry continental climate; hot summers, cold winters. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons.

Frequently asked questions

Do Japanese citizens need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No. Japanese passport holders enter visa-free for up to 30 days per visit, within 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is Uzbekistan visa-free for Japanese?

Yes, for stays of up to 30 days.

Can Japanese visit China without a visa in 2026?

Yes. China's unilateral visa-free policy for Japanese citizens allows stays of up to 30 days and is currently valid until 31 December 2026.

What about Russia?

Russia requires an e-visa for Japanese citizens, applied for online and valid for a 16-day stay. Arcadia does not process visas.

Is Uzbekistan suitable for older travellers?

Yes. High-speed trains, short transfers and comfortable hotels inside the old towns make it a relaxed trip; we adapt the walking pace to the group.
