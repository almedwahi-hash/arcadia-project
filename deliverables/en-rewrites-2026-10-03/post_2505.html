Tourism in Zakopane is not just a list of cable cars; a successful trip depends on the season, the weather, the location of your accommodation and how far your group can walk. The town is the base for the Tatra Mountains, and it can be made a quiet family stop after Krakow rather than squeezed into a long there-and-back journey.

Zakopane at the foot of the Tatra Mountains in southern Poland

How many nights does Zakopane need?

Two nights give you a mountain day and a town day, and three nights are better for a family because they provide an alternative if the weather changes. A day trip from Krakow is possible, but it is affected by traffic on the road and reduces flexibility.

Gubałówka for families

You reach it by funicular or on foot, and the hill offers views of the Tatras when the sky is clear. The official Zakopane website notes that the suggested summit route is relatively short, but operation, visibility and activities change with the season. See the official Gubałówka page.

Kasprowy Wierch and the cable car

Booking early can be important in the busy seasons, and operation may be suspended because of wind or maintenance. The mountain is colder than the town centre, so do not rely on the Zakopane weather alone. Check with the operator on the day of your visit, and do not buy a ticket from an unreliable source.

Morskie Oko and the Tatra trails

The route to the lake is long even though it is not a technical trail, and it may not suit every child or older person. Entry, parking, transport and the rules change; check with the Tatra National Park before setting off, carry water and rain clothing, and do not start late.

Krupówki Street and its surroundings

The street is the hub for restaurants, shops and evening life, but it is not the best location for every family because of the crowds and noise. You can visit it without staying on it, leaving time to try the local products after checking that the ingredients suit your needs.

Zakopane in summer and winter

  • Summer: walking, views and outdoor activities, with sudden rain and crowds.
  • Autumn: beautiful colours, with shorter days and more changeable weather.
  • Winter: snow and seasonal activities, but driving and walking require preparation.
  • Spring: some trails may remain snowy or muddy, so do not assume they are ready.

Choosing mountain accommodation

Check the gradient of the road, the actual distance to transport, the parking, the heating and the lift. A beautiful view can mean a remote location or a difficult road in snow. For a family, send the number of bags and the bed type you need, and do not rely on photos alone.

Safety in the mountains

The Tatra trail network is extensive and protected by the park's rules. Check the trail conditions, the warnings and the weather, and do not head out onto a mountain trail in city shoes. The European emergency number is 112, but prevention starts with choosing a trail that suits the weakest member of the group. Source: the official Zakopane website.

Getting there from Krakow

The journey time changes with traffic, holidays and snow. A private car gives flexibility for luggage and stops, while the bus can be economical. Do not schedule an activity with a fixed time straight after arrival. Read the Krakow guide when building your route through the south.

A three-day itinerary

  1. Arrival, Krupówki and a light walk around.
  2. Gubałówka or Kasprowy, depending on the weather and booking.
  3. A nature trail suited to the group, or an indoor alternative and relaxation.

To compare the areas of the south, see the mountain resorts guide, and for the full trip see Poland for families.

Entry documents: Poland is part of the Schengen area, and the requirements differ according to the traveller's nationality and residence status. Check the official European Union portal and your airline before paying. Arcadia does not issue or process visas.

Activities by season

In summer (June–September): the Gubałówka funicular and its summer toboggan run, Morskie Oko lake by horse-drawn carriage (a two-hour walk back if you wish), the Chochołowska Valley by bike, and the rope park for children on Krupówki. In winter (December–March): ski school for children from the age of 4 on the gentle Harenda slopes, the snow slides on Gubałówka, horse-drawn sleigh rides in the snow, and the thermal baths (Termy Bukovina and Chochołowskie Termy) with their warm outdoor pools in the middle of the snow, the best family activity in the region. In every season: the Krupówki market with its smoked "oscypek" cheese and wool, and the Tatra Museum.

Approximate cost

A family wooden chalet or a 4-star hotel costs USD 100–180 per night (higher at New Year and in February), the Gubałówka funicular USD 8 per person return, the horse-drawn carriage to Morskie Oko USD 15–20 per person, the thermal baths USD 15–25 per person, and a private ski lesson for a child USD 40–60 per hour including equipment. A family of four spends USD 350–550 a day including accommodation and the car. Halal food in Zakopane

Halal options are fewer than in Krakow: two Turkish restaurants on Krupówki, fish restaurants (the grilled mountain trout is famous) and vegetarian pizza, and hotels that cook halal chicken and meat on advance request when you book with us. Take some dry meals with you from Krakow for the long mountain day.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zakopane suitable for children?

Yes, provided you choose a short activity and a back-up plan for the weather, and do not assume that every trail is suitable for a pushchair.

Is a day trip enough?

It gives you a glimpse, but two or three nights are more comfortable and reduce the impact of traffic on the road.

Is the cable car guaranteed to run?

No; wind, maintenance and capacity can all have an effect, so follow the official source.
