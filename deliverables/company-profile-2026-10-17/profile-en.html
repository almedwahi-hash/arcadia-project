Who we are

Arcadia is a tourism and ground-services company based in Almaty with offices and partners in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Russia, Poland and China. We have served families and individual travellers for years, and we offer companies and event agencies a complete service for incentive trips, conferences, training, team building and business missions, for any group size and from any country.

What we organise for companies

Incentive trips

Rewarding top performers and sales teams: 4- and 5-star hotels, curated excursions, gala dinner and awards, gifts and photography.

Conferences and meetings

Hotel and congress venues, screens and sound, simultaneous interpretation, registration desk, on-site event coordinator.

Training and team building

A training room in the morning and a group activity in the afternoon: mountains, lakes, Kazakh yurts, cooking workshops, team challenges.

Business missions

The Canton Fair, Yiwu and specialised fairs, factory and supplier appointments, business interpreter, car with driver.

Events and gala dinners

Award ceremonies and closing dinners in a hotel ballroom or heritage venue, with décor, entertainment and photography.

VIP services and logistics

Airport meet-and-greet, coaches and executive cars, English- and Arabic-speaking escorts, halal meals and prayer arrangements on request, 24/7 support.
