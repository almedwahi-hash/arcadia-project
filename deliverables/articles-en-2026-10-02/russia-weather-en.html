Introduction: why Russia?

Russia is one of the most varied and exciting destinations in the world, offering visitors a unique blend of history, culture and striking natural scenery. Before you pack your bags, though, it is important to understand the weather in Russia month by month, so that your trip is comfortable and enjoyable. In this article we look at how the weather can affect your journey and what you need to know before you travel.

Weather in Russia month by month: what you need to know

Winter in Russia: December to February

Winter in Russia is cold and harsh, with heavy snowfall in most cities, including Moscow and St Petersburg. Temperatures can drop to -20°C or lower, especially in the northern regions. If you plan to visit Russia in winter, make sure you wear heavy clothing that can stand up to the cold. This is an ideal time for lovers of winter sports such as ice skating. See our Moscow guide for more details on winter activities.

Spring in Russia: March to May

As spring begins, the snow gradually melts and flowers come into bloom in the public parks. The weather becomes milder, which makes spring a wonderful time to explore famous Russian cities such as St Petersburg. Temperatures range from 5 to 15°C, so lighter clothing is best, with a warm coat kept to hand. This is a good time to visit the palaces and museums and enjoy their interiors; for more information, see our St Petersburg guide.

A practical step-by-step guide

When planning your trip to Russia, it is important to book tickets and hotels in advance, especially in the high tourist seasons such as summer. Public transport such as the metro and buses can be used to keep costs down, and it is widely available in the major cities.

If you plan to visit rural areas or places far from the capital, check the train timetables and book your train tickets before you travel. Trains in Russia are a comfortable and enjoyable way to travel between cities.

Prices and budget in detail

Prices in Russia vary considerably depending on the city and the season. In Moscow, a night in a mid-range hotel can cost around 100 US dollars, while cheaper options can be found in the suburbs. As for food, a meal in a mid-range restaurant costs between 10 and 20 US dollars.

Entry tickets to museums and attractions range from 5 to 15 US dollars, and you can get discounts when you buy tickets online or as part of a guided tour. For more details on offers and programs, see Offers and Reservations.

Important tips before you travel

Check that your passport is valid and sort out your entry requirements well before you travel: citizens of Saudi Arabia (since 11 May 2026), the UAE, Qatar and Oman enter Russia visa-free for up to 90 days, while citizens of Kuwait and Bahrain use the e-visa. It is also a good idea to check the weather forecast a few days before departure to avoid surprises.

It is best to carry some cash in Russian roubles, as some shops may not accept credit cards. Learning a few basic Russian phrases is also recommended, as it makes it easier to communicate with local people.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can you travel to Russia without a visa? It depends on your nationality. Citizens of Saudi Arabia (since 11 May 2026), the UAE, Qatar and Oman enter Russia visa-free for up to 90 days, and citizens of Kuwait and Bahrain use the e-visa. Many other nationalities need a visa to enter Russia.
  • What is the ideal time to visit Russia? It depends on your interests: summer suits outdoor activities, while winter is ideal for those who love snow.
  • Is English widely spoken in Russia? In the major cities, yes, but in rural areas it may be better to communicate in Russian.
