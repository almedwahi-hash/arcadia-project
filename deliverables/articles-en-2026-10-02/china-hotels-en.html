The choice of hotel in Beijing and Shanghai shapes the comfort of the trip more than in any other city: both cities are enormous, the metro is the main way of getting around, and the difference between a hotel near a central metro station and one in an outlying area is an hour a day in the crowds. This guide is written for Arab families: the best areas to stay in each city, 5- and 4-star hotels with family and connecting rooms, prices in yuan and riyals, and tips on halal food, payment and registration.

Beijing: where to stay

  • Wangfujing: the city's most famous shopping street, 15–20 minutes' walk from the Forbidden City; major hotels, restaurants and metro stations. The best choice for families on a first visit.
  • Sanlitun and Chaoyang: the embassy district with its modern malls and international and Arab restaurants, about half an hour by metro from the sights.
  • The Central Business District (CBD / Guomao): towers and luxury hotels, suited to those combining business with sightseeing.
  • Niujie: the historic Muslim quarter with its great mosque and halal restaurants, simpler hotels and lower prices, 20 minutes from the centre.

The best hotels in Beijing for families

The Peninsula Beijing

In Wangfujing, one of the most luxurious hotels in the city, with an indoor pool, very spacious rooms and family-friendly service. 2,500–4,500 yuan per night (about 1,300–2,370 SAR, or $350–630).

Grand Hyatt Beijing

Above the Oriental Plaza mall in Wangfujing, with a covered tropical-style pool that children love and a metro station beneath the hotel. 1,500–2,500 yuan (about 790–1,320 SAR, or $210–350).

Hilton Beijing Wangfujing

Some of the largest rooms in Beijing, a pool, and close to the Forbidden City. 1,200–2,000 yuan (about 630–1,050 SAR, or $170–280).

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun

A modern hotel in the heart of Sanlitun with a pool and views, and halal and Arab restaurants nearby. 1,200–2,000 yuan (about 630–1,050 SAR, or $170–280).

Novotel Beijing Peace (Wangfujing)

The best-located mid-range choice: family rooms at a good price, a few minutes from Wangfujing. 700–1,100 yuan (about 370–580 SAR, or $100–155).

Serviced apartments in Sanlitun and Chaoyang

Two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen and washing machine, suited to large families and to those who want to cook their own food. 800–1,500 yuan (about 420–790 SAR, or $110–210).

Shanghai: where to stay

  • The Bund (Waitan) and Nanjing Road: the historic waterfront with its view of the towers, the most famous shopping street, and metro stations. The best choice for tourists.
  • Lujiazui (Pudong): the district of the towers, the aquarium and the science museum, with luxury hotels in the towers and panoramic views.
  • Jing'an: a quiet, upmarket district with malls and international hotels, 10 minutes by metro from the Bund.
  • Near Disneyland: the resort hotels, for those setting aside two days for the theme park.

The best hotels in Shanghai for families

Grand Hyatt Shanghai (Jin Mao Tower)

A hotel on the upper floors of the Jin Mao Tower in Pudong, with unforgettable views and a pool at a great height. 1,500–2,800 yuan (about 790–1,470 SAR, or $210–390).

The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong

Facing the Bund across the river, with a spa, a pool and family rooms. 2,500–4,500 yuan (about 1,300–2,370 SAR, or $350–630).

Le Royal Méridien Shanghai (Nanjing Road)

A tower on Nanjing Road by People's Square, one metro stop or about 20 minutes' walk from the Bund, with spacious rooms and a pool. 1,100–1,800 yuan (about 580–950 SAR, or $155–250).

The Portman Ritz-Carlton, Shanghai (Jing'an)

A classic hotel in the Shanghai Centre complex, with restaurants, shops and a pool; a favourite with families. 1,800–3,000 yuan (about 950–1,580 SAR, or $250–420).

Shanghai Disneyland hotels

The Shanghai Disneyland Hotel and the Toy Story Hotel are inside the resort, with park-entry benefits for hotel guests (check the current terms) and an atmosphere that children love. 1,500–3,500 yuan (about 790–1,840 SAR, or $210–490).

Serviced apartments in Jing'an and Nanjing Road

Modern two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen, 800–1,500 yuan (about 420–790 SAR, or $110–210), a practical choice for large families.

Booking tips for China

  • Registration: hotels register foreign guests with the police automatically on arrival, using the passport; choose hotels that are used to foreign guests (all those we have mentioned are).
  • Payment: international cards are accepted in the large hotels, but restaurants and taxis rely on Alipay and WeChat Pay, which you link to your own card, as explained in our guide to China travel apps.
  • Breakfast: the buffet in the large hotels has items that are clearly halal (eggs, bread, fruit, vegetarian dishes); avoid meats that are not labelled. Halal restaurants are covered in halal food in China.
  • Internet: hotel Wi-Fi does not open Google services or WhatsApp without a VPN installed before you travel; see the internet and VPNs in China.
  • Family rooms: ask for connecting rooms in advance; Chinese hotel rooms are usually for two people.

How much does a family stay cost?

For a family of four, 4 nights in Beijing and 3 in Shanghai in 4-star hotels: about 5,500–8,000 SAR (about $1,470–2,130); in 5-star hotels: 10,000–16,000 SAR (about $2,670–4,270). The full cost is set out in China travel costs 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area for families to stay in Beijing?
Wangfujing for being close to the sights, and Sanlitun for the halal and Arab restaurants.

And in Shanghai?
Nanjing Road and the Bund for a first visit, and Pudong for those who want the views.

Do Chinese hotels accept Saudi bank cards?
The large hotels do, and in restaurants you pay through Alipay linked to your card.

Are there hotels near the mosque in Beijing?
Yes, in Niujie, in simpler categories and at lower prices.
