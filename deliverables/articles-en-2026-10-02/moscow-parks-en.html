Introduction: why Russia?

Russia stands out for its captivating cultural and geographical variety, and Moscow is one of the leading cities drawing visitors from all over the world. If you are looking for a distinctive experience that combines natural beauty with modernity, Moscow's parks and leisure gardens offer that and more. In this article we look at the best parks you can visit in Moscow and how to get the most out of your visit.

Moscow's parks: a green paradise in the heart of the capital

Gorky Park: the ideal destination for families

Gorky Park is one of the best-known parks in Moscow, combining leisure activities with wide green spaces. You can enjoy a stroll along the tree-lined paths, or hire a boat on the ponds in summer. Entry to the park is free, but some activities carry a small charge of between 300 and 500 roubles. To reach the park, take the metro and get off at Park Kultury station.

Zaryadye Park: technology meets nature

Zaryadye Park is a unique experience that brings technology and nature together. It lies next to Red Square and features a floating bridge with a superb view over the Moskva River. Visitors can wander through different natural landscapes, such as tundra and forest. It is advisable to book tickets for some of the interactive shows online in advance to avoid the crowds. A practical step-by-step guide

Start your day with a visit to Gorky Park in the early morning to enjoy the calm, then move on to Zaryadye Park in the afternoon for the interactive shows. Do not forget to stop at one of the local cafes for coffee or Russian tea. If you plan to visit more than one park in a day, the metro is the best way to save time and money.

Prices and budget in detail

Moscow offers a wide range of leisure options, and you can shape your budget around the activities you prefer. Entry to the parks themselves is free, with paid activities inside costing up to 500 roubles, while the interactive shows at Zaryadye Park can cost up to 1,000 roubles. It is best to set aside a daily budget of between 2,500 and 4,000 roubles to cover everyday expenses such as food and transport.

Important tips before you travel

Make sure your passport is valid and check the entry rules for your nationality before you travel: citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Oman enter Russia without a visa for up to 90 days, while citizens of Kuwait and Bahrain use the e-visa. It is best to book tickets for the interactive shows in advance to avoid the crowds, especially in summer. You should also carry cash in Russian roubles, as foreign Visa and Mastercard cards do not work in Russia; visitors pay in cash or with UnionPay or Mir cards.

It is also important to check the weather before you travel, as it can be cool even in summer.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the best way to reach Moscow's parks? The metro is the fastest and most efficient way to get around Moscow.
  • Are there free activities in Moscow's parks? Yes, many of the parks offer free activities such as strolling and walking along the nature trails.
  • When is the best time to visit Moscow's parks? May to September is the best period to visit the parks, when the weather is mild and suited to outdoor outings.
