The best districts to stay in Moscow for families

  • Tverskaya and Red Square (the city centre): everything on foot: the Kremlin, GUM, Nikolskaya Street and Zaryadye Park. The most expensive, and best suited to a stay of 3–4 nights.
  • Arbat and Smolenskaya: the pedestrian Arbat Street and its restaurants, 15–25 minutes' walk from the Kremlin, with slightly lower prices.
  • Moscow City (the towers): skyscrapers and modern hotels with views, and the Afimall shopping centre; suitable for those who want luxury and quiet, and 15 minutes by metro from the centre.
  • Kievskaya: near the Evropeisky shopping centre and Kievsky railway station, with the Aeroexpress to Vnukovo airport; practical for families flying in or out of Vnukovo.
  • Komsomolskaya: beside Leningradsky station, where the Sapsan trains to St Petersburg depart; practical for families continuing to St Petersburg.
  • Sokolniki and Prospekt Mira: near the Cathedral Mosque, the halal restaurants and Sokolniki Park, with budget prices.

5-star hotels for families

Four Seasons Moscow (now independently run)

Right beside Red Square, with an indoor pool, spacious family rooms and a children's service. The most expensive in the city, and suitable for those who want the undisputed best location. Approximate price: 45,000–80,000 roubles per night (about 2,000–3,550 SAR, or $530–950).

The Carlton, Moscow (formerly Ritz-Carlton)

On Tverskaya Street facing the Kremlin, with a rooftop bar famous for its view, a spa and a pool. Approximate price: 40,000–70,000 roubles (about 1,800–3,100 SAR, or $475–830).

Lotte Hotel Moscow

Near Arbat, one of the best hotels for families in Moscow: connecting rooms, an indoor pool, a spa, and restaurants with halal options on request. Approximate price: 30,000–50,000 roubles (about 1,350–2,200 SAR, or $355–590).

Radisson Collection (Hotel Ukraina)

One of Stalin's seven skyscrapers, on the Moskva River, with family rooms and a pool, and river cruises departing from right in front of it. A favourite with Gulf travellers for years. Approximate price: 20,000–35,000 roubles (about 900–1,550 SAR, or $235–415).

Hotel Metropol and Hotel National

Two historic hotels minutes from Red Square with classic decor, well suited to honeymooners and to those who like old-world character. Approximate price: 20,000–40,000 roubles (about 900–1,800 SAR, or $235–475).

4-star hotels with excellent value

Hilton Moscow Leningradskaya

A historic Stalin-era skyscraper beside three railway stations, including Leningradsky station for the Sapsan to St Petersburg, with spacious rooms and a pool, 15 minutes by metro from the centre. 12,000–20,000 roubles (about 530–900 SAR, or $140–235).

Novotel Moscow Kievskaya

Next to Kievsky station and the Evropeisky shopping centre, with family rooms for four; handy for the Aeroexpress to Vnukovo airport. 10,000–16,000 roubles (about 450–710 SAR, or $120–190).

Azimut Smolenskaya

Next to Arbat Street, with modern rooms and a reasonable price, and halal restaurants nearby. 9,000–15,000 roubles (about 400–670 SAR, or $105–180).

Plaza Garden Moscow WTC (formerly Crowne Plaza World Trade Centre)

On the river near Moscow City, with large rooms and a pool; suitable for large families. 11,000–18,000 roubles (about 490–800 SAR, or $130–215).

Serviced apartments in Moscow City

The Moscow City towers have two-bedroom serviced apartments with a kitchen and panoramic views, an excellent choice for families who want a kitchen and prefer to cook. 12,000–25,000 roubles (about 530–1,100 SAR, or $140–295).

What to watch out for when booking

  • Payment: international booking sites generally do not accept Gulf cards for payment in Russia, where foreign Visa and Mastercard cards do not work, and hotels ask for payment on arrival in cash (roubles) or with a local card. Arcadia books and pays on your behalf at a fixed price in riyals. The details are in Russia for Saudi travellers.
  • Breakfast: the buffet includes pork in some dishes; ask the hotel to mark the halal items, or have breakfast at the nearby halal restaurants listed in our guide to halal restaurants in Moscow.
  • Registration: the hotel registers your stay officially (with the migration authorities) and gives you a slip; keep it until you leave.
  • Family rooms: most Russian hotel rooms are for two people; ask in advance for connecting rooms or a suite for a family of four or more.
  • Season: book at least two months ahead for trips in July and August and at New Year.

How much does a family stay in Moscow cost?

For a family of four for 4 nights: about 2,200–3,500 SAR in a 4-star hotel, and 5,500–9,000 SAR in a 5-star hotel. For the wider cost of a trip, see our guide to the cost of travel in Russia.
