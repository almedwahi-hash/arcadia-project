Burabay (also known as Borovoe; Burabay is its Kazakh name) is the "Switzerland of Kazakhstan", as locals call it: blue lakes among granite rocks and pine forests in the middle of the steppe, 250 kilometres north of Astana. It is the main nature destination close to the capital, so it is added to Astana itineraries as a day trip or a two-night stay, and it suits families who want boats, easy walks and clean air without rugged mountains. This guide covers getting there from Astana, the main lakes and sights, activities, the best time to go, hotels, and the cost in Saudi riyals.

Where is Burabay and how do you get there?

  • By car from Astana: about 3 hours on a good road. This is what we use in our programs, in a private car with a photo stop in the steppe.
  • By the fast Talgo train: from Astana to Kurort-Borovoe (Borovoe Resort) station in two and a half to three hours, then 20–30 minutes by car to the lakes.
  • From Almaty: a flight to Astana (1 hour 45 minutes), then the car or the train.

The main sights in Burabay

  • Lake Burabay (Auliekol): the main lake and the legendary Zhumbaktas rock (the Sphinx rock), which stands in the middle of the water and is visited by boat or seen from the shore.
  • Mount Okzhetpes: a rocky peak overlooking the lake, best seen from the shore and from the viewpoints around it.
  • Lake Shchuchye (Shortandy): a large, deep lake with most of the hotels on its shores.
  • Lake Bolshoye Chebachye and the quiet area for camping and fishing.
  • Granite rock formations in the forest: rocks in strange shapes along short trails that children love.
  • The small Nature Museum in the village, with animals of the region.

Activities for families

  • Boats: rowing boats, motor boats and jet skis on Lake Burabay from May to September.
  • Walking and cycling: flat trails around the lakes among the pines, and bicycles for hire.
  • Horse riding: at small farms near the village.
  • Swimming: the water is cold (18–22°C in July) and suits young people more than small children.
  • Winter: sledging, family skiing, walking on the frozen lake, and ice fishing.

Best time to visit Burabay

  • June – August: the main season, 22–28°C, with every activity open; hotels fill up at weekends with Astana residents.
  • September: the most beautiful colours, and calm after the season ends.
  • December – February: snow and severe cold (-20°C or lower), suitable for those who want a winter atmosphere with a warm hotel and a spa.

Hotels in Burabay

There are resorts on Lake Shchuchye with lake-view rooms, spas and indoor pools (the best fit for families), smaller hotels in Burabay village near the main lake, and wooden cabins and glamping for honeymooners. Prices are $60–200 per night depending on the season, and early booking is essential in summer. For hotels in the capital, see the best hotels in Astana.

Suggested itinerary: Astana and Burabay in 4 days

  1. Day 1, Astana: Baiterek, Khan Shatyr and the Grand Mosque, as in our Astana travel guide.
  2. Day 2: leave for Burabay in the morning, boats and the Zhumbaktas rock in the afternoon, overnight on the lake.
  3. Day 3: the Okzhetpes viewpoints and the forest rock trails in the morning, then return to Astana in the evening or stay a second night.
  4. Day 4, Astana: Nur Alem and the National Museum, then the flight to Almaty or home.

Food and prayer

Restaurants in Burabay are mostly Kazakh and halal (shashlik, plov, grilled lake fish), and there is a mosque in Burabay village and another in Shchuchinsk. Bottled water and medicines are available in the village, though there are fewer pharmacies than in the cities.

Approximate cost

For a family of four, two nights in a 4-star resort with a private car from Astana, an Arabic-speaking guide and boats: about 2,500–4,000 SAR ($670–1,070). For a wider breakdown of prices, see the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Burabay from Astana?
About 250 kilometres: 3 hours by car, or two and a half to three hours by train.

Is a day trip enough?
It is possible (6 hours on the road), but one night on the lake is much better.

Burabay or Kolsai for a family?
Kolsai is more beautiful and more rugged, near Almaty; Burabay is easier and quieter, near Astana. See also the top 10 attractions in Kazakhstan.

Are the activities suitable for older travellers?
Yes, the boat rides and the walk around the lake are on level ground, and the hotels have lifts.
