Hotels in Samarkand and Bukhara are an experience in themselves. In Samarkand there is the new Silk Road Samarkand complex, with international hotels on an artificial lake; in Bukhara, 19th-century historic houses have been turned into boutique hotels with inner courtyards, a few steps from the Kalyan Minaret. This guide helps Arab families choose: the best areas to stay in both cities, tried-and-tested 5-star, 4-star and boutique hotels, prices in US dollars and Saudi riyals, and what suits children and older travellers in old cities with narrow lanes.

Samarkand: where to stay

  • The Silk Road Samarkand complex: a new tourist city on a lake, 15 minutes from the Registan, with several international hotels, pools, spas, a congress centre and the "Eternal City" with its bazaars and craftsmen. The best choice for families who want pools and plenty of space.
  • The city centre near the Registan: mid-range and boutique hotels 5–15 minutes' walk from the square, suited to those who want to return to the hotel between tours.
  • University Boulevard and the modern city: modern business hotels at good prices, 10 minutes by car from the sights.

The best hotels in Samarkand

Samarkand Regency Amir Temur

The most luxurious hotel in the Silk Road Samarkand complex and in the whole of Uzbekistan: spacious suites, indoor and outdoor pools, a large spa and several restaurants. $250–450 per night.

Hilton Samarkand Regency

In the same complex with a view of the lake, family rooms, a pool and a kids' club, and a generous breakfast. $180–300.

Hilton Garden Inn Samarkand

The mid-range option inside the Silk Road Samarkand complex, with modern rooms and a reasonable price, plus access to the complex's facilities. $110–180.

Eternal City hotels (boutique hotels inside the complex)

Several small boutique hotels in traditional style inside the "Eternal City", for honeymooners and lovers of an oriental atmosphere. $120–220.

Boutique hotels near the Registan

Traditional houses with inner courtyards, minutes from the Registan and Shah-i-Zinda, with family rooms and a home-style breakfast; most have no lifts. $60–120. We choose the ones that suit older travellers (ground-floor rooms).

Hotels in the modern city

Modern 4-star hotels with small pools and prices of $70–120, suited to a practical stay.

Bukhara: where to stay

  • The old city (around Lyabi-Hauz and Po-i-Kalyan): boutique hotels in historic houses with decorated courtyards, with every sight within walking distance. The most beautiful option, but the lanes are cobbled and a car cannot always reach the door.
  • The edges of the old city: modern 4-star and 5-star hotels with pools, 5–10 minutes away by car, suited to large families and to the summer heat.

The best hotels in Bukhara

Modern 5-star and 4-star hotels

Several modern international and regional hotels with pools and spas have opened in recent years on the edges of the old city, at prices of $120–250. They are the best choice for families with small children in summer, because temperatures in Bukhara exceed 40°C.

Historic boutique hotels

The 19th-century houses of Bukhara's merchants, with courtyards and carved wooden ceilings, two minutes from Lyabi-Hauz, with family rooms and a traditional breakfast. The most beautiful stay in the whole of Uzbekistan. $70–150. We recommend ground-floor rooms for older travellers.

Mid-range hotels

Clean 3-star and 4-star hotels at prices of $40–80, suited to a two-night stay.

Booking tips for Uzbekistan

  • Book early in spring and autumn: good hotels fill up two months ahead in season.
  • Older travellers: boutique hotels usually have no lifts; ask for the ground floor or choose the modern hotels.
  • Payment: international cards are accepted in the large hotels, and cash (US dollars or som) in the small boutique hotels.
  • Breakfast: halal everywhere, and traditional in the boutique hotels (bread, cheeses, jams, eggs).
  • Getting around: the high-speed train runs between the cities, and hotels arrange the transfer from the station, or you can use Arcadia's private car.

How much does the stay cost for a family?

For a family of four, 3 nights in Samarkand and 2 in Bukhara in 4-star hotels: about 2,500–4,000 SAR (or $670–1,070); in 5-star hotels: 5,000–8,000 SAR (or $1,330–2,130). For wider trip costs, see Uzbekistan facts: entry, flights and costs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best hotel in Samarkand for families?
Hilton Samarkand Regency in the Silk Road Samarkand complex, thanks to the pools and kids' club, and Samarkand Regency Amir Temur for those who want the most luxurious.

Should I stay in the old city in Bukhara?
Yes, if you are not travelling with small children or older relatives who need a lift; otherwise the modern hotels with pools are a better fit.

Do hotels accept cards?
The large ones do, and the small ones prefer cash.

How many nights in each city?
Two to three nights in Samarkand and two nights in Bukhara, as in the 7-day Uzbekistan program.
