There are natural places in Kazakhstan that lie beyond the usual Almaty tours, but "hidden" does not mean easy to reach or unregulated. Kaindy Lake, the Kolsai Lakes, Charyn Canyon and Altyn-Emel National Park offer completely different landscapes, and each has its own road, timing and preparation to suit a family.

The planning rule: do not combine Kaindy, Kolsai, Charyn and Altyn-Emel in a single day. The distances are long, and some roads and trails change with the weather. Set aside a clear day for each area, and check the road conditions and any permits or fees close to the date of your visit.

The best nature destinations around Almaty

Kaindy Lake: the sunken forest

Kaindy is known for its mountain water and the spruce trunks that rise from the surface of the lake, so that it looks like a forest emerging from the water. The last stretch from the village of Saty is a rough track that needs a 4x4, local transport or a walk, depending on the season and the arrangements actually in place.

Kaindy is not a city promenade: wear sturdy shoes, carry an extra layer, and do not stand on slippery edges for the sake of a photo. With small children, ask in advance about the length of the drive and the walk, and about toilets.

Kolsai Lakes: a calmer day in nature

Kolsai suits those who want a mountain lake and a wide view, with walking options of varying difficulty. A first visit does not need to attempt all the lakes; a family can enjoy the first lake and return at ease. See our Almaty travel guide to find out what you need before setting off.

Charyn Canyon: the Valley of Castles

Charyn is not just a photo stop along the road. The area includes several canyons and trails, and the Valley of Castles is the best-known option. Kazakhstan's official tourism portal presents a route that passes through several canyons and takes a long day, with a recommendation to wear sports shoes and clothing; so the route should be chosen according to the family's fitness, not according to the photos alone.

Shade is limited and temperatures can climb, so start early and carry water and sunscreen. Do not stray from the marked trail, and do not let children go near the edges.

Altyn-Emel: the dunes and the Aktau Mountains

Altyn-Emel National Park combines desert expanses with colourful geological formations, among them the Singing Dune and the Aktau Mountains. But it is remote and needs better planning than a short tour: a suitable vehicle, fuel, water, and confirmation of the entry routes and of accommodation if the visit runs over several days.

What about Talgar Peak?

Talgar Peak is in the Trans-Ili Alatau range above Almaty, not in the Altai Mountains. It is a professional mountaineering objective, not an ordinary family sightseeing stop. You can enjoy Almaty's mountains from developed sites such as Shymbulak without presenting the peak as a trip that is easy or safe for everyone.

Best time to visit

Late spring, summer and early autumn are generally suitable for the lakes and trails, but each area has its own conditions. Almaty may be warm while Kaindy stays cold, and the summer heat can make Charyn exhausting at midday. Use our guide to the best time to visit Almaty as a starting point, then check the forecast for the area itself before the tour.

A suggested five-day itinerary

  1. Day 1: arrival and rest in Almaty.
  2. Day 2: Almaty and Shymbulak, or an activity nearby.
  3. Day 3: Charyn Canyon, with a trail that suits the family.
  4. Day 4: Kolsai and Kaindy if the road, the time and the family allow, or Kolsai only at a more relaxed pace.
  5. Day 5: a light day in Almaty, then preparing for departure.

Altyn-Emel deserves a separate program or a longer stay rather than being squeezed into this schedule.

What to put in the car and the bag

  • Enough water and a snack that suits the children.
  • Sturdy shoes, layers of clothing and a light waterproof.
  • Sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses.
  • Your child's usual medicine and a simple first-aid kit.
  • A power bank and a map saved for offline use.
  • Rubbish bags; leave the place as you found it.

Transport: an ordinary car or a 4x4?

The decision depends on the destination and on the state of the road on the day of the visit. The main roads may be good, but the last stretch to a natural site can change after rain or maintenance work. We cannot describe any one vehicle as always suitable; the local organiser confirms the type of car before departure, taking into account the number of passengers, the luggage and child seats.

Budget without misleading figures

There is no single "daily average" that holds for all of these trips. The cost of a day inside Almaty is different from a private car to Kaindy or an Altyn-Emel program. Ask for a quote that sets out the car, the driver's hours, the guide, park fees, meals and any overnight stay. For comparison, see the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

Safety and respect for nature

  • Follow the trails and local instructions, and do not climb on fragile formations.
  • Do not approach or feed the animals.
  • Avoid swimming or boating except where it is permitted and organised.
  • Tell the driver or guide about any health condition or mobility difficulty.
  • Change the plan in bad weather; a photo is not worth the risk.

Frequently asked questions

Can Kaindy and Kolsai be visited in one day?
It is possible in some programs and conditions, but it is a long day. With small children, Kolsai on its own may be more comfortable and more realistic.

Is Charyn suitable for children?
It suits many families when you choose a short trail, start early and carry water, while keeping a constant eye on children near the edges.

Do we need a guide?
A local driver or guide is useful for the remote roads and for changes in access and weather, especially if this is your first visit or you do not speak Russian or Kazakh.

Is Talgar Peak part of the program?
It is not treated as a family tour. It is a professional mountaineering objective that requires experience, equipment and the appropriate permits, and it is not a substitute for the developed viewing sites.
