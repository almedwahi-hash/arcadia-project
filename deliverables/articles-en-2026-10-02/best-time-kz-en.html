The first question families ask before booking a trip to Kazakhstan is: when should we go? The short answer: from mid-May to mid-September for nature, lakes and mild weather, and from December to February for snow and skiing. In this guide we explain each season month by month, with the weather in Almaty and Astana, the activities suited to each period and the prices to expect.

Quick summary

  • Best for families: June, July and August (the Gulf summer school holiday, 25–32°C in Almaty, and the lakes and mountains at their most beautiful).
  • Best for price and quiet: May, September and early October.
  • Best for snow: January and February (Shymbulak and the snow parks).
  • Least suitable: November and March to early April (changeable weather, melting snow and mud, and some mountain roads closed).

Summer (June–August): the season for Gulf families

This is the season when most of our clients travel, because it coincides with the school holidays and offers an escape from the Gulf heat. Almaty is warm by day (around 30°C), but as soon as you climb to the Kolsai and Kaindy lakes, the Assy Plateau or the Shymbulak cable car, the temperature drops to 15–20°C and you will need a light jacket. For more places to visit, see our top 10 attractions in Kazakhstan. All the mountain roads are open and every activity is available: horse riding, boat trips, tours of Charyn Canyon and walks in the mountain pastures. The only drawback: prices are at their peak, and hotels and drivers must be booked at least two months in advance. Read more in Kazakhstan as a summer destination for Gulf families.

Spring (April–May): flowers and lower prices

April is the month of wild flowers and tulips on the Kazakh steppe, and May combines mild weather, fewer crowds and prices about 20–30% lower than in summer. The caveats: some high mountain passes stay closed by snow until late May, and spring rain is common in Almaty. Very suitable for families travelling outside the school holidays.

Autumn (September–October): the golden autumn

September, in our view, is the most beautiful month in Almaty: 22–26°C by day, the trees turning gold, and nature still fully open. Early October is still good, but after the middle of the month the real cold begins and snow arrives in the mountains. For more on the city itself, see our Almaty travel guide.

Winter (December–February): snow and skiing

For those who want a real snow experience with the children: Shymbulak resort 30 minutes from Almaty, a children's ski school, ice skating at Medeu (one of the highest-altitude ice rinks in the world), and the ice festivals in Astana. The cold in Astana is severe (-15°C and below), so we advise families to keep their winter program to Almaty. Read more in winter tourism in Kazakhstan and Almaty in winter.

Dates to watch

  • Nowruz (21–23 March): the country's biggest festival, with street celebrations, but some museums and offices are closed.
  • Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha: peak demand from the Gulf; book flights early.
  • 6 July (Capital Day): a public holiday with celebrations and crowds in Astana.
  • 1 September: the start of the Kazakh school year; prices fall immediately afterwards.

How to choose by the children's ages

  • Children under 5: May, June or September (mild weather, neither hot nor bitterly cold).
  • Ages 6 to 12: July and August for nature and adventure, or February for snow and skiing.
  • Teenagers: any season; winter is excellent for skiing and snowmobiling.

Prices by season

For a family of four for one week (excluding flights): summer $2,500–3,800, spring and autumn $1,900–3,000, winter $2,000–3,200 (skiing raises the cost). The full breakdown is in the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kazakhstan hot in summer?
Almaty reaches 33°C in July, but humidity is low, and the mountains 30 minutes away are ten degrees cooler. Astana is less hot and windier.

Can Kaindy Lake be visited in winter?
Yes, but the road needs a 4x4 and may be closed after heavy snow; May to October is best.

When is travel cheapest?
May and September–October: almost the same nature at lower prices and with lighter crowds.

Do I need a visa?
Citizens of the Gulf states are visa-exempt for 30 days. Details in Kazakhstan visa.
