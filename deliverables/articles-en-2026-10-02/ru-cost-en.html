One city or two?

Moscow only

Suitable for a shorter trip or a more cautious budget. Transfer costs are lower and there is more time for Red Square, the Kremlin, the museums and the parks without changing hotel.

Moscow and St Petersburg

A rich option for a relatively long first visit, but it needs careful planning of the train or domestic flight, the luggage and the check-ins. Seven nights is a practical minimum, and eight or nine nights is more comfortable with children.

Factors that raise or lower the price

  1. Date: busy seasons, events and holidays affect hotels and flights.
  2. Hotel location: a central hotel may cost more but saves travelling time.
  3. Rooms: two rooms or a family suite, and the extra-bed policy, change the budget.
  4. Guide: the days and hours of service should be clearly stated in the quote.
  5. Tickets: some attractions require advance booking and a set time slot.
  6. Travel between cities: class, departure time and luggage affect the price.

The best time from a budget point of view

Late spring and early autumn may offer a good balance between walking and weather, but prices do not follow a fixed rule. Compare one trip on the same date and do not rely on an old estimate. For the seasons, see the best time to visit Moscow and Russia's weather month by month.

How do we cut the cost without weakening the trip?

  • Choose one city if the stay is short.
  • Plan one main pre-booked activity per day and leave the rest flexible.
  • Compare the hotel's location against transport cost and time, not the room price alone.
  • Send the children's ages before pricing so that rooms and seats can be determined accurately.
  • Ask for international flights to be separated from the local programme so you can see where the price changes.

Adding St Petersburg to the programme

The prices above are for the Moscow programme. When the trip is split between Moscow and St Petersburg, Sapsan high-speed train tickets (USD 180–320 for four people) are added, along with the difference in hotels and tours in the second city, and the price is set in the quote according to the number of nights in each city.

Flights from Riyadh, Dubai or Doha to Moscow cost USD 450–900 per person depending on the season, and the e-visa (for citizens of Kuwait and Bahrain only; Saudis, Emiratis, Qataris and Omanis enter visa-free) is around USD 50 per person. Figures rise by 20–30% during White Nights week (mid-June) and at New Year, and fall by the same proportion in September and from mid-January to March.

How do you pay in Russia?

International cards do not work inside Russia, so the easiest approach is to pay for the whole programme in advance (hotels, train, car, tickets, and a daily dinner if you wish), leaving you needing only cash for shopping and independent meals: USD 100–150 a day for the family, exchanged at a bank in the city.
