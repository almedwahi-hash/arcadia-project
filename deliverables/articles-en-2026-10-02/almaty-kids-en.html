Almaty is one of the easiest cities for families travelling with children: short distances, nature half an hour from the hotel, halal food everywhere, and activities that suit everyone from two-year-olds to teenagers. In this guide we have gathered ten experiences that our Gulf family clients have tried on their trips to Kazakhstan, with practical tips for each activity.

1. The Shymbulak cable car

The first experience for every family: three cable-car stages from Medeu up to an altitude of 3,200 metres. Children see snow even in June, and at the top there is a café and a snow play area. It is best to go up in the morning, before the clouds come in, and to bring a jacket, because it is ten degrees colder at the top.

2. Medeu ice rink

The highest ice-skating rink in the world. In winter, children can rent skates for a token price; in summer, they can climb the "Health Stairs" with their 842 steps or ride bikes in the park next door.

3. Big Almaty Lake

A turquoise lake among the mountains, 40 minutes away by car. Swimming is not allowed because the lake is a source of drinking water, but walking along the path, taking photos and horse riding at the entrance are a favourite with younger children.

4. The zoo and the First President's Park

Almaty Zoo has snow leopards, bears and birds of prey, with modest entrance fees, and the First President's Park, with its fountains and wide lawns, is ideal for family outings in the afternoon.

5. Kok Tobe tower and the city cable car

A short cable car ride from the city centre to Kok Tobe hill, where there is a small Ferris wheel, a petting zoo, children's rides and a night-time view over the whole city. A good choice for the evening.

6. The water park and the aquarium

The water park on the edge of the city has indoor pools and water slides, and the indoor aquarium is there for rainy or cold days.

7. The horse farm on the Assy Plateau

Two and a half hours from the city lie vast meadows where thousands of horses graze. Children ride with an instructor, and the family has a traditional Kazakh lunch in a yurt. A full-day experience, in summer only.

8. Charyn Canyon

A day trip to "Kazakhstan's Grand Canyon", three hours away. The walk through the canyon is easy for children over six, and it is best to go in spring or autumn to avoid the heat. See also our guide to the top 10 tourist attractions in Kazakhstan.

9. Kolsai and Kaindy lakes with an overnight stay

For families with children over eight: a night in the wooden cabins at Kolsai Lake, kayaking in the morning, and a visit to Kaindy with its sunken trees. The road is mountainous, so choose a driver who knows the area.

10. The Green Bazaar and Arbat Street

An easy cultural experience: dried fruit, nuts and mountain honey to taste at the Green Bazaar, then a stroll along Arbat Street with its artists and amusements, finishing with ice cream in Panfilov Park near the wooden cathedral.

A suggested five-day program with children

  • Day 1: Arrival, the First President's Park, Kok Tobe in the evening.
  • Day 2: Medeu and Shymbulak, lunch at the top, the Green Bazaar.
  • Day 3: Big Almaty Lake in the morning, the aquarium or the Aquapark in the afternoon.
  • Day 4: Charyn Canyon (or the Assy Plateau in summer).
  • Day 5: The zoo, Arbat Street, shopping at Dostyk Plaza, departure.

Yes. The cable car and the path at Big Almaty Lake are set up for families, and Charyn suits children over six.
