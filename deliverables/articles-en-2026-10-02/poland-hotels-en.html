Warsaw and Krakow are the two stops on every family trip to Poland, and the hotel in each city shapes the day: in Krakow the Old Town is small and you can walk from the hotel to every sight, while in Warsaw the distances are greater and being close to the metro and the Old Town matters. This guide recommends the best areas and hotels in both cities for Arab families: 5- and 4-star hotels with family and connecting rooms, serviced apartments, prices in zloty and riyals, and tips on the season and halal breakfast. It complements our guide to hotels and cottages in Poland, which covers the mountains and the countryside.

Where to stay in Warsaw

  • The Old Town and Krakowskie Przedmieście: the Royal Castle, the Old Town Square and the restaurants on foot; the prettiest choice for families and for a short stay.
  • The city centre (Śródmieście) around the Palace of Culture: the central railway station, the metro, the Złote Tarasy mall and most of the international hotels. The most practical.
  • The new Wola district: modern towers and new hotels at lower prices, a few minutes by metro from the centre.

The best hotels in Warsaw for families

Hotel Bristol and Raffles Europejski (Royal Route)

Two historic luxury hotels on the Royal Route next to the Presidential Palace, with a pool and spa at the Bristol, and family suites. 1,200–2,500 zloty per night (about 1,200–2,500 SAR, or $320–670).

InterContinental Warsaw

A tower in the city centre with a pool on the 43rd floor overlooking the city, and family rooms and serviced apartments. 700–1,200 zloty (about $190–320).

Warsaw Marriott

Opposite the central railway station, with spacious rooms, a pool and a health club; ideal for those travelling on to Krakow by train. 600–1,000 zloty (about $160–270).

Hilton Warsaw City

In Wola, with a large pool and good prices for families. 450–800 zloty (about $120–215).

Novotel Warszawa Centrum and ibis

The Novotel is next to the Palace of Culture, with family rooms for two adults and two children at a reasonable price; the ibis hotels are a little further out and cheaper. 350–650 zloty (about $95–175).

Serviced apartments in the city centre

Two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen near the metro, 350–700 zloty (about $95–185), the most economical choice for large families.

Where to stay in Krakow

  • The Old Town (Stare Miasto): inside the Planty, the ring of gardens around the Main Square; everything on foot, higher prices, and streets that are sometimes closed to cars.
  • Kazimierz: the historic district of restaurants, cafés and halal restaurants, 15 minutes' walk from the Main Square.
  • Near the railway station and Galeria Krakowska: modern hotels with malls, 10 minutes' walk from the Old Town, and practical for arriving from Warsaw.

The best hotels in Krakow for families

Sheraton Grand Krakow

On the Vistula River next to Wawel Castle, with an indoor pool and family rooms; the best choice for families with children. 700–1,300 zloty (about $190–350).

Hotel Stary and the boutique hotels of the Old Town

Historic townhouses a few steps from the Main Square with luxurious rooms, for honeymooners and those who want the old-world character. 800–1,600 zloty (about $215–425).

Radisson Blu Krakow

Next to the Planty, 5 minutes from the Main Square, with spacious rooms and a good breakfast. 500–900 zloty (about $135–240).

Holiday Inn Krakow City Centre and Hilton Garden Inn

Two practical hotels with family rooms and reasonable prices: the Holiday Inn a few minutes' walk from the Old Town, the Hilton Garden Inn across the river about 20 minutes' walk away. 400–700 zloty (about $105–185).

Vienna House Andel's Cracow

Next to the railway station and Galeria Krakowska, modern and comfortable, 10 minutes' walk from the Main Square. 450–800 zloty (about $120–215).

Serviced apartments in Kazimierz

Restored two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen in the middle of the restaurants, 350–650 zloty (about $95–175).

Booking tips for Poland

  • Visa: a confirmed, cancellable hotel booking is part of the Schengen application file, as explained in our Poland Schengen visa guide.
  • Breakfast: the Polish buffet contains pork in the sausages and bacon; the safe items are obvious (eggs, cheeses, bread, fruit), so ask about the meats.
  • Family rooms: rooms for four are common at the Novotel and in the apartments, and less common in the historic hotels.
  • Payment: Gulf bank cards are accepted almost everywhere.
  • The train between the two cities: two and a half hours on the fast train; hotels near the two stations save time.

How much does a family stay cost?

For a family of four, 3 nights in Warsaw and 3 in Krakow in 4-star hotels: about 3,000–4,500 SAR (about $800–1,200); in 5-star hotels: 6,000–10,000 SAR (about $1,600–2,670). The full cost is set out in the cost of a Poland trip for families.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area for families in Krakow?
The Old Town for those who want everything on foot, and Kazimierz for being close to the halal restaurants.

And in Warsaw?
The city centre near the station and the metro for practicality, and the Old Town for the atmosphere.

Are Polish hotels suitable for conservative families?
Yes: the service is professional and accustomed to Gulf guests, and you can ask for rooms away from the bar.

How many nights in each city?
Three nights in each, as in the 7-day Poland program.
