A honeymoon in Kazakhstan is now requested by many Gulf newlyweds in place of the traditional destinations, and for clear reasons: mountain scenery and turquoise lakes a few hours from the Gulf, visa-free entry for most Gulf countries, a high level of privacy in mountain resorts and luxury camps, halal food everywhere, and a lower cost than Europe or the Indian Ocean islands. This guide suggests a one-week itinerary, the best romantic hotels and resorts in Almaty, Astana and Burabay (Borovoe), the best season, and the approximate cost in Saudi riyals.

Why Kazakhstan for a honeymoon?

  • A short distance: 4–6 hours by direct flight from the Gulf to Almaty.
  • No visa: citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman enter visa-free for 30 days. The details are in our Kazakhstan entry guide.
  • Privacy: mountain resorts, cabins and luxury tents (glamping) away from the crowds, and restaurants with views instead of packed streets.
  • Variety: in a single week you see a modern city, snowy mountains, lakes, a rock canyon that resembles the Grand Canyon, and a futuristic capital.
  • Cost: a luxury week for two costs far less than in Switzerland or the Maldives.

A Kazakhstan honeymoon itinerary: 7 nights

Days 1–2: Almaty, the city between the mountains

Arrival and a private welcome at the airport, with a stay in a hotel overlooking the mountains. The first evening is at Kok Tobe, reached by cable car, to watch the sunset and the city lights and have dinner at the top. Day two: Medeu and Shymbulak, going up to Talgar Pass at 3,200 metres for lunch above the clouds, then Panfilov Street, the wooden Ascension Cathedral and the cafés of the city centre.

Day 3: Big Almaty Lake and a horse farm

A turquoise lake at 2,500 metres surrounded by peaks, an hour from the city, followed by a horse farm in the foothills for a photo session on horseback. With a full extra day, the Turgen Gorge and the Assy Plateau are an alternative. For more places to see, read the top 10 attractions in Kazakhstan.

Days 4–5: Charyn Canyon and the Kolsai and Kaindy lakes

The two most beautiful days of the trip. Charyn Canyon with its red rock formations, then an overnight stay in a cabin or luxury tent near Kolsai Lake: clear starry nights, a private barbecue, and a morning on the lake by boat. The next day brings Kaindy Lake with its sunken trees, the most famous image of Kazakhstan, before the return to Almaty in the evening.

Days 6–7: Astana, the futuristic capital

A morning flight to Astana (about 1 hour 45 minutes). The Baiterek tower, the Khan Shatyr tent, the Nur Alem sphere, the Astana Grand Mosque, and dinner at a restaurant overlooking the river. Astana's luxury hotels are among the best in Central Asia and their prices are competitive. Read our Astana travel guide.

Alternative for days 6–7: the resort area of Burabay (Borovoe), 3 hours from Astana: lakes among pine forests and rocks, lakeside hotels, boats and walks; a quieter option than the city.

The best romantic hotels and resorts

  • Almaty: the five-star hotel in Esentai Tower with mountain views, the hotels on Dostyk Avenue, and the Shymbulak mountain resort for those who prefer to sleep at 2,200 metres. See the best hotels in Almaty.
  • Kolsai Lakes: wooden cabins and luxury tents with proper beds and private bathrooms; they are booked up early in summer.
  • Astana: the luxury hotels on the bank of the Ishim River near Baiterek.
  • Burabay: the resorts on Lake Shchuchye, with rooms overlooking the water and spas.

Special experiences for newlyweds

  • A private dinner at the top at Kok Tobe or Shymbulak, with a table reserved for sunset.
  • A professional photo shoot at Kaindy Lake, Charyn Canyon or the lavender fields near Almaty in early summer.
  • Horse riding in the Turgen Gorge or on the Assy Plateau.
  • A hot-air balloon flight near Almaty (seasonal).
  • Spas and baths in Almaty's hotels, and the well-known Arasan Baths with separate sections.
  • Skiing together at Shymbulak if the wedding is in winter, with a private instructor.

Best time for a honeymoon in Kazakhstan

  • May – June: spring, flowers, full lakes, and fewer crowds than in summer. The best time, in our view.
  • July – August: warmer and busier, but all the mountain roads are open.
  • September – October: golden autumn colours, settled weather and lower prices.
  • December – February: for lovers of snow, skiing and a winter atmosphere, though the road to Kaindy usually closes because of snow.

How much does a honeymoon trip cost?

Estimates for two people, 7 nights, excluding international flights:

  • 4-star level with a private car and guide: about 9,000–13,000 SAR ($2,400–3,470).
  • 5-star level with private experiences (photo shoot, private dinner, lakeside cabin): about 15,000–22,000 SAR ($4,000–5,870).
  • International flights: 1,500–3,000 SAR ($400–800) per person return, depending on the season.

Compared with other destinations, that is noticeably less than Switzerland or the Maldives at the same level. The full breakdown is in the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

Tips for newlyweds

  • Book the Kolsai cabins at least two months ahead in summer, as their number is limited.
  • Tell the hotel that you are on your honeymoon; most hotels offer room decoration and a room upgrade free of charge.
  • Pack for two climates: 30°C in Almaty and 10°C at the lakes and on the mountain passes.
  • Food is halal in Kazakh, Uzbek and Turkish restaurants; see our guide to halal restaurants in Almaty.
  • Gulf bank cards work normally in Kazakhstan, unlike in Russia.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kazakhstan suitable for a honeymoon for conservative couples?
Yes. It is a Muslim-majority country, privacy is available in the resorts and cabins, and family restaurants are widespread.

How many days are enough?
7 nights are ideal for Almaty, the lakes and Astana; 5 nights are enough for Almaty and the lakes only.

Are the roads to Kolsai and Kaindy difficult?
The road to Kolsai is paved, while Kaindy needs a four-wheel-drive vehicle for the last few kilometres; we provide one as part of the program.

Can Uzbekistan be added?
Yes, by a short flight from Almaty; for the Uzbekistan part, see our suggested 7-day Uzbekistan program.
