Medeu and Shymbulak are the two best-known sights in Almaty and the first thing Gulf visitors ask for after they arrive: a cable car that climbs from an ice rink at 1,700 metres to a mountain resort at 2,260 metres and on to a pass at 3,200 metres, all of it 30 minutes from the city centre. In winter this is the leading ski resort in Central Asia; in summer it becomes a place for walking through alpine meadows, taking photographs and having lunch above the clouds. This guide explains how to get there, what to do in each season, what tickets cost, and what children and older travellers need to know.

Medeu: the highest skating rink in the world

Medeu is an ice rink in a mountain valley at 1,691 metres. It is the highest Olympic-size rink in the world, and dozens of speed-skating records have been set there. The rink operates from late autumn until spring, and skates can be rented on site in all sizes, including children's sizes. In summer the area is used for walking and cycling. Next to the rink is the Medeu dam, reached by a long staircase (842 steps) with a superb view over the valley; the climb suits younger visitors better than older ones.

Shymbulak: the mountain resort

Shymbulak is a ski resort at 2,260 metres, directly above Medeu. It has a hotel, restaurants and cafés with mountain views, a ski school, equipment rental shops and a children's play area. The resort is open all year, and the cable car runs in summer and winter apart from short maintenance periods in spring and autumn.

How to get there from Almaty

  • By car or taxi: 25–35 minutes from the city centre to Medeu along Dostyk Avenue and then the mountain road. The Yandex Go app accepts international cards and cash.
  • By bus no. 12: it runs from the city centre directly to Medeu and is the cheapest option.
  • With an Arcadia program: a private car with an Arabic-speaking guide from your hotel, including tickets, waiting time and the return journey. This is the most comfortable option for families with children.

On winter weekends the road above Medeu is sometimes closed to cars and the cable car becomes the only way up to Shymbulak, so start early.

The cable car: three stages

  1. Medeu to Shymbulak: enclosed gondola cabins, about 15 minutes, arriving at the resort plaza.
  2. Shymbulak to the second-level station (about 2,850 metres): open chairs or cabins, depending on the season.
  3. Up to Talgar Pass (3,180 metres): the highest point visitors can reach, with a panoramic view of the snow-covered Tian Shan peaks even in August.

Tickets are sold per stage or as a combined ticket for all three. Approximate prices in 2026: the combined return ticket costs about 7,000–10,000 tenge for adults (about 50–75 SAR, or $13–20), with a discount for children, and the first stage alone costs less than half of that. Children under 5 usually travel free. Prices change every season, and daily ski passes in winter follow a different system.

What to do in winter (December to March)

  • Ice skating at Medeu: an easy experience for the whole family, with skates for rent.
  • Skiing at Shymbulak: runs for beginners and experts totalling more than 20 kilometres, lessons for children from age 4 with English-speaking instructors, and full equipment rental (suit, boots, skis and helmet).
  • Snow tubing for small children in a dedicated area.
  • Hot chocolate and lunch in the resort restaurants with a view of the snow.

The ski season usually starts in late November or December and runs until April, depending on the snow. Full details are in Kazakhstan in winter.

What to do in summer (June to September)

  • Ride up to Talgar Pass, where snow stays on the neighbouring peaks and the temperature is 10–15°C while Almaty is at 30°C.
  • Walking trails from Shymbulak: short, easy trails for families (30–60 minutes) and longer ones to a waterfall and small lakes.
  • Lunch in the resort restaurants, which serve Kazakh and European dishes; see our guide to halal restaurants in Almaty.
  • Photography: the best light is in the morning, before the clouds that usually gather in the afternoon.

Weather and clothing

The difference between Almaty and Talgar Pass can reach 20 degrees. Even in July, take a light jacket, comfortable shoes, sunglasses and sunscreen, because the sun is strong at this altitude. In winter the temperature at Shymbulak is between -5 and -15°C and full winter clothing is essential; suits can be rented on site.

Tips for children and older travellers

  • The altitude of 3,180 metres can cause mild dizziness for some people. Go up gradually, pause at Shymbulak before the last stage, and drink water.
  • Pushchairs are allowed in the enclosed gondola, but the upper stages use open chairs that are not suitable for infants.
  • Travellers with blood pressure or heart conditions should stay at Medeu and Shymbulak and skip Talgar Pass.
  • The ideal visit lasts 3–4 hours. In our programs it is combined with Kok Tobe or the Green Bazaar on the same day.

Best time to visit

Summer (June to September) for green landscapes and walking, and winter (December to March) for skiing. July and August are the busiest months with Gulf visitors, so early mornings on weekdays are best. See the best time for family travel.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit Shymbulak in summer?
Yes. The cable car runs all year, and summer is the best time for walking and views.

How long does it take to go from Medeu up to Talgar Pass?
About 40–50 minutes across the three stages, including stops.

Is it suitable for small children?
Yes, as far as Shymbulak, which has a play area and restaurants. The last stage is better for children over 5.

Do I need to book in advance?
Not for the cable car, but ski lessons and the hotel need booking in winter.

Is there halal food at Shymbulak?
Yes, the restaurants serve halal Kazakh dishes; confirm the dish when you order.
