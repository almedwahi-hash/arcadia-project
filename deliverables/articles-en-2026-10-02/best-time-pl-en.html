Poland is a destination for every season, but each season has a different face: a mild, green summer in Warsaw and Krakow, a quiet golden autumn, a snowy winter in Zakopane with Christmas markets, and a flowering spring at lower prices. For most Arab families, the best time to visit Poland is from mid-May to mid-September, and for those who want snow, December to February in the south. This guide explains the weather month by month, the dates and prices to know about, and how to choose according to your children's ages.

Quick summary

  • Best for families: June, July and August (18–25°C, daylight until 9 pm, and every activity open).
  • Best for price and quiet: May, September and early October.
  • Best for snow and winter atmosphere: December (Christmas markets), January and February (skiing in Zakopane).
  • Least suitable: November and March (grey and rainy, with neither snow nor greenery).

Summer (June–August): the family season

The weather is mild and pleasant (it rarely goes above 30°C), the days are long, and the parks, lakes and outdoor activities are all open: rafting on the Dunajec river near Zakopane, the Gubalowka funicular, the Wieliczka salt mine, and the Baltic beaches of Gdansk. This is the season most Gulf families choose to escape the heat; the only drawbacks are higher prices and a crowded Krakow in July and August, so book your hotels two months in advance. Spring (April–May): flowers and lower prices

May is the smartest month for anyone who can travel outside the school holidays: 15–20°C, Warsaw's parks in bloom (Lazienki Park and the free Chopin concerts every Sunday from mid-May), and prices about 25% lower than in summer. April is colder and less settled, but fine for the cities.

Autumn (September–October): the golden autumn

The "Polish golden autumn" is a local expression for September and early October: gentle sunshine, autumn colours in the mountains and parks, and no crowds. Excellent for Krakow and Zakopane in particular. After mid-October the rain and fog begin.

Winter (December–February): snow and Christmas markets

December means the Christmas markets in Warsaw and Krakow, with their lights and stalls (from late November to early January), and January and February usually mean reliable snow in Zakopane: skiing for beginners and children, sledging, horse-drawn sleigh rides in the snow, and wooden chalets. The cold in the cities is relatively moderate (around zero) compared with Russia or Kazakhstan. Details in winter in Krakow and Zakopane.

Dates to watch

  • Easter (March/April): widespread shop closures on the Sunday and Monday.
  • 1–3 May: a long holiday weekend, with domestic crowds in Zakopane and Krakow.
  • 15 August, 1 November and 11 November: public holidays; museums may be closed.
  • 24–26 December: almost everything is closed; plan Christmas at the hotel or in Zakopane.
  • Sundays: most shopping centres are closed on most Sundays (the Sunday trading law); the traditional markets and restaurants stay open.

How to choose by the children's ages

  • Children under 5: June or September; mild weather and cities that are easy to explore with a pushchair.
  • Ages 6 to 12: July and August (outdoor activities, water parks, Energylandia near Krakow) or February for skiing.
  • Teenagers: any season; winter is excellent for skiing and snow.

Prices by season

For a family of four for one week (excluding flights): summer $2,600–3,900, spring and autumn $2,000–3,000, December and the ski season $2,300–3,500. The full breakdown is in the cost of tourism in Poland 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is Poland hot in summer?
No; the average is 23–25°C, with short spells reaching 30°C. Evenings are relatively cool.

When is there snow in Zakopane?
Usually from mid-December to early March, with the ski season at its peak in January and February.

Do Gulf families need a visa?
Citizens of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman need a Schengen visa from the Polish embassy; start two months ahead for the summer season. UAE citizens enter the Schengen area visa-free. Details in Poland visa.

When is travel cheapest?
November and March are the cheapest, but the weather is grey; the best balance of price and weather is May and September.
