The cost of a family trip to Kazakhstan in 2026 comes down to a few simple decisions: the number of nights, the hotel category, and whether you stay in Almaty or add Kolsai and Shymbulak. How does the cost vary from one family to another?

Season and arrival date

Summer dates and school holidays can push up flight and hotel prices. Travelling in late spring or early autumn may offer a wider choice, but the decision depends on the activities that matter to your family. The guide to the best time to visit Almaty will help you choose.

Hotel location and room type

Two separate rooms do not cost the same as one family room, and the policy on children and extra beds can differ from hotel to hotel. Compare breakfast, space, cancellation terms and the hotel's location, not just the number of stars. You can consult the Almaty hotels guide.

Number of long-distance excursions

Kolsai, Charyn and the mountain destinations need more time and different transport from a tour within Almaty. Grouping nearby sites into one day is better than spreading them out at random, but the day should not become exhausting for the children.

Children's ages

Children's ages affect rooms, seats and some tickets. Sending each child's actual age from the start prevents the price changing after booking.

A balanced seven-day family programme

  1. Day 1: arrival, rest and a light walk near the hotel.
  2. Day 2: Almaty's sights, markets and parks.
  3. Day 3: Shymbulak with flexible time for the family.
  4. Day 4: a quiet day or an activity suited to the children's ages.
  5. Day 5: Kolsai or a long nature excursion for those who are comfortable with the drive.
  6. Day 6: optional extras, shopping and restaurants.
  7. Day 7: spare time and departure according to your flight time.

Practical ways to reduce the budget

  • Fix your dates early and ask for a quote that sets out everything that is included.
  • Choose a hotel close to your daily route rather than the cheapest one far from everything.
  • Do not add a long-distance excursion every day; a flexible day saves money and is less tiring for the children.
  • Tell us how many bags you have, because a small car may not suit a family of four.
  • Keep a separate budget line for food and shopping so that these expenses are not mixed up with the programme.

Frequently asked questions

Are these prices fixed?

No. They are an indicative range for the programme with 4-star hotels, and the price becomes exact once the date, number of travellers, children's ages, hotel and tours are known.

Are seven days suitable for children?

Usually yes, provided the programme includes lighter days and the long excursions are not packed together without rest. Six to eight days is a practical length for a first visit.

Are there food options suitable for families?

There are plenty of options in Almaty. If you have halal requirements or food allergies, you should inform the restaurant and the organiser in writing and check the ingredients and the method of preparation.

How do I get a clear comparison?

Ask for a quote that states the hotel name or category, room type, transport, guide hours, tours and tickets, and the policy on children. That way the comparison is fair.
