The cost of a family trip to Kazakhstan in 2026 starts with a few simple decisions: the number of nights, the hotel level, and whether you stay in Almaty or add Kolsai and Shymbulak. For a family of two adults and two children for seven nights, the local programme usually ranges between $2,800 and $4,500 depending on the season and the details, before international flights and personal expenses.

Important before you compare: the figures below are estimates, not a final price. They change with the travel date, the departure city, the number of travellers and the children's ages, the hotel level, room and flight availability, and the type of tours. How does the cost change from one family to another?

The season and the arrival date

Summer dates and school holidays can push up flight and hotel prices. Travelling in late spring or early autumn may give you a wider choice, but the decision depends on the activities that matter to your family. Our guide to the best time to visit Almaty will help you choose.

Hotel location and room type

Two separate rooms do not cost the same as a family room, and the policy on children and extra beds can differ from one hotel to another. Compare breakfast, space, cancellation terms and the hotel's location, not just the number of stars. You can consult our Almaty hotels guide.

The number of long-distance trips

Kolsai, Charyn and the mountain destinations need more time and different transport from a tour within Almaty. Combining nearby sites in one day is better than spreading them out at random, but the day must not become exhausting for the children.

The children's ages

The children's ages affect the rooms, the seats and some of the tickets. Sending each child's real age from the start prevents the price changing after you have booked.

A balanced seven-day family itinerary

  1. Day 1: arrival, rest and a light walk near the hotel.
  2. Day 2: the sights of Almaty, the markets and the parks.
  3. Day 3: Shymbulak, with flexible time for the family.
  4. Day 4: a quiet day or an activity suited to the children's ages.
  5. Day 5: Kolsai or a long nature trip for those who can manage the road.
  6. Day 6: optional extras, shopping and restaurants.
  7. Day 7: spare time and departure according to your flight time.

Practical ways to reduce the budget

  • Fix your dates early and ask for a quotation that sets out everything that is included.
  • Choose a hotel close to each day's route rather than the cheapest one far from everything.
  • Do not add a long-distance trip every day; a flexible day saves money and tires the children less.
  • Tell us the number of bags, because a small car may not suit a family of four.
  • Keep a separate line for food and shopping so that these expenses are not mixed up with the programme.

Frequently asked questions

Is $2,800–4,500 a fixed price?
No. It is an indicative range for the local programme for a family of four, and the price becomes exact once the date, the ages, the hotel and the tours are known.

Are seven days suitable for children?
Usually yes, provided the programme includes light days and the long trips are not packed together without rest. Six to eight days is a practical length for a first visit.

Are there food options that suit a family?
There is a wide choice in Almaty. If you have halal requirements or a food allergy, you should inform the restaurant and the organiser in writing and check the ingredients and the method of preparation.

How do I get a clear comparison?
Ask for a quotation that states the hotel's name or category, the room type, the transport, the guide's hours, the tours and tickets, and the policy on children. That way the comparison is a fair one.
