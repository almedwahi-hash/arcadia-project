Five days is the length most often requested by Gulf families visiting Kazakhstan for the first time, especially on a short break or an Eid holiday. The question is always the same: is it enough? Yes, if you stay in Almaty and its surroundings and do not try to add Astana. This itinerary is designed to cover the best of Almaty, its mountains, lakes, rock canyon and the city itself, at a pace that suits children and older travellers, with approximate costs and alternatives for each day depending on the season.

Day 1: arrival and a view over the city

Direct flights from Riyadh, Jeddah and Dubai arrive in Almaty in the morning or around midday. After the airport pick-up and hotel check-in, we start with something that takes no effort: the Kok Tobe cable car from the city centre to the hilltop, with its TV tower, restaurants, children's play area and sunset view over the city and the mountains. Dinner follows on pedestrian Panfilov Street, near the wooden Ascension Cathedral. Our guide to traditional dishes of Kazakhstan will help you choose what to order.

Day 2: Medeu and Shymbulak

Leave at 9 am for the Medeu ice rink, then take the cable car to Shymbulak resort and on up to Talgar Pass at 3,200 metres, where there is snow even in summer. Lunch is at the resort restaurants, and in the afternoon you return to the city to visit the Museum of Folk Musical Instruments or the Park of 28 Panfilov Guardsmen. In winter this becomes a full ski day; see Kazakhstan in winter.

Day 3: Big Almaty Lake and horses

Big Almaty Lake lies at 2,500 metres, turquoise water among the peaks, an hour from the city (by car to the viewpoint, then a short walk). Lunch is at a mountain restaurant on the way back, followed by a horse farm in the Assy area or near the lake for riding and photos, or the First President's Park and its fountains in the city for those who prefer to rest. The evening is free for shopping at Dostyk Plaza or Mega.

Day 4: Charyn Canyon and Kolsai

The longest day and the most beautiful. Leave at 7 am and head east to Charyn Canyon (3 hours), whose red rock recalls the Grand Canyon, and walk for about an hour through the "Valley of Castles". Then continue to the first Kolsai Lake (2 hours) for lunch on the shore and a boat ride, returning to Almaty at night (3.5 hours). With small children or older travellers we recommend one of two options: visit Charyn only and return in the afternoon, or stay overnight at Kolsai, visit Kaindy Lake the next morning and push day 5 back. In winter (December to March) this day is replaced by Charyn alone or by a second day at Shymbulak, because the road to Kaindy is closed by snow. Read more in the top 10 attractions in Kazakhstan.

Day 5: the bazaar, shopping and departure

In the morning, the Green Bazaar for dried fruit, honey and gifts, then the pedestrian Arbat (Zhibek Zholy) street and, if time allows, the Central State Museum of Kazakhstan. Flights to the Gulf usually leave in the afternoon or evening, and the airport transfer is three hours before departure.

Alternatives by interest

  • For honeymooners: replace day 3 with an overnight stay in the Kolsai cabins.
  • For families with small children: replace day 4 with the water park, the aquarium and the zoo; see family trips in Kazakhstan.
  • For those who want Astana: we do not recommend it in five days. It needs at least seven, as in our one-week Kazakhstan itinerary.

How much does a 5-day program cost?

Estimates for a family of four (two children), excluding international flights:

  • 4-star hotels + private car and Arabic-speaking guide + tickets: about 6,500–9,000 SAR ($1,730–2,400).
  • 5-star hotels + an overnight at Kolsai: about 10,000–14,000 SAR ($2,670–3,730).
  • Direct flights: 1,500–2,800 SAR ($400–750) per person return, depending on the season.

The full breakdown is in the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

Best time for this itinerary

From May to October every day can be done exactly as described. In winter the program becomes a snow trip (two days at Shymbulak, one at Charyn, two in the city). See the best time for family travel.

Tips before you travel

  • Saudi and other Gulf citizens enter visa-free for 30 days; see our Kazakhstan entry guide.
  • Pack for two climates: the city is warm and the mountains are cold, even in summer.
  • Mountain days call for trainers, sunscreen and water.
  • Gulf bank cards work in Kazakhstan; carry a little cash for the bazaar.

Frequently asked questions

Are 5 days enough for Kazakhstan?
They are enough for Almaty and its surroundings in full, but not enough to add Astana.

Is the program suitable for older travellers?
Yes, with day 4 changed to Charyn only or to an overnight stay at Kolsai.

How long is the flight from Riyadh?
About four and a half hours direct to Almaty.

Can it be done in winter?
Yes, in a winter version that focuses on Shymbulak, the city and Charyn.
