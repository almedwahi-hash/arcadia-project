Sochi is the only destination in Russia that combines sea and mountains in a single city: warm Black Sea beaches in summer, the ski resorts of Krasnaya Polyana in winter, and between them subtropical gardens, waterfalls, the largest theme park in Russia and the venues of the 2014 Olympics. For Arab families who have already visited Moscow and Saint Petersburg and are looking for a new destination in Russia, Sochi is the natural next step. This guide covers getting there from the Gulf, the best areas to stay, activities for every season, halal food and approximate costs.

Where is Sochi and how do you get there?

Sochi stretches for about 140 kilometres along the Black Sea coast in southern Russia, near the Georgian border, and its international airport is in the Adler district. There are no direct flights from Saudi Arabia, and the usual routes are:

  • Via Moscow: a flight from Riyadh or Jeddah to Moscow (about 5 hours), then a domestic flight to Sochi (two and a half hours). This is the most common route on Arcadia programs, because it lets you combine Moscow and Sochi in one trip.
  • Via Dubai, Doha or Abu Dhabi: there are seasonal direct flights from the UAE and Qatar to Sochi, which suit those travelling from the UAE.

Saudi citizens have entered Russia visa-free since 11 May 2026 (up to 90 days per calendar year), as explained in our guide to Russia visa requirements for Arab travellers. Citizens of the UAE, Qatar and Oman also enter visa-free, while citizens of Kuwait and Bahrain still use the e-visa; see the visa exemption agreements.

Sochi's districts: where should a family stay?

  • Central Sochi: the seafront promenade, the botanical garden (the Dendrarium), restaurants and the yacht marina. It suits those who want a city atmosphere and evening walks.
  • Adler and the Olympic area: close to the airport, with the Olympic Park, the Sochi Park theme park and large hotels with their own private beaches. The best choice for families with children.
  • Krasnaya Polyana and Rosa Khutor: the mountain village 45 minutes from the sea on the fast Lastochka train, with European-style hotels, cable cars, skiing in winter and walking in summer. Many families split their stay between the sea and the mountains.

Best things to do in Sochi for families

1. Sochi Park: Russia's Disneyland

The largest theme park in Russia, inspired by Russian folk tales, with rides for children and teenagers, roller coasters and daily shows. It needs a full day and is located in the Olympic area.

2. The Olympic Park and the musical fountain

The stadiums of the 2014 Winter Olympics and the Olympic flame, and in the evening the dancing fountain show set to music. Electric bikes and carts can be hired for getting around the large site.

3. The cable cars at Rosa Khutor

A chain of cable cars climbs from 560 metres to 2,320 metres, with views over the Caucasus. In summer: the Rosa Peak platform, a rope park, walking trails and petting farms. In winter: the best skiing in Russia, with more than 100 kilometres of pistes.

4. Skypark: the longest suspension bridge in Russia

A footbridge 439 metres long across a valley at a height of 207 metres, with a giant swing and bungee jumping for the adventurous, and a safe walkway for families with children.

5. Waterfalls and nature

The Agura Waterfalls are half an hour from the city along an easy path, the 33 Waterfalls in the Shakhe valley are reached on a four-wheel-drive trip, and Mount Akhun has an observation tower overlooking the city and the sea.

6. Beaches and the sea

The beaches are mostly pebble, and the sea is warm from June to September (24–27°C). The large hotels in Adler have private beaches with parasols and sunloungers, and they are the best fit for conservative families.

7. The Dendrarium and Riviera Park

A subtropical botanical garden with a small cable car, peacocks and palm trees, and Riviera Park, with children's rides and a dolphinarium.

Halal food in Sochi

There are fewer halal options than in Moscow, but they are available: Caucasian restaurants (Azerbaijani and Dagestani) serving shashlik, kebab and fresh bread, whose meat is mostly halal; Turkish restaurants in the city centre and in Adler; and Uzbek restaurants for plov and manty. Look for the word "Халяль" on the storefront, and avoid Russian meat dishes unless the source of the meat is stated. Seafood and fish from the Black Sea are a safe choice in every restaurant. There are prayer facilities in the city, and in the large hotels you can ask for a room with the direction of the qibla indicated.

Best time to visit Sochi

  • June – September: beach season, with temperatures of 26–30°C; the best time for families. August is the busiest month, with Russian holidaymakers.
  • October – November: quiet, with lower prices and colourful mountains; the sea is cold.
  • December – March: ski season at Rosa Khutor, while the city by the sea stays mild (8–12°C).
  • April – May: spring and blossom; a good time for those who want to avoid the heat and the crowds.

How much does a family trip to Sochi cost?

These are estimates for a family of four for 5 nights in summer, in roubles and Saudi riyals (one riyal is about 21–24 roubles):

  • Accommodation: a 4-star hotel in Adler or Rosa Khutor, 12,000–25,000 roubles per night (about 550–1,100 SAR, or $145–295).
  • Domestic flight Moscow–Sochi: 6,000–15,000 roubles per person one way.
  • Activities: Sochi Park about 3,000–4,000 roubles per person, the cable cars 2,500–4,000 roubles, Skypark about 2,000 roubles.
  • Food: 1,500–3,000 roubles per person per day.
  • Approximate total: 9,000–15,000 SAR (about $2,400–4,000) for the family, excluding international flights, depending on the level of hotel.

  1. Days 1–3, Moscow: Red Square and the Kremlin, Zaryadye Park and a river cruise; see our Moscow guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sochi safe for families?
The resort areas are calm, with a visible security presence, and the city welcomes millions of visitors a year. Check your own government's travel advice and the flight situation before booking, as conditions in southern Russia can change.

Are there direct flights from Saudi Arabia to Sochi?
No. You travel via Moscow, or via the UAE and Qatar on the seasonal direct flights.

How many days are enough?
4–5 nights to cover the sea, the mountains and the theme park, added to 3 nights in Moscow.

Are the beaches suitable for conservative families?
The private hotel beaches in Adler are a comfortable option, and modest swimwear is common and accepted.

Is English widely spoken?
It is limited outside the large hotels, which is why an Arabic-speaking guide makes a big difference in Sochi.
