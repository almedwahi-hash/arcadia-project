Astana's hotels are among the newest in Central Asia. Most were built after 2010 as the city grew into its role as capital, and they include international names at prices clearly below Dubai and Istanbul. The city is divided by the Ishim River into the new Left Bank (home to Baiterek, Khan Shatyr and the main sights) and the older Right Bank (markets and restaurants). This guide recommends the best hotels in each category for Arab families, the best areas to stay, prices in tenge, and booking tips for a capital with a harsh winter and a short, busy summer.

Where to stay in Astana

  • The Left Bank (the new government district): around Baiterek, Nurzhol Boulevard and Khan Shatyr, with luxury hotels, sights and malls within walking distance. The best choice for tourists.
  • The Expo and Nur Alem area: modern hotels near the Expo 2017 site and the science museum; quieter and slightly further out (10 minutes by car).
  • The Right Bank (the old city): near the older main mosque, the Artyom bazaar and local restaurants; lower prices, and 15 minutes by car from the sights.

5-star hotels in Astana

The Ritz-Carlton, Astana

In Talan Towers on the Left Bank, the most luxurious hotel in the city, with an indoor pool, a spa and views of Baiterek. Family rooms and suites. Approximate price: 180,000–300,000 tenge per night (about 1,300–2,200 SAR, or $360–600).

The St. Regis Astana

On the riverbank near the government district, with butler service, a pool and spa, and a restaurant overlooking the river. A favourite with honeymooners. 150,000–250,000 tenge (about $300–500).

Rixos President Astana

The hotel most Gulf visitors choose: a Turkish group that knows Arab guests, a pool and spa, fully halal restaurants, and a location close to Baiterek. 100,000–180,000 tenge (about $200–360).

Hilton Astana

Next to the Expo area, Nur Alem and the Mega Silk Way mall, with spacious rooms and a pool. It suits families with children thanks to the mall and museum next door. 80,000–140,000 tenge (about $160–280).

Sheraton Astana

In a modern complex on the Left Bank, with family rooms, a pool and a health club, at competitive prices for the 5-star category. 70,000–120,000 tenge (about $140–240).

4-star hotels with excellent value

Astana Marriott

Near Baiterek and the Khan Shatyr mall, with modern rooms, a pool and halal restaurants nearby. 60,000–100,000 tenge (about $120–200).

Wyndham Garden Astana

Next to Nur Alem, a practical hotel with good prices and spacious rooms, suited to short stays. 40,000–70,000 tenge (about $80–140).

Hilton Garden Inn Astana

On the Left Bank, with reasonable prices, clean modern rooms and a good breakfast. 45,000–75,000 tenge (about $90–150).

Serviced apartments around Khan Shatyr

Modern two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen and washing machine in the new residential towers, suited to large families and longer stays. 35,000–70,000 tenge (about $70–140).

Booking tips for Astana

  • Winter: from November to March, choose a hotel connected to a mall or close to the sights, to reduce walking in the cold (-20°C).
  • Summer: book early; international conferences and forums fill the hotels without warning.
  • Payment: Gulf bank cards work normally in Kazakhstan, unlike in Russia.
  • Food: breakfast in every hotel includes clearly halal items, and Rixos is fully halal.
  • Airport: 20–30 minutes from most hotels, and the airport pick-up is included in Arcadia programs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best hotel in Astana for Gulf families?
Rixos President for a fully halal stay, and Hilton Astana for those travelling with children, because of the mall and Nur Alem next door.

Are hotels in Astana expensive?
No. A 5-star hotel costs about 700–1,300 SAR ($190–350) per night, clearly less than Dubai.

Where should I stay: the Left Bank or the Right Bank?
The Left Bank for tourists, because the sights and malls are there.

Are there serviced apartments for large families?
Yes, around Khan Shatyr and Baiterek at reasonable prices, and we book them regularly.
