Beijing and Shanghai, or Just One City?

Beijing is stronger for history and the Great Wall of China, while Shanghai offers a modern and quite different urban experience. Combining the two cities in eight days is possible, but it requires coordinating the luggage and the train or domestic flight. With young children, nine or ten nights may be more comfortable, or you can focus on one city with trips nearby.

What Pushes the Cost Up?

  1. Adding many cities and long internal distances.
  2. Central hotels and large family rooms.
  3. Dates that coincide with holidays and major events.
  4. Private tours and an escort for the whole day.
  5. Train tickets, domestic flights and luggage.
  6. Activities with expensive tickets or advance booking.

The Best Time in Terms of Comfort

Spring and autumn are generally suitable for walking, but China is vast and there is no single climate for all its cities. Beijing and Shanghai can differ in temperature and humidity on the same date. Check the forecast for each city, and avoid building the plan on a general description of China as a whole.

Food That Suits the Family

There are plenty of options in the big cities, but the word "halal" must be verified directly, according to the restaurant, the branch and the method of preparation. Send food allergies in writing, and keep a Simplified Chinese copy of the important requirements. We do not rely on old lists, because restaurants change.

Practical Tips Before You Travel

  • Save the addresses of the hotels and stations in Chinese on your phone.
  • Check in advance which communication and payment apps you will use during the trip.
  • Do not fill the arrival day with activities after a long flight.
  • Specify the size of the car, the number of bags and the child seats required.
  • Plan a lighter day between Beijing and Shanghai if the transfer is early.

What Makes the Cost Jump Suddenly?

Travelling during Golden Week (1–7 October) or the 1–5 May holiday, when hotel prices double; booking tickets to the sights late (the Forbidden City has a limited number of visitors per day); domestic flights instead of the train; and paying in cash at poor exchange rates.
