Beijing and Shanghai, or a single city?

Beijing is stronger for history and the Great Wall of China, while Shanghai offers a modern and quite different urban experience. Combining the two cities in eight days is possible, but it calls for coordinating the luggage and the train or domestic flight. With young children, nine or ten nights may be more comfortable, or you can concentrate on one city with trips nearby.

What pushes the cost up?

  1. Adding many cities and long domestic distances.
  2. Central hotels and large family rooms.
  3. Dates that coincide with holidays and major events.
  4. Private tours and a guide for the whole day.
  5. Train tickets, domestic flights and baggage.
  6. Activities with expensive tickets or advance booking.

The best time in terms of comfort

Spring and autumn are usually suitable for walking, but China is vast and there is no single climate for every city. Beijing and Shanghai can differ in temperature and humidity on the same date. Check the forecast for each city, and avoid building the plan on a general description of China as a whole.

Food that suits the family

There are plenty of options in the big cities, but the word "halal" should be verified directly for each restaurant, branch and method of preparation. Send any food allergies in writing, and keep a copy of the important requirements in Simplified Chinese. We do not rely on old lists, because restaurants change.

Practical tips before you travel

  • Save the addresses of the hotels and stations in Chinese on your phone.
  • Check in advance the communication and payment apps you will use during the trip.
  • Do not fill the arrival day with activities after a long flight.
  • Specify the size of the car, the number of bags and the child seats required.
  • Plan a lighter day between Beijing and Shanghai if the transfer is an early one.

What pushes the cost up suddenly?

Travelling in Golden Week (1–7 October) or the 1–5 May holiday, when hotel prices double; booking attraction tickets late (the Forbidden City has a limited number of visitors each day); domestic flights instead of the train; and paying in cash at poor exchange rates. How many days do we need for China?

Eight days is the practical minimum for Beijing and Shanghai, and nine or ten days are more comfortable for a family. A single city can suit a shorter trip.
