China is the size of a continent, and every region has its own climate, but for most Arab families visiting Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an and Guangzhou, the best time to visit China is from April to May and from September to October. This guide explains the weather month by month in the main cities, the Chinese holidays to avoid, and the best time for traders travelling to the trade fairs.

Quick summary

  • Best for family holidays: April and May, September and October (except Golden Week, 1–7 October).
  • Acceptable: March and November (a mild chill in Beijing, temperate in the south).
  • Summer (June–August): hot, humid and crowded because of the Chinese school holidays; possible with planning.
  • Winter (December–February): cold in Beijing, mild in Guangzhou; prices are lower, but avoid Chinese New Year.

Chinese holidays to avoid

  • Chinese New Year (Spring Festival): a full week between late January and late February (the date changes every year: 17 February in 2026 and 6 February in 2027). Hundreds of millions of people travel, trains are full and many shops are closed.
  • Golden Week (1–7 October): the peak of domestic tourist crowds; the Great Wall and the Forbidden City are packed.
  • Labour Day holiday (1–5 May): similar crowds, but shorter.
  • School summer holidays (July–August): the sights are crowded with Chinese families, and hotel prices are at their peak.

Spring (April–May): the best season for Beijing and Xi'an

Mostly clear skies, cherry blossom from late March to mid-April, and mild temperatures for climbing the Great Wall. See Beijing with kids to plan your days. For traders, the Canton Fair is held in Guangzhou from mid-April to early May, and hotel prices there rise sharply during that period.

Autumn (September–October): the most beautiful season for Shanghai and the south

The humidity drops, the typhoon season winds down, and Beijing's trees change colour in late October. The last two weeks of October and early November are an excellent period, with lower prices after Golden Week. The autumn session of the Canton Fair runs from mid-October to early November.

Summer: how to make the trip work with children

The heat and humidity in Shanghai and Beijing are tiring for children, but the trip is possible if you choose indoor activities at midday (museums, the aquarium, shopping malls) and outdoor sights in the morning, and visit the Great Wall first thing in the morning. Consider adding a cool city such as Kunming or the mountains of Zhangjiajie. Costs are higher in summer; see China travel costs 2026.

Winter: the cheapest season and warm alternatives

Beijing is dry and cold (around zero), but the Great Wall covered in snow is a rare sight, and the ice festival in Harbin (January) is a unique experience for those who can cope with -20°C. For families who want warmth: Guangzhou and Shenzhen at 15–20°C, and Hainan Island at 22–26°C. Prices in December and early January are the lowest of the year, except for the week of Chinese New Year.

The best time according to the purpose of your trip

  • A family with young children: April–May or September–October.
  • A trader visiting fairs and factories: April (the spring Canton Fair), or October–November; avoid January–February because the factories close for the Spring Festival.
  • A limited budget: late November to mid-December, or early March.
  • Nature and mountains: May–June and September; see nature in China.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do we need, and which cities?
Ten days for Beijing, Xi'an and Shanghai is ideal; the day-by-day plan is in our 10-day China itinerary for families, and help with choosing cities is in the best cities to visit in China.

Does the visa take a long time?
It depends on your nationality. Citizens of Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain enter China visa-free for up to 30 days under a policy that currently runs until 31 December 2026, and citizens of the UAE and Qatar are visa-exempt for 30 days under standing mutual agreements. For other nationalities, check the latest rules in our China visa guide 2026 and start a month before you travel.

When does the rainy season start in Shanghai?
The "plum rain season" runs from mid-June to early July: rain almost every day and high humidity.

Is China suitable during Ramadan?
Yes; halal restaurants are open, and in the coming years Ramadan falls in February and March, when the days are short and the weather is cool.
