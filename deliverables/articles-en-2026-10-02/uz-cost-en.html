Are Seven Days Enough for Three Cities?

They are enough for a quick first trip if the train times and hotels are well coordinated, but it can be tiring with young children or elderly travellers. If Samarkand and Bukhara are the priority, you can cut down the stops in Tashkent. And if comfort matters more, add a night or drop a city rather than squeezing in every sight.

The Best Time for Families

Spring and autumn are generally suitable for walking around the historic squares. Summer can be hot, so outdoor tours are arranged in the morning and midday is left for rest. Winter is less crowded but calls for warm clothing and a flexible plan. Do not rely on a seasonal average alone; check the forecast before each city.

What Pushes the Price Up?

  1. Booking three cities in a short period, with a lot of travelling between them.
  2. Two rooms instead of a family room, or hotels in a prime historic location.
  3. Train tickets at specific times or in a higher class.
  4. A private guide for the whole day instead of selected tours.
  5. Arriving in and departing from two different cities.

Tips for an Arab Family

  • Choose comfortable shoes, because the squares and old towns involve a lot of walking.
  • Write down food requirements and allergies clearly, and do not rely on an old label for a restaurant.
  • Keep a copy of the train times and station names on your phone.
  • Do not plan more than two major sites a day with children.
  • Ask about the lift and the room size if you have a pushchair or an elderly traveller with you.

Where to Save and Where Not to Save

Save on: the season (summer and winter are 20–30% cheaper), heritage hotels instead of international ones in Samarkand and Bukhara (more beautiful and cheaper), and local food (an excellent meal costs USD 8–12 per person). Do not save on: the high-speed train (the slow alternative wastes half a day), an Arabic-speaking guide (Uzbekistan without the historical commentary is half the experience), and a hotel with a pool if you travel between June and August.
