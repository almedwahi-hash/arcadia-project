Poland is the smartest European choice for a Saudi family: mild summer weather, safe old towns, mountains, lakes and sea, prices well below those of western Europe, and halal food that is easy to find in the big cities. The only hurdle is the Schengen visa, and this guide explains it step by step for Saudi travellers, along with flights from Riyadh and Jeddah, the cost in Saudi riyals, and the best times to go according to the Saudi school holidays.

The Schengen visa from the Polish embassy for Saudi travellers

  1. Book an appointment at the visa application centre used by the Polish embassy in Saudi Arabia 6 to 8 weeks before travel in summer (the embassy website lists the current centre and cities).
  2. Documents: a signed Schengen application form, a passport valid for 3 months after your return with two blank pages, two photos with a white background, a flight booking, confirmed hotel bookings for every night, travel insurance with cover of 30,000 euros, a bank statement for 3 months, and an employment letter or commercial registration. For children: a birth certificate, and parental consent if the child is travelling with only one parent.
  3. Fees: 90 euros per adult (about 390–400 SAR), 45 euros for children aged 6–11, free under 6, plus the visa centre's service fee.
  4. Processing usually takes 15 days (it can take up to 45 at busy times). Since April 2024 Saudi citizens have been eligible for five-year multiple-entry Schengen visas, even on a first application.

The best time for Saudi travellers, by holiday

  • Summer holiday (July–August): the best; 22–26°C, daylight until 9 pm, and the lakes, mountains and beaches all open. Book hotels two months ahead and apply for the visa in May.
  • Eid al-Adha: in late spring in the coming years, with mild weather; the mosque in Warsaw and the Islamic centre in Krakow hold the prayers.
  • Mid-year holiday (January): reliable snow in Zakopane for skiing and children's sledging, and Krakow lit up; the cold is moderate (around zero).
  • Spring holiday (March): grey and rainy; the least suitable.

Halal food and prayer

Warsaw and Krakow have dozens of halal restaurants (Turkish, Lebanese and Indian) and kebab chains in every district, and in Zakopane the hotels cook halal meals on advance request. There is the central mosque in Warsaw (Wiertnicza Street), an Islamic centre in Ochota, and an Islamic centre with a prayer hall in Krakow. The list is in halal restaurants in Warsaw.

Tips specific to Saudi travellers

  • Carry your insurance card and hotel bookings in print; they may be requested on entry to the Schengen area.
  • Shopping centres are closed on most Sundays; plan your shopping for other days.
  • SIM card: Orange or Play from the operators' shops, registered with your passport (about 25 SAR, or $7, for 20 GB), or European roaming on Saudi SIM cards with weekly packages.
  • Child seats are compulsory in cars; we provide them with the driver.
  • The Saudi embassy in Warsaw is available for emergencies.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter other Schengen countries on a Polish visa?
Yes, but Poland must be the main destination (the most nights); you can add two nights in Prague or Vienna.

Is Poland cold in summer?
It is mild: 22–26°C by day and 14–17°C at night; bring a light jacket for the evening.

Are people accepting of the hijab and abaya?
Yes, Poland is safe and respectful towards tourists, and Saudis visit in growing numbers every summer.

How long does the visa actually take?
Usually 15 days; in June and July it can take longer (up to 45 days), so apply in May.
