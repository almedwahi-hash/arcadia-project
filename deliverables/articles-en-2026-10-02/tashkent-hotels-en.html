Tashkent is the gateway to Uzbekistan: most international flights arrive there, and the high-speed train to Samarkand and Bukhara departs from it. Most families spend one or two nights in the city at the start of the trip and one night at the end, so it matters that the hotel is close to the sights and the railway station, with a good breakfast and a pool for the children. This guide recommends the best hotels in Tashkent for Arab families in each category, the best areas to stay, prices in US dollars and Saudi riyals, and booking tips.

Where to stay in Tashkent

  • The city centre (Amir Temur Square and Broadway Street): the large hotels, museums, restaurants and a metro station, 10 minutes from the railway station. The best choice for tourists.
  • Mirabad district: modern business hotels and shopping centres, 10 minutes from the centre.
  • The Old City (Chorsu and Hazrati Imam): boutique hotels near the bazaar and the religious complex, with a traditional atmosphere and everything within walking distance.
  • Tashkent City: the new district with its towers, large park and Tashkent City Mall; very modern hotels and evening fountains that children love.

5-star hotels in Tashkent

Hyatt Regency Tashkent

In the city centre near the theatre and the large park, with a pool, family rooms and a generous breakfast. The first choice for most of our families. $180–280 per night.

InterContinental Tashkent

A modern tower in the Tashkent City area with views of the park and the fountains, a pool and spa, and a mall beneath the hotel. $170–280.

Hilton Tashkent City

Next to Tashkent City Park and the mall, with connecting family rooms and a pool; very well suited to children. $150–240.

Established hotels near Amir Temur Square (Lotte City Hotel Tashkent Palace and others)

Long-established upper-range hotels with oriental decor, gardens and pools near Amir Temur Square, at lower prices than the newer international towers. $120–200.

4-star hotels with excellent value

Wyndham Tashkent

In the city centre, with spacious rooms and a pool, at a price that suits families. $90–140.

Radisson Blu Hotel, Tashkent

Near the centre, with a good breakfast and a pool; a quiet business hotel. $100–160.

Serviced apartments in Tashkent City

Two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen in the new towers, suited to large families and longer stays. $80–150.

Boutique hotels in the Old City

Traditional courtyard houses near Chorsu with a home-style breakfast and prices of $50–90, for those who prefer an authentic atmosphere.

Booking tips for Tashkent

  • Arrival night: flights from the Gulf usually arrive at dawn; book the previous night so that you can check in immediately, or ask for an early check-in.
  • Railway station: the Afrosiyob train departs early in the morning; hotels in the centre are 10–15 minutes from the station.
  • Payment: international cards are accepted in the large hotels, and money can be changed at banks and official exchange offices.
  • Breakfast: halal in every hotel.
  • Summer: choose a hotel with a pool; the temperature reaches 38°C in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best hotel in Tashkent for families?
Hyatt Regency for its location and pool, and Hilton Tashkent City for those travelling with children, because of the park and the mall.

Are hotels in Tashkent expensive?
No. An international 5-star hotel costs about 600–1,000 SAR ($160–270) per night, and a 4-star hotel about 350–550 SAR ($95–145).

Should I stay in Tashkent City or the city centre?
Tashkent City for families with children (park, fountains and mall), and the city centre for those who want museums and restaurants within walking distance.

Do hotels accept Saudi bank cards?
Yes, in the large hotels, but carry some cash for boutique hotels and the bazaar.
