Uzbekistan has a continental desert climate: a very hot summer, a cold winter, and a short but ideal spring and autumn. That is why the best time to visit Uzbekistan is from April to early June, and from September to early November. This guide explains each month, when the festivals are held, and how to plan if you have to travel in summer.

Quick summary

  • Best of all: April and May (spring, blossom and Nowruz), and September and October (autumn and the fruit season).
  • Acceptable with planning: June and late August (hot but dry; morning and evening activities).
  • Least suitable: July and early August (40°C and above in Bukhara and Khiva), and December–February (cold and fog in the cities, but lower prices).

Spring (March–May): the first golden season

April is the month we recommend first: 20–25°C, clear skies, gardens in bloom, and the Registan in Samarkand is comfortable to walk around all day long. In late March the Nowruz festival is held, a chance to see folk festivities and traditional food (sumalak). May is warmer, but excellent for Khiva and Bukhara before the heat sets in. The drawback: this is peak season for European tourists, so book the boutique hotels in Bukhara early.

Autumn (September–November): the second golden season

September and October combine ideal weather with the fruit season: grapes, pomegranates and melons are at their very best in the markets, and the sun is gentle in the lanes. Early November is still fine in the south. This season suits Gulf families travelling in the mid-term school holiday.

Summer (June–August): how to make the trip work despite the heat

Many families can only travel in the summer holidays. In July the heat approaches or exceeds 40°C in Bukhara and Khiva, but it is dry, and the trip works if you follow these rules:

  • Start the tours at 7 am and finish by 11, then rest at the hotel, then an evening tour from 5 to 9 pm (the monuments are lit up and beautiful at night).
  • Choose hotels with a pool in Samarkand and Bukhara.
  • Add two days in the Chimgan mountains near Tashkent (ten degrees cooler) or at Lake Charvak.
  • Best of all, if possible: the last two weeks of August instead of July.

Winter (December–February): lower prices and empty monuments

Cold (0–8°C), with occasional fog and light snow in Tashkent, but the monuments are almost empty and the hotels are about 30% cheaper. Suitable for families without small children and for anyone who wants to photograph the Registan covered in snow. The high-speed trains run all year round.

Festivals and dates

  • Nowruz: 21 March in every city.
  • Silk and Spices Festival: late May or early June in Bukhara (dates vary each year).
  • Sharq Taronalari (Melodies of the East): late August in Samarkand, held every two years in odd-numbered years (the next edition after 2025 is 2027).
  • 1 September (Independence Day): celebrations and the closure of some streets in Tashkent.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to visit Samarkand?
April or October: the weather is ideal and the light is excellent for photography at the Registan.

Does it rain in Uzbekistan?
A little, mostly in March, April and November; it rarely spoils a whole day.

Can Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan be combined in one trip?
Yes, and the best time to combine them is May or September. See the 10-day Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan trip.

When is travel cheapest?
From December to February, then July.
