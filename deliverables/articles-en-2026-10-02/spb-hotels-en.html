St Petersburg is a city of palaces and canals, and its hotels are as historic as its sights: nineteenth-century aristocratic palaces turned into luxury hotels on Nevsky Prospekt, modern hotels with pools on the banks of the Neva, and spacious apartments in the quiet districts. For Arab families visiting the city for three or four nights (usually after Moscow), the choice of district and hotel determines how much you walk each day and how quiet the nights are, especially in the White Nights season. This guide recommends the best hotels in each category, the districts, prices in roubles and riyals, and booking tips.

Where to stay in St Petersburg

  • Nevsky Prospekt and the city centre: the Hermitage, Kazan Cathedral, the canals and the restaurants, all on foot. The most expensive and the most in demand.
  • The Admiralteysky district and St Isaac's Cathedral: quieter than Nevsky and 10 minutes from the Hermitage, with luxury and boutique hotels.
  • Vasilievsky Island: river views, quiet, and bridges to the centre in 15 minutes' walk, bearing in mind that the bridges are raised at night in summer.
  • The Petrogradsky Side: near the Peter and Paul Fortress and Krestovsky Island with its parks and amusement park; suitable for families with children.
  • Near Moskovsky railway station: practical for those arriving from Moscow on the Sapsan high-speed train.

5-star hotels in St Petersburg

Four Seasons Lion Palace (now independently run)

A historic palace beside St Isaac's Cathedral, with an indoor pool under a glass roof and family suites. The most luxurious in the city. 45,000–80,000 roubles per night (about 2,000–3,550 SAR, or $530–950).

Grand Hotel Europe (formerly Belmond)

On Nevsky Prospekt since 1875, the most famous hotel in Russia, next to the Russian Museum and Arts Square. 35,000–70,000 roubles (about 1,550–3,100 SAR, or $415–830).

Corinthia St Petersburg

On Nevsky, with spacious rooms and excellent family service, and prices below the two palaces above. 20,000–35,000 roubles (about 900–1,550 SAR, or $235–415).

Lotte St Petersburg

Opposite St Isaac's Cathedral, with a spa, a pool and views; a favourite with Gulf families. 25,000–45,000 roubles (about 1,100–2,000 SAR, or $295–530).

Hotel Astoria and Hotel Angleterre

Two historic hotels on St Isaac's Square with classic decor, well suited to honeymooners. 18,000–40,000 roubles (about 800–1,800 SAR, or $215–475).

4-star hotels with excellent value

Cosmos Selection Nevsky Royal (formerly Radisson Royal) and Sonya Hotel (formerly Radisson Sonya)

Two hotels on and just off Nevsky with modern rooms and a good breakfast. 12,000–22,000 roubles (about 530–980 SAR, or $140–260).

Palace Bridge Hotel, formerly Solo Sokos (Vasilievsky Island)

A large pool, a spa and family rooms, 15 minutes' walk from the Hermitage; the first choice for families with children. 12,000–20,000 roubles (about 530–900 SAR, or $140–235).

Crown Hotel, formerly Crowne Plaza Ligovsky (near Moskovsky station)

Next to Moskovsky railway station, a short walk from Nevsky and the Oceanarium, with large rooms and reasonable prices. 9,000–15,000 roubles (about 400–670 SAR, or $105–180).

Serviced apartments near Nevsky

Restored two-bedroom apartments with a kitchen in historic buildings, a good choice for large families. 8,000–16,000 roubles (about 350–710 SAR, or $95–190).

Booking tips for St Petersburg

  • The White Nights: book three months ahead and ask for blackout curtains, because the sun never really sets. For the seasons themselves, see our guide to the best time for family travel.
  • The bridges: in summer the bridges are raised between 1 and 5 in the morning; anyone staying on Vasilievsky Island or the Petrogradsky Side must get back before then or use the fixed bridges.
  • Payment: Gulf Visa and Mastercard cards do not work; pay in cash (roubles), with a local Mir or UnionPay card, or through Arcadia in riyals. See also our guide to visa-free entry to Russia for Gulf citizens.
  • Breakfast: ask for the halal items to be marked; nearby there are halal Uzbek, Turkish and Caucasian restaurants.
  • The floor: historic buildings sometimes have no lift; check before booking for elderly travellers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in St Petersburg for families?
The city centre around Nevsky and St Isaac's for a short stay, and Vasilievsky Island or the Petrogradsky Side for those who want a pool and quiet.

Are hotels expensive during the White Nights?
Yes, prices roughly double, and early booking is essential.

Are there hotels with pools for children?
Yes: Four Seasons Lion Palace, Lotte and Palace Bridge.

How do I pay the hotel?
In cash in roubles, or in advance through Arcadia in riyals.
