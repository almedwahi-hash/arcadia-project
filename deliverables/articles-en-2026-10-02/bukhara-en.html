Introduction: why Uzbekistan?

Uzbekistan, a country in the heart of Central Asia, is one of the most interesting destinations for lovers of history and culture. It has a unique blend of Islamic heritage and ancient architecture that draws visitors from all over the world. Among the Uzbek cities worth visiting, historic Bukhara stands out as one of the most important, a city every traveller should see. Read why choose Uzbekistan to learn more about the beauty and history of this remarkable country.

Historic Bukhara: the jewel of Uzbekistan

Places you should visit

Bukhara is one of the oldest cities in Uzbekistan, and it has many sights that reflect its long history. Among the places you should visit in Bukhara are the Magoki-Attori Mosque, one of the oldest mosques in the country, and the Ark Fortress of Bukhara, whose origins go back to the fifth century AD. Do not forget to visit the central bazaar as well, where you can buy local products and authentic Uzbek handicrafts.

Cultural experiences and food

Alongside the historic sights, Bukhara offers a cultural experience of its own. You can attend traditional music performances that reflect the rich Uzbek heritage, and taste local dishes such as plov and samsa, known for their delicious flavour. It is worth eating in local restaurants for a real experience of Uzbek cuisine.

A practical step-by-step guide

To reach Bukhara, you can fly to Tashkent International Airport and from there take the high-speed train that links the major cities of Uzbekistan. It is best to book tickets in advance to avoid the crowds in peak season. On arrival, you can use local transport such as buses or taxis to get around the city.

Visiting Bukhara in spring or autumn is recommended, when the weather is mild and suitable for walking and exploring the sights. Make sure you wear comfortable clothes and shoes suitable for walking, as you will need to do a lot of it to explore the city.

Prices and budget in detail

Uzbekistan is a relatively economical destination compared with others. Accommodation in mid-range hotels costs between 30 and 50 dollars a night. Meals in local restaurants cost between 5 and 10 dollars per person. Local transport can come to about 20 dollars a day.

Important tips before you travel

Check the entry requirements for Uzbekistan before you travel: citizens of the Gulf states enter without a visa for 30 days, while other nationalities may need to obtain a visa in advance. It is also advisable to exchange some local currency before you arrive to make everyday transactions easier, and to use credit cards in hotels and the larger restaurants.

Try to learn a few basic phrases in Uzbek or Russian so that you can communicate easily with local people. Do not forget to protect yourself from the sun with sunscreen, and drink plenty of water to avoid dehydration.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the best time to visit Bukhara? Spring and autumn are the best times to visit Bukhara, when the weather is mild.
  • Is English widely spoken? English is not very common, so it is best to learn a few basic phrases in Uzbek or Russian.
  • Can credit cards be used in Bukhara? Yes, they can be used in hotels and the larger restaurants, but it is best to carry some cash for small transactions.

A short practical plan for the Arab traveller

To get more out of this complete travel guide to historic Bukhara, start by deciding the length of the trip, the number of travellers and the right type of accommodation before you book.
