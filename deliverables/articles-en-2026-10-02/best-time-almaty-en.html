The best time to visit Almaty depends on the kind of trip your family wants. Late spring to early autumn is generally right for walking and nature, while winter suits those looking for snow and mountain activities. No single month is ideal for everyone; what matters is matching the weather, the length of the day and the state of the roads to the children's ages.

Quick pick: May and June for families who want greenery and long days, July and August for the mountains and summer excursions, September for milder weather and early autumn colours, and December to February for those focused on snow and Shymbulak.

Almaty season by season

Spring: March to May

The weather changes quickly in early spring, and the mountain areas can stay colder than the city. Late April and May are better for walking and the parks, though you should carry a light layer and an umbrella. Some of the more remote nature routes can be affected by rain or lingering snow, so check the route the day before the trip.

Summer: June to August

Summer is well suited to excursions outside the city, to Kolsai and Charyn, and the long days give you more flexibility. On the other hand, prices can rise on the most sought-after dates and the daytime can be hot in the city. Start out-of-town excursions early, carry water and sunscreen, and leave time to rest.

Autumn: September to November

September is a strong choice for families: milder temperatures, fewer visitors than at the summer peak, and beautiful colours in the parks and mountains. From October the weather turns colder and more changeable, and in November the trip should be treated as a transitional period close to winter.

Winter: December to February

Winter suits those who want a snowy atmosphere and Shymbulak, but it calls for the right clothing and a shorter schedule outdoors. Not every day is suitable for the mountain; wind, visibility and road conditions can change the plan, so keep an indoor activity in reserve.

When to visit Kolsai

The Kolsai lakes are a long way from Almaty and need a long day, so looking at the city's weather is not enough. Check the forecast for the area and the state of the road, and consider how well the children and older family members can cope with a long time in the car. For practical details on the city and its surroundings, see our Almaty travel guide.

When to visit Shymbulak

Shymbulak is not only for winter; it can be visited for the views and the mountain air in other seasons. But temperatures at altitude are lower than in central Almaty, and the wind and the operation of some facilities can change. Take an extra layer even on a day that is warm in the city.

How many days does a family need?

Five days suit Almaty with one or two nearby excursions, and seven days give a better balance if you add Kolsai or other distant destinations. Avoid turning every day into a long trip. See our guide to family trips in Kazakhstan, then compare the cost of tourism in Kazakhstan.

What to pack

  • Layers of clothing rather than one heavy item in spring and autumn.
  • Comfortable non-slip shoes for parks and natural areas.
  • Sunscreen and water in summer; gloves and a suitable hat in winter.
  • Your child's usual medicine, with a prescription or the name of the active ingredient if needed.
  • An indoor back-up plan for days that are not suitable for the mountain.

Examples of seasonal prices

A 4-star hotel in central Almaty: $110–130 a night in July compared with $80–95 in May and September. A car with a driver for the Kolsai day: $150–180 in summer and $120–140 in spring and autumn. Flights from the Gulf: $400–650 per person in the summer holidays compared with $300–450 outside them. A family of four saves $500–800 a week by travelling in May or September while keeping the same program. Activities that change with the season

In summer: horse riding on the Assy Plateau, boats at Kolsai, Charyn Canyon, and the evening fountains in Republic Square. In winter: the children's ski school at Shymbulak, the Medeu ice rink, the New Year markets and the city's museums. In every season: the Kok Tobe cable car, the Green Bazaar, the zoo and the aqua park. All the family activities are in Almaty with kids.

Frequently asked questions

Is summer very hot?
Some hours of the day can be hot in the city, while the mountain areas stay milder. Early excursions and a midday break make the day more comfortable.

Is September suitable for children?
Generally yes, especially in the first half, but the evenings are cooler and you should check the forecast before travelling and before mountain excursions.

Can snow be guaranteed on a particular date?
Natural conditions cannot be guaranteed long before the trip. The forecast and what is actually operating are checked close to the day of the activity.
