Sending the right details upfront gets you a faster and more accurate quote. Most delays in planning a family tour come from missing information: we cannot price a program without knowing the children's ages, and we cannot choose the right hotel without knowing how many rooms you need. Here is exactly what to send Arcadia Tourism (or any tour company) before requesting a program, and why each item matters.

Essential details

  • Travel dates, or at least the month and the number of nights. Prices differ by up to 40% between seasons, and hotel availability changes week to week in summer.
  • Number of travellers: adults and children, with each child's age. Hotels charge children under 6, 6–12 and over 12 differently, and attractions have their own age limits.
  • Destination and cities, if you already have a preference, for example "Almaty and the lakes" or "Moscow and St. Petersburg". If not, say so and we will suggest options; see the best destinations for Arab families.
  • Hotel level: 4-star, 5-star, serviced apartments, or a mix. Mention whether you need connecting rooms, a family suite or two separate rooms.
  • Budget range per family, excluding flights. Even an approximate figure helps us choose between a full private program and a lighter one.
  • Departure city, so we can advise on direct flights and arrival times.

Helpful extras

  • Interests: nature and mountains, shopping, history, theme parks, snow activities, or a relaxed pace. This decides whether your week includes Charyn Canyon or an aqua park.
  • Dietary needs: halal is standard in all our programs; tell us about allergies or vegetarian travellers.
  • Mobility and health: elderly travellers, strollers, or anyone who prefers short walks and fewer stairs.
  • Pace preference: one big activity a day with rest, or full days. Families with children under 6 usually prefer the first.
  • Special occasions: a birthday, honeymoon or Eid; we arrange a cake, a special dinner or a photographer.
  • Visa status: your nationalities, so we can confirm visa-free entry or prepare the documents for an e-visa or Schengen application.

Why it matters

With complete details we send a day-by-day program with a fixed price within 24 hours, and the price does not change later. With partial details we have to make assumptions, the quote comes back with questions, and the back-and-forth can take a week, during which hotel prices and train seats move. A complete request also lets us spot problems early: a school holiday that fills hotels, a festival that closes museums, or a mountain road that is closed in your travel month. A ready-to-copy message

You can simply paste and complete this on WhatsApp:

"Hello Arcadia. We are a family of [2 adults + 2 children aged 5 and 9] from [Riyadh]. We want to visit [Kazakhstan] from [10 to 17 July]. We prefer [4-star hotels with a pool and connecting rooms]. Our budget is around [3,500 USD] excluding flights. We like [nature, light hiking, shopping], and we need [halal food and a child seat]. Please send a program and price."

What you receive back

A written program showing each day's activities, the hotel names, what is included (transport, guide, tickets, breakfast) and what is not, the total price, and the payment and cancellation terms. Nothing is booked and no deposit is requested until you approve it. See how much a family trip costs for typical totals.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a quote take?
Within 24 hours when the essential details above are included; same day in most cases.

Do I have to decide the cities myself?
No. Tell us your dates, group and interests and we propose two or three options.

Is the quote free and non-binding?
Yes. You pay a deposit only after approving the written program.

Can I change the program after receiving it?
Yes, we adjust it as many times as needed before confirmation, and most changes are still possible afterwards subject to availability.
