Knowing your budget before you book makes planning far easier, and it stops the most common disappointment: a quote that is double what you expected. Here is how a family trip cost is built, real 2026 ranges for a family of four in each of our destinations, and where you can save without lowering the quality of the trip.

Main cost factors

  • International flights: 350–700 USD per person from the Gulf to Almaty, Tashkent or Warsaw; 450–900 USD to Moscow or Beijing. Children under 2 fly almost free, under 12 at a reduced fare.
  • Number of nights: the biggest lever. Seven nights cost about 40% more than five, not 100% more, because transfers and flights are fixed.
  • Hotel level: 4-star family rooms 80–150 USD per night; 5-star 150–300 USD; serviced apartments 70–120 USD and often the best value for families of five or more.
  • Private transport and guide: a car with driver 90–160 USD per day; an Arabic-speaking guide 60–120 USD per day. On a full package these are bundled.
  • Attraction tickets: 100–300 USD per family per week, more where theme parks are involved (Universal Beijing, Energylandia, Shanghai Disneyland each 250–350 USD per family).
  • Meals: 40–90 USD per day for a family of four in good halal restaurants; Uzbekistan is the cheapest, Russia and China the most expensive.
  • Season: peak weeks (July–August, Eid, New Year) cost 20–40% more than May, September or October.

How to estimate your own trip

  1. Choose the destination and the number of nights (see the best destinations for Arab families).
  2. Pick a hotel standard and multiply the nightly rate by the nights and the number of rooms.
  3. Add 120–220 USD per day for car, driver and guide (or 90–140 for driver only; see private driver vs full tour package).
  4. Add tickets (150–300 USD) and meals (50–80 USD per day).
  5. Add flights and visa fees, and 10% for shopping and extras.

Save without sacrificing quality

  • Travel in May, September or October: same sights, 20–40% lower hotel and flight prices. See the best time for family travel.
  • Stay in one base city with day trips instead of changing hotels every two nights; you save transfers and a day of packing.
  • Choose a family suite or apartment instead of two hotel rooms for families of five or six.
  • Take the guide on the two or three big days only and keep the driver for the rest.
  • Book 6–8 weeks ahead in peak season; last-minute summer bookings in Almaty and Krakow pay the highest rates.

Frequently asked questions

Do children pay the full price?
Hotels are usually free for children under 6 sharing the parents' room, half price for 6–12; tickets are free under 6 or 7 in most countries.

Are tips expected?
Not obligatory; 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for the driver and guide at the end are appreciated.

What is the deposit?
Typically 30% to confirm, the balance 14 days before departure.

Is travel insurance included?
Available on request for 2–4 USD per person per day; we recommend it for Russia and China.
