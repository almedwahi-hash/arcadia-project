Choosing the right destination is the most important decision for a successful family trip. Here is how Kazakhstan, Russia, Poland, Uzbekistan and China compare for Arab families in 2026: flight time, visas, weather, cost, halal food, and what children actually enjoy in each, based on the families Arcadia has taken to all five.

Kazakhstan: nature and easy travel

The easiest first trip from the Gulf: 4–5 hours by direct flight to Almaty, visa-free for GCC citizens, a Muslim-majority country where halal food is the norm, and a summer of 25–30°C with snow-capped mountains thirty minutes from the city. Children love the Shymbulak cable car, Big Almaty Lake, horse riding on the Assy plateau and the canyon at Charyn; parents like the malls and the prices. Astana adds a futuristic capital. Best for: first-time family travel, summer escapes, nature. A week costs 2,500–3,800 USD for four in summer. See tourism in Kazakhstan.

Russia: culture and grand cities

Palaces, the Hermitage, the Kremlin, the fairy-tale metro and the White Nights. Moscow has the most modern family attractions in the region (Dream Island, Moscow Zoo, the river cruises); St. Petersburg has Peterhof's fountains and canal boats. The e-visa takes about four days for GCC citizens, flights are 5 hours, and halal restaurants are easy to find. The catches: foreign bank cards do not work (we prepay services), and summer is the only comfortable season for small children. Best for: families with children over 7, culture lovers, June White Nights. A week costs 3,000–4,500 USD. See tourism in Russia.

Poland: Europe on a budget

A green European holiday at half the price of Western Europe: Krakow's old town and the dragon, Energylandia theme park, the salt mine, the Zakopane mountains and, in winter, Christmas markets and guaranteed snow. Flights are 6–7 hours, a Schengen visa is needed (apply six to eight weeks ahead), halal food is easy in Warsaw and Krakow. Best for: families who want Europe, mild summers, winter snow. A week costs 2,600–3,900 USD. See tourism in Poland.

Uzbekistan: heritage and short distances

The Silk Road cities of Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva are the most beautiful Islamic architecture a child will ever see, the whole country is halal, GCC citizens enter visa-free, and the high-speed train links the cities in two hours. It is also the cheapest of the five. The limits: fewer child-specific attractions (no theme parks outside Tashkent) and very hot summers, so spring and autumn are best. Best for: heritage, pilgrimage sites (Imam al-Bukhari), spring and autumn holidays, families with older children. A week costs 2,200–3,400 USD. See tourism in Uzbekistan.

China: wonders and theme parks

The Great Wall, the pandas, the Terracotta Army, Shanghai Disneyland and Universal Beijing make China the most exciting choice for children aged 6–14, and traders can combine the trip with Guangzhou or Yiwu. It needs more preparation (visa rules by nationality, Alipay and a VPN set up before arrival, a guide for the language) and ten days rather than seven. Halal food exists in every city. Best for: families wanting a big adventure, trade trips, April–May and September–October. Ten days cost 5,000–7,000 USD. See tourism in China.

Which should you choose?

  • First family trip, summer, small children: Kazakhstan.
  • Europe feel, mild weather, theme park: Poland.
  • Culture, palaces, older children: Russia.
  • Heritage, spring or autumn, lowest cost: Uzbekistan.
  • Big adventure, 10 days, or business plus family: China.
  • Two countries in one trip: Kazakhstan with Uzbekistan (10 days).

Frequently asked questions

Which is the safest?
All five are safe for tourists; Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Poland have very low crime rates.

Where do guides speak Arabic?
In all five destinations through Arcadia's local teams.

Which is best for a grandparents-and-grandchildren trip?
Kazakhstan (short flight, no visa, easy pace) or Poland (flat old towns, good hospitals).

Can we combine two countries?
Yes: Kazakhstan with Uzbekistan is the classic 10-day combination, and Poland pairs well with a short stop in Prague or Vienna.
