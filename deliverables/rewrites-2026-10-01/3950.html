Timing makes a bigger difference to a family trip than almost anything else: the same program in Almaty is a joy in June and a mud-and-snow struggle in March. Here is the best time to visit each of our destinations for families, month by month, with the periods to avoid and the trade-offs between weather, prices and crowds.

Kazakhstan

Best: mid-May to mid-September. Almaty is 25–32°C by day, the mountains 10 degrees cooler, and every lake, canyon and cable car is open. June to August is the Gulf family season (school holidays, escape from the heat); May and September are 20–30% cheaper with the same scenery. Winter (December to February) is excellent for snow: skiing at Shymbulak, skating at Medeu, snow parks for children; keep winter trips in Almaty, since Astana drops below -15°C. Avoid November and March to early April: grey, wet and the mountain roads are closed. Details: tourism in Kazakhstan.

Russia

Best: late May to early September. Long daylight (until 11 pm in St. Petersburg), 20–28°C, fountains and river cruises running, and the White Nights in June. September is quieter and golden. Winter is cold (-5 to -15°C in Moscow) but cheap and magical from mid-December to early January with Christmas markets and ice rinks; good for families who want a real winter. Avoid the first ten days of January and early May, when Russians travel en masse, and November, which is dark and slushy. Details: tourism in Russia.

Poland

Best: June to August for families: 22–26°C, rarely hot, parks and lakes open, days light until 9 pm. May and September are cheaper and pleasant. December brings Christmas markets in Warsaw and Krakow; January and February guarantee snow in Zakopane for skiing and sledging. Avoid November and March (grey and rainy). Note that most shopping malls close on Sundays. Details: tourism in Poland.

Uzbekistan

Best: April to early June and September to early November. 20–30°C, clear skies, and Nowruz on 21 March or the Silk and Spices Festival in late May as bonuses. Summer exceeds 40°C in Bukhara and Khiva; it is still possible with early-morning and evening sightseeing and a hotel pool, and late August is better than July. Winter is cold (0–8°C), cheap and uncrowded. Details: tourism in Uzbekistan.

China

Best: April to May and September to October. Mild weather in Beijing, Shanghai and Xi'an, clear skies for the Great Wall. Avoid Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), the Golden Week of 1–7 October and 1–5 May, when hundreds of millions travel; summer is hot, humid and crowded with the Chinese school holidays. Winter is cold in Beijing but cheap, and mild in Guangzhou. Details: tourism in China.

Plan around the season

If your dates are fixed by school holidays, choose the destination that fits them: Kazakhstan, Russia or Poland in July and August; Uzbekistan or China in April, May, September or October. If the destination is fixed, shift by even two weeks (late August instead of July for Uzbekistan, early June instead of late July for Poland) and both the weather and the prices improve. Hotels in all five countries are 20–40% cheaper outside peak weeks. See how much a family trip costs and the best destinations for Arab families.

Frequently asked questions

Which destination is best for the summer school holiday?
Kazakhstan for nature and cool mountains, Poland for a European city break, Russia for palaces and the long days.

Where is the snow guaranteed?
Almaty (Shymbulak) and Zakopane from mid-December to early March; Moscow has snow but city sightseeing in -15°C is hard with small children.

Which is best for Eid al-Fitr or Eid al-Adha?
Depends on the year: when Eid falls in spring or autumn, Uzbekistan is ideal; in summer, Kazakhstan; book flights early in all cases.

When is travel cheapest?
May, September to October and January to February (outside the New Year week) in all five countries.
