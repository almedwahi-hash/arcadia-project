Poland has become one of the easiest European destinations for Gulf families: cities close to each other, spacious hotels at prices below Western Europe, mountains and snow in Zakopane, and halal restaurants in Warsaw and Krakow. This guide answers the questions every family asks before booking: where to stay, how many days are enough, what it costs, and what suits children in each city.

Why Poland works for families

  • Short distances: Warsaw and Krakow are about two and a half hours apart by fast train, and Zakopane is a two-hour drive from Krakow, so you do not spend the holiday on the road.
  • Reasonable prices: hotels, restaurants and transport cost clearly less than in Germany or Switzerland, so a 4-star hotel fits a 3-star budget.
  • Safe and calm: the old towns are pedestrian zones, streets are clean and public transport is reliable.
  • Halal food is available: Warsaw and Krakow have dozens of Turkish, Arab and Central Asian restaurants, and the larger hotels handle special requests.

The best Polish cities for families

Warsaw: the capital and its hands-on museums

The best fit for families with school-age children: the Copernicus Science Centre (experiments children can touch), the Royal Lazienki Park with its peacocks, the rebuilt Old Town, and indoor play areas in the big malls in winter. Two to three days are enough; see Warsaw in 3 days.

Krakow: the prettiest city to walk

The Main Market Square is one of the largest historic squares in Europe, horse carriages circle it, and Wawel Castle sits on the hill above. The Wieliczka Salt Mine is a half-day trip children love, with underground chambers and lakes. Two to three nights.

Zakopane: mountains, snow and cable cars

A mountain town at the foot of the Tatras: the Gubalowka funicular, beginner-friendly ski slopes in winter, and easy walks and the Morskie Oko lake in summer. Families usually sleep two nights in a wooden lodge or a spa hotel. More in Tourism in Poland.

Gdansk: the sea and the coloured facades

A summer option for families who want the coast: Sopot beach nearby, the old harbour and the Amber Museum. Add it to a 10-day programme rather than a 7-day one.

The best time for families

June to September for mild weather and the parks, and mid-December to early January for the Christmas markets and snow in Zakopane. April and May are cheaper and quieter. Month-by-month weather is in Best time to visit Poland.

Approximate cost for a family of four

One week in 4-star hotels with a private car and an Arabic- or English-speaking guide, excluding flights: roughly USD 2,600 to 3,900 in summer and less in spring and autumn. The figure is approximate and depends on the season, the hotels and the number of days; the full breakdown is in the cost of a Poland trip for families, and the saving tips in how to cut Poland trip costs.

Visa and flights

Poland is in the Schengen area: UAE citizens enter visa-free, and other Gulf citizens need a Schengen visa applied for several weeks before travel. We provide the hotel confirmations and itinerary that support the application; visa issuance is not part of our services. Warsaw has direct flights from Doha and Dubai, and one-stop connections from Riyadh and Jeddah. Details for Saudi travellers are in Poland for Saudi travellers.

Halal food and prayer

Warsaw has an Islamic centre and mosque in the Ochota district, and Krakow has a prayer room at its Islamic centre. Turkish, Lebanese and Uzbek restaurants are common in both city centres; our updated list is in halal restaurants in Krakow and halal food in Warsaw.

Quick tips for families

  • Book family rooms or apartment hotels in the city centre; walking is easier than public transport with children.
  • Gulf bank cards work everywhere and the currency is the zloty; there is no need to carry much cash.
  • In winter temperatures drop below zero: layered clothing and non-slip shoes for the children.
  • A private car with a driver makes the Zakopane leg much easier; compare the options in private driver or full tour package.

Frequently asked questions

Is Poland suitable for small children?

Yes. Pushchairs are welcome in museums, parks and trains, hotels provide cots, and the old towns are car-free.

How many days are enough for Poland?

Seven days cover Warsaw, Krakow and Zakopane without rushing; ten days add Gdansk or more time in the mountains.

Do people speak English?

In hotels, restaurants and tourist sites, yes. Our guide accompanies you on the tours in Arabic or English.

Summer or winter for a family?

Summer for parks, the coast and walking; winter for Christmas markets, snow and beginner skiing in Zakopane. Both work, and prices rise only around New Year.
