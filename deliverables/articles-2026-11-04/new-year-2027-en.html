Moscow: the biggest New Year in the region

Red Square turns into a winter fair with an ice rink in front of the fully illuminated GUM store, and the midnight fireworks are visible from the Moskva embankments. By day: the Kremlin, the winter displays around Victory Park and the city panorama from Ostankino Tower. It is genuinely cold, but museums and malls are warm and a private car takes you door to door. Gulf citizens enter Russia visa-free, and because foreign bank cards do not work in Russia we arrange payments and a local card for you. Warsaw and Krakow: Christmas markets and quiet snow

Krakow's market on the Main Square runs until the first days of January: wooden stalls, pottery, sweets and horse carriages. Warsaw lights the whole of Krakowskie Przedmiescie, and the Old Town becomes a postcard. For guaranteed snow add two nights in Zakopane, two hours from Krakow, for the cable car and beginner skiing. The Schengen visa means booking at least four to six weeks ahead. Almaty: the closest skiing to the Gulf

Four hours from Riyadh or Dubai, visa-free, and only a two-hour time difference, so the children arrive fresh. Shymbulak resort at 2,200 metres has ski schools for beginners and sledging for children, the Medeu Olympic rink offers open-air ice skating, and the Kok Tobe cable car gives a view of the New Year fireworks over the city. Halal food is everywhere, as the country is majority Muslim. Details in winter tourism in Kazakhstan and Almaty in 3 days.

Which one to choose?

  • Children under six or a first winter trip: Almaty, with a short flight, milder cold and shorter distances.
  • Teenagers who want a big-city atmosphere: Moscow, with the largest celebration, museums and malls.
  • A family that loves markets, walking and photos: Krakow and Warsaw, provided the Schengen visa is arranged early.
  • The smallest budget: Almaty, then Poland, then Moscow.

When to book

Central hotels in Moscow and Krakow fill up for 30 December to 1 January at least a month ahead, and flights rise after mid-November. Booking now secures family rooms and the lower price. If you prefer quiet, the week after New Year (3 to 10 January) is clearly cheaper with the same snow.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cold suitable for children?

Yes, with layered clothing, gloves and winter boots, and by alternating indoor and outdoor activities. Almaty is the mildest and Moscow the coldest.

Do these countries really celebrate New Year?

Yes. In Russia and Kazakhstan New Year is the main winter holiday, with lights, markets and fireworks, and in Poland the Christmas markets run until the first days of January.

How many days are enough?

Five to seven days for one destination. Combining Moscow with Saint Petersburg, or Krakow with Zakopane, needs eight days.
